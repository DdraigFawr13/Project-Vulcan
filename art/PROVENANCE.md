- `person.<race>.<facing>.<frame>`, `clothes.<race>.<facing>.<frame>`, `hair.<short|long|braid>.<race>.<facing>` (252 frames; races human, elf, dwarf, orc, giant, halfling, goblin; facings s, n, e, w (w is e mirrored); frames 0 standing, 1 and 2 walk) in `sprites-people`: drawn by `tools/draw-people.mjs` and `tools/draw-people-hair.mjs` from `art/palette.gpl` (skin tones and the grey `tint light/mid/dark` ramp added for P2.1), 2026-10-18; no outside inputs.
