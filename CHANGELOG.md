# Changelog

One dated line per merged milestone.

- 2026-10-17 · P0.1 · Repo setup: Vite, Phaser 4 and strict TypeScript scaffold; `npm run check` with timings, boundaries, size limits, code map and reading-cost checks; CI; docs, toolkit, project agents and skills.
- 2026-10-17 · P0.2 · Content fork: 24 Story Generator content files copied at `7866aea` into `src/content/data/`, with schemas, a validator (shapes, ids, references, tier caps) and `contentHash`; races made generic, eight fantasy creatures added, place names drawn from every culture.
- 2026-10-17 · P0.3 · Engine core: named sfc32 RNG streams, the game clock (one tick a minute, 06:00 start), a scheduler with minute, hour, day and month cadences, `emit` with a template and slot check, a lever queue that stamps late commands on the next tick, replay from the command log, and the calendar's day, month and season lines; ledger templates in the new `events.json`.
- 2026-10-17 · P0.4 · Worker loop, map and art pipeline: the simulation runs in a Web Worker at pause, 1×, 2× and 3× (10, 30, 100 game minutes a second) on a real-time clock with a 250 ms catch-up cap; a flat 150 by 150 grass map owned by the engine's new map system (`terrain.json`) and drawn from 16 by 16 chunks pulled once per frame; drag, wheel, pinch and key camera on fixed zoom steps; HUD with clock, speeds, tile readout and error screen; `art/palette.gpl`, `tools/draw-tiles-base.mjs`, `tools/pack-sheet.mjs` and the `tiles-base` sheet.
- 2026-10-17 · P0.5 · Saves, export and debug inspector: the worker builds and checks saves (format version, content hash, shape) before one replaces the running world; IndexedDB store `ledger` v1 with generated ids, names, thumbnails and a 60 s real-time autosave slot (also on tab hide); NDJSON ledger download; a side panel (bottom sheet on phones) with the tile inspector, the ledger's newest 200 lines and "why?" chains from the engine's new `whyOf`; the map records the line that last changed each tile; New game from a seed in the save panel.
- 2026-10-17 · Phase 0 review · The map draws with Phaser's plain tilemap layer (the GPU layer drew grass as brown stripes on phones); a lowest zoom step fits the whole map, centred; "why?" only on lines that explain more than the world's beginning; the first month and season get their ledger lines; `emit` refuses causes that are not earlier lines; the calendar is validated; a fresh or just-loaded world never replaces the autosave; speed keys wait while a world loads; the purity check catches `**=` and locale methods; continue-after-load is tested against an unbroken run.
- 2026-10-17 · P1.1 · Surface terrain: the valley is generated from the `worldgen` stream: a climate drawn by weight (temperate, pine valley, steppe), a trough-shaped valley in value noise with spurred walls, 5 to 8 mountain ranges on its rims, each of one rock (a second `highlands` layout with mountains inside the valley is kept in content for later) (granite, slate, limestone, sandstone) with snow-capped peaks (a snow layer over the rock), rock share varying by seed, scree and crags, biomes from the forked biome table, fertility, and trees, bushes, herbs and boulders from the new `features.json`; a start tile with 400 buildable tiles, 40 trees and 20 stone within 30, reachable without crossing rock, on every seed (`map.test.ts`); `npm run map:stats` and `map:render`; the inspector shows biome, height, fertility and what grows on a tile; `tiles-world` and `sprites-nature` sheets drawn by `tools/draw-nature.mjs` on a second plain tilemap layer; the repo's Phase 1 spec.
- 2026-10-17 · P1.2 · Water: rivers, streams, lakes and springs carved from the valley's height in a new water step (`src/engine/map/water.ts`, priority flood in `flood.ts`, dials in `terrain.json` `generator.water`): the floor tilts down the valley so one shallow main river runs it and leaves by the low end, streams start at springs below the ranges and wind down by drop-weighted drains, hollows become lakes (deep in the middle, spreading a little past the brim), shores get sand, scree or marsh in patches; a `water` layer and `riverExit` in the map state and saves; the `map.watered` ledger line; water kinds say whether they are deep and drinkable, and a `spring` kind; the inspector's water, drinkable and crossing rows; a shore autotile (47-frame blob) and a deep-water set (16) drawn by `tools/draw-water.mjs` on a third tilemap layer, and a spring marker; drinkable water near the start and no course ending inland on every seed (`map.test.ts`, `map:stats`, which also holds water to 6 to 14% of the map and generation under 300 ms).
- 2026-10-17 · P1.4 · Aura, seasons and weather: a `weather` system runs daily after the calendar on the `weather` stream: spells of the forked `weather.json` kinds drawn by season, wetter on wetter maps, a valley-floor temperature from the climate, the month (`seasons.json`) and the spell, and per-tile temperature by height worked out when asked; still lake water freezes by freezing degree-days (`map.ice`, shallow sooner than deep, springs, streams and rivers never), with `weather.changed`, `weather.iceFormed`, `weather.frozenOver` and `weather.thawed` lines; 2 to 4 surface aura spots of distinct schools (peaks Air, springs Water, old woods Life, deep lakes Mind) over a faint background, and one deep spot on level 4 (cavern Earth, obsidian Fire), with `map.aura` and `map.auraDeep` lines; ice frames and school glows (`tools/draw-ice.mjs`); the clock shows the weather and the inspector temperature, ice and aura; `map:stats` gains a year-of-weather table and `map:render` a winter picture; a year on every seed freezes still water and never a spring (`map.test.ts`).
- 2026-10-18 · P1.5 · Lighter world map and site picker: a region rolled from the seed (`src/engine/region`, dials in the new `region.json`): an 80 by 56 grid of height, warmth and rain with biomes, named ranges and rivers, six to eight neighbouring sites of the content cultures (culture by biome and site preference, name from its syllables, kind by race) and three valleys walled by heights, one per climate where possible, each named and with its nearest neighbours and walking days; a world seed `<seed>/<n>` settles valley `n` with its climate, and `region.named` and `region.settled` lines; a site picker before the world begins (the region map in the game's palette with tap areas of about 40 px, three valley cards with the real map each makes, Settle here, New seed and Roll); a Region button reopens the map with the settled valley; ten seeds give ten distinct neighbourhoods (`region.test.ts`, `map:stats`).
- 2026-10-17 · P1.3 · Underground: four levels below the surface, each made from sub-states of the `veins` stream only when stairs or a shaft first reach it (`MapState.levels`, null until then; a reached level adds about 135 KB to a save, measured in `save.test.ts`); each range's rock runs down beneath it, bedrock bodies of the four stones elsewhere, earth, clay and groundwater on level 1, snaking tunnels and deeper caverns; copper and tin (level 2), iron and sulphur (3), gold, silver, gems and obsidian (4) as `features.json` veins, visible once reached, two copper and two tin veins and one iron vein promised near the start (copper and tin within 30 tiles on every seed, `levels.test.ts`); `cutStairs` and `sinkShaft` test orders until colonists dig, with `map.stairsCut`, `map.shaftSunk`, `map.levelReached` and `map.digRefused` lines; `z` on `setTile`, chunks and the inspector (which shows what lies below); a level control (▲, ▼, `<`, `>`) and a not-reached note; eight new ore items; `map:stats` gains a per-level table and `map:render` a picture per level; new ground and vein art.
- 2026-10-18 · Game audit · Morgan's answers recorded as V-022 to V-035 (people slice and food in the first playable, predators in Phase 5, ambitions, skip-ahead, 30-person target, newcomers, phone first, underground fog, the Story Generator world in P1.5, save files, sound, AI-made final art, no mod loader); VR-55 revised, VR-56 taste board, VR-57 phone first.
