# Art sheet

1. Take the sheet's row from the art plan and list every frame name it needs using the naming pattern `<category>.<name>[.<variant>][.<facing>][.<frame>]`.
2. Brief `art-maker` with the frame list, sizes and palette. Scripted art first; a paid generator (OpenArt) only with Morgan's approval and a credit estimate (SAFE-04).
3. Review the contact sheet at 1x and 3x: readable at 32 px, palette only, consistent light direction, names exact.
4. Publish the contact sheet as an artifact, keep the PNG under `vulcan/<milestone>/`, and land the sheet in the milestone PR with its provenance lines.
