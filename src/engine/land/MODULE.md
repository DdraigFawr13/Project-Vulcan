# land

Work on the land itself (P2.4, P2.9, P3.1, P4.1, P4.7): felling and quarrying, foraging, mining and the cellar, and farming. Each takes something from the ground for haulers to carry. Uses `band` and `crafts` (a shaft's ladder and a field are build plans; clay is for the potter).

- **Public API:** `index.ts`: `FORAGE_TABLES`, `farming`, `foraging`, `gatherMinute`, `mining`, `takeUpFarming`, `takeUpForaging`, `takeUpGathering`, `takeUpMarked`, `takeUpMining`, `regrowWoods`.
- **Felling and quarrying (P2.4, P3.1):** `gather.ts`: someone able to fell (the `jobs.json` job `work.fell.job`: strength 8) takes it up while wood (`haveOf`, plus trees being felled) is under its level (`wantOf`): a fallen log, else the tree nearest the store within `fell.reach` (not within `fell.camp` of the start), for `minutes × 2 × skilled ÷ (skilled + woodcutting level)` minutes; its logs lie around its tile. Quarrying is its twin for stone (`work.quarry.good`): the boulder nearest the store within `quarry.reach`, `quarry.minutes`.
- **Regrowth (V-202):** `regrow.ts`: a tree felled for wood is noted in `map.regrow` (`noteFelled`); at dawn `regrowWoods` (run by people) puts each due one back after `regrow.years` if a living tree stands within `regrow.near` tiles and nothing is on or planned for the tile, one `land.regrown` line citing the fellings; else the clearing stays clear.
- **Foraging (P2.9, V-015):** `forage.ts`: the same shape as felling, for food. While food is under its stock level, a free person takes up foraging: the nearest ripe bush within `forage.reach` of the store that they can get beside, worked for `minutes × 2 × skilled ÷ (skilled + foraging level)` minutes; the bush stays on the map, its `amount` of berries lie on the open tiles around it for haulers; a `bush` row records the picking. A bush is ripe when the month is in `forage.months` and it was never picked or not for `regrowDays` days; out of season none is. The tally is `band` `tally.ts`.
- **Mining (P4.1, V-086):** `mine.ts` (+ `shaft.ts`, `clay.ts`, `minetask.ts`), the Mine row after Quarry (`work.mine`). While `oreShort` > 0 a free able person takes up the nearest vein tile they can get beside (`vein` minutes), else the metal's shaft: sinking a level (`shaft` minutes; the first takes the `ladder` logs, fetched until held) or, once done, tunnelling (`nextDig`, `dig` minutes a tile); one of each a shaft. Else the cellar (`cellar.ts`), else clay for a wall plan (`mine.clayDug`). Ore and rubble are hauled up (`haul.kinds`). More in mine `MODULE.md`.
- **Farming (P4.7, V-015, V-089):** `farm.ts`, a Work sheet kind after Forage (`farm`, jobs.json, the farming skill; content `work.farm` with minutes a tile to `sow` and `reap` at skill `skilled`). A free person takes up reaping a ripe field, else sowing a field waiting in a month its crop is sown in when seed can be reached (`work.tookUpReaping` citing the field's `field.ripe`; `work.tookUpSowing` citing its last line). A sower fetches seed from the store, then works tile by tile from beside each, one seed a tile, shorter with farming skill (practising it); the `plan` person field holds the field's id. Each reaped tile lays its yield (by soil) for haulers: seed first, until the Seed plate's want is met, then the crop's item. The last tile sown or reaped ends in build `finishSowing` / `finishReaping`. Idle farmers carry muck (`muck.ts`). Seed grain is life.json `lastResort`: `mind.ts` eats it only at Food `life.seedAt` (0) with no other food to reach, one `goods.ateSeed` line a person a day (person field `seeded`). Dry spells: `water-fields.ts` (water: `isWaterSpot`, where wells join).
- **Test marks (`?debug`):** before choosing a tree, a free person able to fell takes the nearest marked tree they can get beside (`goods` `markedTree`), with a `work.tookUpMarked` line citing the mark's order.
- **Events:** `work.tookUp`, `work.felled`, `land.regrown`, `work.tookUpMarked`, `work.tookUpClearing`, `work.tookUpReaping`, `work.tookUpSowing`, `mine.sank`, `mine.dug`, `mine.veinMined`, `mine.clayDug`, `mine.noLadder`, `farm.harvestHome`, `goods.ateSeed`.
- **State owned:** table `bush` (x, y, picked, line; P2.9, `FORAGE_TABLES`); person fields `task` (`sink`, `tunnel`, `mine`, `clay`) and `what`.
- **Tests:** `forage.test.ts` (foraging, the tally); `mining.test.ts`; `regrow.test.ts`.
