# colony

The colony as a whole (P6.6, V-146, V-158, V-159): its standing, the elder, factions and their petitions, and the mood in one word.

- **Public API:** `index.ts`: `colonySystem`, `COLONY_TABLES`, `colonyRow`, `standingOf(world, content)`, `standingWord(content, n)`, `causesOf(world)` (biggest first), `elderOf(world)` (0 for none), `trustOf(world, content, p)`, `talkersOf(world, content, p)` (the living they told or heard a tale from in `friendDays`: callings' friends), `factionsOf(world)`, `factionOf(world, person, law)`, `hardTimes(world, content)`, `addCause(ctx, kind, cause, text, causes)` (engine defy's leaving), `colonyMood(world, content)` (word, rank, the biggest causes).
- **Content:** `colony.json` (`standing` start, `per`, words and causes; `elder`; `faction`; `petition.days`; `mood.causes`); laws.json settings' `harsh` and `faction` names; tales.json tale `store-out`.
- **Standing (`standing.ts`):** start + the causes still counting, held 0 to 100. Each cause is a `colony.standing` line and a `cause` row for its `days`: a fed winter (at most `most` hungry days a person; tallied in system state), the laws kept (every `every` days, `share` per cent of answers obey), a harsh setting in hard times (winter or a store short of the harvest) by its `harsh`, the store out (`storeOut`: every `every` days that the store holds `food` days or less while someone holds the `hungry` memory, at any law; the line's `cause` is `storeOut` and the tale `store-out` spreads it), a petition heard, let stand or not answered, the elder's death.
- **Elder (`elder.ts`, `vote.ts`):** every `elder.every` days each villager votes for whom they trust most: trust (founding band, traits, roles, listeners in `friendDays`, laws kept; `least` to stand) plus, through the voter's eyes, `vote` friend, shared trait and plight points; the sitting elder's `incumbent` boost is lost once standing falls `drop`. Most votes, `vote.least` and nobody level. `colony.elder` names them and why. Friends: those they told or heard a tale from in `friendDays`, and housemates. Dead, standing falls by `elderDied` for its days.
- **Factions (`faction.ts`):** daily, per law away from its custom (not `split`), each person's grievance is how far short of obeying they weigh it alone (law `weigh` with `alone`); `least` people and `share` per cent at `strong` or more form one (`colony.faction`, a name from the setting's `faction` list) and bring a petition for the setting one step back towards the custom (`colony.petition`). Members stay while short of obeying alone; newcomers at `strong` join (`colony.joined`); under `least`, it breaks up (`colony.disbanded`), but not in its first `hold` days, when it keeps every living member. One formed against the same setting within `keepName` days of breaking up takes its old name (system state `faction:<law>.<setting>`). The law moving away from the setting ends it: heard if it moved as far back as asked, else not answered. The `letStand {faction}` lever turns the petition down; left `petition.days`, it is not answered.
- **Leans:** law `leans.ts` reads these rows (this module imports law): standing leans every answer `(standing − start) ÷ per`; the elder's friends lean `sway` with the elder's answer; members lean `lean` against.
- **Mood (`mood.ts`):** the mean of the living's moods, worded by life.json `mood`; causes are needs under their line (how far under × weight ÷ total weight) and memories with mood, summed and averaged over the living, the `mood.causes` biggest.
- **State owned:** tables `standing` (one row: standing, elder, line, friends), `cause`, `faction`; state `winter`, `hungry`, `people`, and per setting the last faction name and when it broke up.
- **Invariants:** no RNG; reads people, traits, roles, tales, answers, laws, needs, memories and the store; writes only its own tables. Must not import `people`.
- **Tests:** `colony.test.ts` (a faction forms from the grievance table; a petition heard when the law steps back; let stand; the elder's death; low standing leans a law; a faction holds `hold` days and keeps its name).
