# family

Families (P6.1, V-145, V-161): unwed adults drawn to each other grow close at leisure, court, wed with a feast on the green and set up one household; a wed woman and man have children, who grow up and want homes; orphans and children strangers leave are taken in; the old die of old age. Nobody is told whom to marry: there is no lever (V-041).

- **Public API:** `index.ts`: `familySystem` (in `SYSTEMS` after tales), `FAMILY_TABLES`, `bondOf(world, p, q)`, `bondsOf`, `otherOf`, `courtingOf(world, id)` (0 for no one), `ageOf(world, content, p)` (years from `born`), `grown` (at their race's `maturityYears`), `unwed` (alive, grown, no living partner), `mayCourt` (both unwed, each drawn to the other: `drawn.ts`, which draws each person's `orientation` from stream `kin`), `likeOf` (an hour together's worth).
- **Content:** `family.json` (schema `schemas-family.ts`): `hour`, `leisure` (`doing` values), `near`, `like` (`base`, `shared`, `clash`), `told`, `fade`, `court` (`from`, `days`, `part`), `orientations` (`drawn`: other, same, both, none; `weight`), `feastDays`, `words` (the lines' phrases), `memories` (`wed`, `feast`); events `family.*`; memories `married`, `wedding-feast`.
- **Closeness (`close.ts`):** each hour every pair who may court and are both at `leisure` within `near` tiles gains `likeOf`: `base`, + `shared` per trait both hold, − `clash` per trait of one the other's traits.json `excludes`, never under 0. Daily it fades `fade` per cent (rounded up) and gains `told` for each tale one told the other the day before (tales `tale` rows, `heard`); a friendship at 0 is dropped.
- **Courtship (`court.ts`, daily at `hour`):** a bond at `court.from` or more whose two may court and court no one begins courting, closest first (`family.courting`, citing their newest telling, else their arrival lines); one that fades to `court.part` ends (`family.parted`); death or a partner ends it with no line. After `court.days` they wed (`family.wed`, citing the courtship): partners both ways (build `householdOf`), `married` about each other, and the home: a built one before a planned one, the older of two alike (the other's `owner` set to 0, free for anyone; people `home.ts` carries the other's bedroll over), else `wantsFrom` now and `wants` the wedding line, so build's house wants plan them one (V-053). A wedding waits a day if a supper is already called; a wed bond goes when either dies.
- **The feast:** while the store holds `feastDays` days of food for the living (goods `foodDays`), build `callWedding` calls a supper with the `couple` for that evening (a supper keeps to its day, `on`, and a harvest home called the same day moves to the next); people `feast.ts` holds it round the fire on the green, never the hall (`family.feast`, the `wedding-feast` memory about the first of the two). Else `family.noFeast`.
- **Children (`birth.ts`, daily):** a wed woman and man (`birth`: `perMille` a day times race `fertility`, from coming of age to `until`% of lifespan, `restDays` between, none under `leanDays` of food or while she holds `hungry`) is found with child (`family.expecting`, citing the wedding); after `carryDays` band `drawChild` makes the child (one parent's race, her culture, `inherit` gifts and weaknesses), born to her `household` (`family.born`, the `child-born` memory). `kin.ts`: grown, they leave it (`family.cameOfAge`, `wantsFrom`/`wants`); a child whose household head died passes to the other parent, else is taken in (`family.takenIn`, citing the deaths or the line they were left on) by a wed couple with no child at home, those who cannot have their own first. Band `childJobs`: a child helps only at `child.jobs`, from `helpFrom`% of maturity.
- **Left (`left.ts`):** a stranger waiting at the green who came for one of `left.kinds` is weighed once (`weighed`) and leaves a child aged within `ages`, `percent`% of the time (`family.left`, citing their coming). **Old age (`age.ts`):** from `old.from`% of lifespan, `old.per` in 100,000 a day for each year past it, health `die` citing their birth or coming. Health's grief adds `lost-spouse`, `lost-child`, `lost-parent`; one mourning a spouse does not court. **Orientation (`drawn.ts`):** each person draws one of `orientations` by `weight`; the founders always hold a woman and a man drawn to each other.
- **State owned:** table `bond` (a, b, close, state, since, line, due, expect, last); the `person` fields it writes (`partner`, `orientation`, `household`, `fostered`, `weighed`, `wantsFrom`, `wants`) are band's.
- **Invariants:** stream `kin` only; whole numbers; each person courts or is wed to at most one living person; a bond's `a` is the lower id.
- **Tests:** `family.test.ts` (an hour side by side adds `likeOf`, one tile too far adds nothing; a close pair courts, weds citing the courtship with partners and memories set, and feasts on the green citing the wedding; orientation and the founders' pair); `kin.test.ts` (a birth to the mother's home citing her line; old-age deaths and the orphan taken in citing them; a stranger's child left and taken in).
