# asks

What the colony is asked for, and its answer (6C Slice 3, V-225).

- **Public API:** `index.ts`: `asksSystem` (every minute, after `lordSystem`; commands `takeAsk`, `passAsk`; table `regard`), `addAsk(ctx, NewAsk)` (an asker side adds an open ask citing its line), `wantsOf(ctx, def)` (a content ask's tool or goods and count now, a `share` of the food in store), `settleAsk(ctx, ask, causes, data)` (on its day: `done`, `late` or `passed`, a line; the cost is the asker side's).
- **Content:** `asks.json` (schema `schemas-asks.ts`, checks `validate-asks.ts`): `from` (the lord's first asking visit), `midwinter`, `grumble`, `lord.double` (tempers doubling a cost), `lord.asks` (one a temper: words, a `tool` kind or a `good`, `count` or `share` of the store, `quality`, `due`). `tools.json` kind `blade` (2 ingots, only for asks). Also `regard` (start, done/passed/late moves, `days` due, `comes`, `words`), `village` (an ask by stranger trouble), `gifts` (by village kind, `else`), `shrine` (`done`/`passed` memories, `asks` by sacred tag). A `good` may join several with `|`.
- **Levers (V-041):** `takeAsk {ask, level}` takes an open (or let-pass) ask up at priority 1 to 4, or moves a taken one (`ask.taken`); `passAsk {ask}` lets it pass (`ask.letPass`). Neither names a person; both work while paused. An ask nobody answers passes on its day (`ask.unanswered`).
- **The table:** goods `asks.ts` owns `ask` (source, asker, what, tool or good, quality, count, got, `held` kinds set apart, cite, line, due, state, level, answer, going, settled), `fitsAsk`, `takenAsks` (priority then age), `setApart`, `apartOf`; goods `barnOf` and `fromBarn` count what asks hold, so the hungry or angry take from it as from the tithe (defy, V-222) and the ask is short.
- **The work:** a taken ask is the job table's `ask` row (people `jobtable.ts`), put in each grown-up's order at the ask's level, before their own kinds at that level (people `jobs.ts`). Crafts `askwork.ts`: for each taken ask by priority, someone who will work it (`askmade.ts` `willWork`) carries what fits to the tithe barn (band `askhaul.ts`: a load marked `ask`, set apart on delivery, `ask.ready` once all is in); a tool nobody has made yet is made at the forge by whoever free can smith, the most skilled first (`ask.tookUp`, then crafts `smith.ts` with field `ask`; `ask.made` citing the ask, saying if it falls short of the quality). The lord's ask: one who would refuse the Lord law (Loyal will under `grumble`) never works it; a grumbler (under `obey`) takes `grumble` minutes in 100.
- **The lord's asks (engine lord `asks.ts`):** from his `from`th visit, after the tithe, `lord.asked` (citing `lord.tithed`) brings his temper's ask, due at his next visit or by midwinter; each visit first settles those due (`ask.done` / `ask.late` / `ask.passed` / `ask.unanswered`) and adds the favour cause `askDone` +6, `askLate` −6 or `askPassed` −4 (a cruel lord doubling the costs), each three years; a midwinter ask is settled hourly once its day comes.
- **Neighbours (`villages.ts`):** each minute a stranger newly come whose trouble has an `asks.village` ask asks for their village (`neighbour.asked`, citing the coming), unless it asked within the year; due in `regard.days`. Hourly, on the day, someone from the village fetches it (`settleAsk`), and its `regard` row (from `start`, 0 to 100) moves with `ask.regard` citing the settling; done, the village's next stranger brings its `gifts` kind to the barn (`ask.gift`). Its own people work it first (crafts `askwork.ts`).
- **The shrine (`shrine.ts`):** at each harvest-home supper, the shrine asks due are settled (the faithful gain `shrine.done` or `shrine.passed`), then each creed with a shrine and a living priest asks for its faith's first sacred tag's ask (`faith.asked`), due the next harvest home. Only its own faithful work it (crafts `askmade.ts` `willWork`).
- **Guests (`guests.ts`):** each minute a stranger just turned away (with a partner) stops (`waits` −2, band `strangers.ts` waits) and asks to stay a season (`guest.asked`, citing the turning away), one a season, open `guests.answer` days; person field `guest`. Taken up: band `admit`, `guest.stays`, `guests.against` for all who did not speak for them; at the season's end `guest.tale` (methods `seen.also`) and the band's word again. Let pass or unanswered: band `sendBack`. A feud's is a `neighbour` ask moving its regard.
- **Masters (`masters.ts`, V-236):** a master comes (band `walkIn`), asks at the green (source `wanderer`, good `guest`) and is settled as a guest; see `docs/LORD_AND_ASKS.md` "Slice 7 part 1 build".
- **Dreams (`dreams.ts`, hourly):** an ambition `dreams.days` unfulfilled asks once (`dream.asked`, citing it): `dreams.house` or `dreams.skills[skill]`, open `answer` days; worked by the dreamer and that skill's people (`willWork`). All set apart: done, `backed`, goods laid out again; passed: `unbacked`.
- **Events:** `ask.taken`, `ask.letPass`, `neighbour.asked`, `ask.regard`, `ask.gift`, `faith.asked`, `guest.asked`, `guest.stays`, `guest.tale`, `dream.asked`, `master.*` (here); `ask.tookUp`, `ask.made`, `ask.ready` (people); `lord.asked`, `ask.done`, `ask.late`, `ask.passed`, `ask.unanswered` (lord).
- **State owned:** table `regard` (place, regard, gift); state `seen` (the newest stranger's coming heard), `home` (the last harvest supper asked at), `guest` (the last guest ask's season), `master` (year); stream `asks`. The `ask` table is goods', the person field `ask` people `jobfields.ts`. Hourly it lets go of a carrier who gave up the way.
- **Invariants:** an ask is answered only by a lever; nobody is picked to work it; every ask cites the line it came from.
- **Tests:** `asks.test.ts` (the lord's ask cites his visit and passing costs favour; a fine blade by the best smith unordered; a refuser never works it; never settled twice); `askers.test.ts` (a village's ask and regard and gift; the shrine's, its faithful only; a guest stays and is talked over again; a dream, by its trade); `masters.test.ts` (a master asks, stays, leaves a hint; let stay keeps their way).
