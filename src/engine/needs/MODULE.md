# needs

Needs and mood (P2.2, P3.5, P5.2): food, rest, warmth, recreation, beauty, privacy and water, each 0 to life.json `scale`, and the mood they make.

- **Public API:** `index.ts`: `needsSystem`, `sees(world, content, q, p)` and `seenBy(world, content, p, but)` (the one who-saw-it rule: awake, same level within life.json `sight`, or the same house), `NEEDS_TABLES`, `seedPerson(world, content, p)` (a newcomer's needs and mood, P5.9), `feltAt(world, content, i)`, `needOf(world, person, need)`, `moodOf(world, person)`, `changeNeed(content, row, by)` (held between 0 and the scale), `crossed(ctx, person, row, def)` (writes the line when a need crosses its warning line; the mind calls it after a meal), `addMemory(ctx, person, id, cause, about?)`, `addMemories(ctx, people, id, cause)`, `memoriesOf(world, person)` (memories, below) and `warmLineOf(world, content, person)`: the warmth warning line moved by traits and held memories (life.json `warmthLine`, V-046), read by people `warm.ts`.
- **Hourly:** each need falls its `fall`, except: rest rises `sleep.bed` (in their own bed, P3.4), `sleep.bedroll` (on their own laid-out bedroll) or `sleep.ground` while asleep (`doing: sleep` and not walking); warmth rises `warmth.rise` while the felt temperature is at least `warmth.comfort`, else falls `perDegree` for each degree short (felt, `feltAt`: the tile's temperature (weather `tileTemperature`) plus the heat of lit fires (fire `heatAt`, P3.6) on the surface, `warmth.below` underground, `warmth.bedroll` warmer asleep in one); recreation rises `relax.gain` while relaxing or praying (6B), `relax.company` more with someone else relaxing within `relax.reach` tiles. `comfort.ts` (P3.5, V-067): beauty, awake indoors, moves up to `beauty.rate` towards `beauty.neutral` + `perPoint` × the room's beauty (build `roomAt`, worked out once an hour and only inside a built house), outdoors `beauty.outdoors` towards neutral; privacy, asleep in a room, falls `privacy.perOther` for each other sleeper there, else rises `privacy.gain` when every bed in the room is theirs alone and they are its only sleeper, else holds. The bed's warmth bonus (`warmth.bedroll`) applies in a bed too. Eating happens in the people's mind (people/mind.ts), on the minute.
- **Mood:** the needs' mean weighted by `weight`, plus the live memories' points times `life.memory.weight`, held between 0 and the scale, worded by `mood` (first whose `at` it reaches). A better word must be earned by `again` over its threshold, so it does not flicker.
- **Memories (V-091, V-154):** `memory.ts`, table `memory` (person, memory (a `memories.json` id), until (fresh till), line (the cause), about, weight, since, first, last, days, mask (the days it came in the last `windowDays`), fresh (first time)) and `memorySeen`. Weight = content `weight` (1) + 1 a first time + for a memory with a `heavy` name 1 per `spellDays` it came in the window, never falling. `bandOf`: light under `life.memory.heavy`, lasting from `lasting`. Light ones go when fresh ends; heavy ones stay, with no mood, until `lingerDays` after `last` (`goesAt`); lasting ones stay for life. Only fresh ones (`memoriesOf`) move mood, the warmth line and law answers; `heldMemoriesOf` is every row. `addMemory` refreshes a held non-stacking memory and marks today on its run; a stacking one (content `stacks`) adds rows up to that many. A need with a `memory` (food `went-hungry`, warmth `cold-night`) refreshes it each hour under its line, citing the low line. `diet.ts` (`taste` rows, `tasted` from people `eat.ts`): `sameFood.kinds` or fewer kinds in `sameFood.days` gives `same-food`. Faded ones drop hourly; no lines.
- **Thought (V-154):** `thought.ts` `thoughtOf`: the lowest need under its line (its `thoughts`, why = its low line), else a memory got in `recentHours`, else the strongest (`strongestFirst`: weight, fresh, mood, newest); a memory's `thoughts` (`heavyThoughts` once heavy), else `life.memory.thoughts.good|bad`, else `none`; a hash of person and key picks the line. `memoryName`: the heavy name ("The hungry {season}") once heavy.
- **Cloaks (V-092):** `cloak.ts`: on the surface and outside a built house, the worn cloak (goods item `state: 'worn'`) adds `life.cloak.warmth` to the felt temperature; each such hour under `warmth.comfort` (without it) it wears `life.cloak.wear` from the item's `wear`; at 0 it goes with `goods.cloakWornOut`, citing the unloading.
- **Quiet winter (V-092):** a need with `quiet: true` (warmth) writes `need.low` with `quiet: 'no'` the first time a game day per person (`noted` on its row), else `'yes'`; events.json makes `need.low` notable only for `quiet: no`. Text and lines unchanged.
- **Events:** emits `goods.cloakWornOut`, `need.low` when a need goes under `low` (citing the weather spell for warmth, the house or room built for beauty and privacy, else the need's last line, at first the arrival; the wording is the one template with the need's `lowWord`, so privacy reads "Gwallog is crowded."), `need.met` when it is back to `low + again` (citing the low line), and `mood.changed` (way, word) citing the lowest need's last line. Each has the person as actor.
- **State owned:** tables `need` (person, need, value, low (1 while under the line), line, noted), `memory` and `mood` (person, value, word, line), laid out in `init` after the people. No system state.
- **Invariants:** whole numbers only; reads the people's `doing` and `path` and the weather's `temp` and `spell`, writes no other system's rows.
- **Tests:** `memory.test.ts` (weights, bands, linger, same food, thought order); `needs.test.ts` (a week on three seeds keeps every need but privacy above its line, the only lines being the barracks' privacy and the mood it drags down, rations go down, the band sleeps on its bedrolls; hunger and cold write lines citing the arrival and the spell, and the mood falls).
