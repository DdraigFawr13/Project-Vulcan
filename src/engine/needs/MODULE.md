# needs

Needs and mood (P2.2, P3.5): food, rest, warmth, recreation, beauty and privacy, each 0 to life.json `scale`, and the mood they make.

- **Public API:** `index.ts`: `needsSystem`, `NEEDS_TABLES`, `needOf(world, person, need)`, `moodOf(world, person)`, `changeNeed(content, row, by)` (held between 0 and the scale), `crossed(ctx, person, row, def)` (writes the line when a need crosses its warning line; the mind calls it after a meal).
- **Hourly:** each need falls its `fall`, except: rest rises `sleep.bed` (in their own bed, P3.4), `sleep.bedroll` (on their own laid-out bedroll) or `sleep.ground` while asleep (`doing: sleep` and not walking); warmth rises `warmth.rise` while the felt temperature is at least `warmth.comfort`, else falls `perDegree` for each degree short (felt: the tile's temperature (weather `tileTemperature`) on the surface, `warmth.below` underground, `warmth.bedroll` warmer asleep in one); recreation rises `relax.gain` while relaxing, `relax.company` more with someone else relaxing within `relax.reach` tiles. `comfort.ts` (P3.5, V-067): beauty, awake indoors, moves up to `beauty.rate` towards `beauty.neutral` + `perPoint` × the room's beauty (build `roomAt`, worked out once an hour and only inside a built house), outdoors `beauty.outdoors` towards neutral; privacy, asleep in a room, falls `privacy.perOther` for each other sleeper there, else rises `privacy.gain` when every bed in the room is theirs alone and they are its only sleeper, else holds. The bed's warmth bonus (`warmth.bedroll`) applies in a bed too. Eating happens in the people's mind (people/mind.ts), on the minute.
- **Mood:** the needs' mean weighted by `weight`, worded by `mood` (first whose `at` it reaches). A better word must be earned by `again` over its threshold, so it does not flicker.
- **Events:** emits `need.low` when a need goes under `low` (citing the weather spell for warmth, the house or room built for beauty and privacy, else the need's last line, at first the arrival; the wording is the one template with the need's `lowWord`, so privacy reads "Gwallog is crowded."), `need.met` when it is back to `low + again` (citing the low line), and `mood.changed` (way, word) citing the lowest need's last line. Each has the person as actor.
- **State owned:** tables `need` (person, need, value, low (1 while under the line), line) and `mood` (person, value, word, line), laid out in `init` after the people. No system state.
- **Invariants:** whole numbers only; reads the people's `doing` and `path` and the weather's `temp` and `spell`, writes no other system's rows.
- **Tests:** `needs.test.ts` (a week on three seeds keeps every need but privacy above its line, the only lines being the barracks' privacy and the mood it drags down, rations go down, the band sleeps on its bedrolls; hunger and cold write lines citing the arrival and the spell, and the mood falls).
