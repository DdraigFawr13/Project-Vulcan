# weather

Seasons and daily weather over the valley, the temperature of every surface tile, and ice on still water.

- **Public API:** `index.ts`: `weatherSystem` (run daily at midnight, after the calendar), `tileTemperature(content, map, floorTemp, i)` (whole degrees C at a surface tile: the valley floor's temperature less the terrain's `lapse` per 1,000 m above `map.floor`; worked out when asked, never stored), `weatherWords(content, kind, temp)` (the spell's words at that temperature), `isFrozen(map, i)`.
- **Daily:** with chance `seasons.change` (always on the first day) a new spell's kind is drawn from the forked `weather.json` season weights, wet weighted up and dry down by `map.rainfall / seasons.rainfall`; then the day's valley-floor temperature (`map.temperature` + the month's `seasons.months` entry + the kind's `shift`, rounded, + a whole-degree wobble within `jitter`) and whether it is wet (the kind's `wet` chance). All draws come from the `weather` stream, in that order.
- **Ice:** each lake tile (`map.water` lake; never springs, streams or rivers) keeps freezing degree-days in `map.ice`'s low 7 bits: a day below 0 °C at the tile adds its cold, a day above takes off `seasons.ice.thaw` times its warmth, held between 0 and `cap`. The tile is frozen (top bit) at `shallow` or `deep` by its kind. A change of frozen raises its chunk's version (`raiseChunk`), so the worker resends it. The tile's kind never changes: ice is a state of the water.
- **Events:** emits `weather.changed` (only when the spell's kind changes, and on the world's first day; cites that day's calendar line), `weather.iceFormed` (no lake ice yesterday, some today), `weather.frozenOver` (at least `ice.over` of lake water frozen, once until it drops below) `weather.thawed` (the last ice gone), and on any other day the frozen count changes `weather.iceSpread` or `weather.iceShrank`, each citing the spell in force. Every lake tile that froze or thawed that day has its `map.tileCauses` set to the day's ice line, so the inspector shows since when. Reads none.
- **State owned:** `world.systems.weather`: `kind` (index into `content.weather`), `temp` (today's valley-floor temperature), `wet` (0 or 1), `spell` (the current spell's line), `frozen` (lake tiles frozen), `over` (1 while frozen over); `world.map.ice`.
- **Invariants:** whole degrees only, no transcendental math (V-006); `seasons.json` has one entry per forked weather kind and one month offset per calendar month, and `ice.cap` is under 128 (content checks).
- **By design:** every lake tile sits at one height, so all share one day's cold and differ only by depth: shallow water freezes in a day or two, then the lake holds until the thaw takes it in a day or two.
- **Tests:** `src/engine/map/map.test.ts` (a year on every seed in `seeds.ts` freezes still water and never a spring, and each frozen tile cites its ice line); the engine determinism test covers the rest.
