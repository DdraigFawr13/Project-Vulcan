# band

The band as bodies and hands, below every kind of work: who they are and newcomers (P2.1, P5.9), where they stand and walk, what they are able for and put first (P2.5), learn, carry, eat and hold, and the day's tally. The work systems and `people` build on it; it imports none of them.

- **Public API:** `index.ts`: `ATTRIBUTES`, `Day`, `Home`, `KNAP_TABLES`, `LOOK_AGAIN`, `LineHook`, `LineNeed`, `NEWCOMER_FIELDS`, `PRIORITY_TABLES`, `Person`, `SIDES`, `TALLY_START`, `TOOL_TABLES`, `Tally`, `WALK_FIELDS`, `able`, `addPerson`, `besideOnSurface`, `besideReachable`, `bestHeld`, `busy`, `canStandBeside`, `capOf`, `cooked`, `couldHave`, `dayOf`, `declineIfUnable`, `declinedLine`, `deliver`, `drawBand`, `drawSmithing`, `eat`, `edgeSpot`, `equip`, `fetchKind`, `gain`, `giveTool`, `goBeside`, `haulMinute`, `heldMaterial`, `heldSpeed`, `jobsInOrder`, `knapping`, `layAll`, `layAround`, `levelOf`, `lineOf`, `masterOf`, `materialSpeed`, `nameFrom`, `mostUsed`, `neededKind`, `needOf`, `outranked`, `people`, `practise`, `priorityLine`, `progressOf`, `reachableOf`, `readyFor`, `rollTally`, `same`, `scaled`, `setLineHook`, `setPriority`, `skillOf`, `skillRow`, `spotOf`, `stepAlong`, `strangersSystem`, `takeUpHauling`, `takeUpKnapping`, `tallyAdd`, `Teacher`, `teacherOf`, `toGreen`, `todayLine`, `toolFactor`, `toolPercent`, `toolWaits`, `toolsDaily`, `toolsOfKind`, `tradeOf`, `traitPace`, `useHeld`, `walkTo`, `wander`, `wantTool`, `worked`, `wornHeld`, `yesterdayOf`. `people/index.ts` re-exports the old public names.
- **Band:** `band.ts` draws `band.size` people (V-016) from the valley's neighbours (`valleyOf(...).valley.near`, nearer ones more often), or `band.homeCulture` for a plain seed. Each gets the culture's race, a given name from its list, no family name, an age from maturity + `minAdult` to `ageShare`% of the lifespan, attributes from the race's means plus dice, two traits by weight that do not exclude each other, looks, and for each `band.skills` entry a potential and a level. One skill drawn towards high potentials is raised as the one they made a living by; `trade` is the best skill now. Whole bands are redrawn up to `band.tries` times until someone has every `band.must` skill at `band.able` or more, then the likeliest person is trained up to it. No two given names alike.
- **Strangers (P5.9):** `strangers.ts`, `road.ts`, `edge.ts`, `welcome.ts`, `verdict.ts`, `row.ts`: `docs/STRANGERS_AND_HOMES.md`.
- **Known skill (Bundle 6):** `band.ts` `knownOf`, `bringKnown`; strangers.json `known` and `weigh.skill`.
- **Walking (P2.3):** `walk.ts`: each minute a person on a way gains `walking.speed` stride and takes the next step once it covers the step's cost, so water and marsh slow them; a way blocked since it was found is found again from where they stand, or they stop. An idle person stands `band.wander.rest` minutes, then strolls to an open tile within `band.wander.radius` of the camp, drawn from the `people` stream. Then they see round them (map `see`, V-205).
- **Lines:** `lines.ts`: the line under which a rung of the mind fires for food, water, rest, recreation (each need's `seek`) and warmth (needs `warmLineOf`). Every reader asks `lineOf(ctx, p, need)`: the sim's line hook (`setLineHook(sim, hook)`, `Sim.hooks`, not saved) when set, told today's line, else today's (`todayLine`). A tamer's food line (`flock.tame.back`), water's while asleep and every warning line (`low`) are not hooked.
- **Priorities, the colony's day and learning (P2.5, V-015):** `priority.ts`: the player's `setPriority {person, job, level}` lever sets what matters more to someone, 1 first to 4 last, for each `work.priority.jobs` kind (a `priority` row; no row is the `start` level); a person looking for work tries the kinds in their own order (ties in content order), leaves felling to someone free and able who puts it higher, and when not able for work raised above the start level declines once per change with a `work.declined` line naming the attribute. Setting a priority makes them look again at once. `day.ts`: `dayOf` is the colony's customary day from content (`work.hours`, `life.sleep.from`); the work-hours law sets everyone's hours from it (V-099: no lever changes the day). `learn.ts`: a minute of work practises its skill (`xp`); `work.learn.start + perLevel × level` minutes raise it by one, with a `skill.rose` line citing the taking-up line. Pace, teaching: teach MODULE.md.
- **Carrying (P2.4):** `haul.ts`: anyone free carries the nearest load outside the store (`haul.load`; a plated good only until the stores hold its level, `storedEnough`; past it, it lies where made) to a store that takes it (`slotFor`, else `growStore`); home-store goods move to a store of their kind once one stands. Ripe fields come before bushes. `fetch.ts` fetches a kind for a craft. `eat.ts` eating; `tally.ts` the day's counts of logs felled, items hauled, berries picked and food eaten, moved to `yesterday` (`yesterdayOf`) at midnight, no lines.
- **The midden (V-072):** haulers carry `offal` (`work.haul.midden`) to `build` `middensOf(world)` and nowhere else; it is used up there with no line (`haul.ts`).
- **Tools (Bundle 3, V-195):** `tools.ts` (`toolFactor`, `scaled`, `worked`, worn and handed-down tools), `knap.ts` (needed tools, knapping): `docs/TOOLS_AND_SMITHY.md`.
- **State owned:** tables `person` fields from `row.ts` (newcomers) and `walk.ts` (`path`, `stride`, `rest`), `skill` (person, skill, level, potential, xp, used), `priority` (person, job, level, line, declined), the tool tables; the day's tally. The `person` table itself is declared by `people` `PEOPLE_TABLES`.
- **Invariants:** draws only from the `people` stream (the band in `init`, `wander` each minute); only teaching takes a level past its potential; imports no other people-side system.
- **Tests:** `strangers.test.ts` (slow tier); `priority.test.ts` (priorities, declining, skills, the day); `lines.test.ts` (today and food hooks; `lines.ts` is the only reader); `people.test.ts` (ten bands, each able to build and cook); `tools.test.ts`; `needed.test.ts`; `goods.test.ts`; `handdown.test.ts`.
