# goods

Items as stacks on tiles, the band's handcart, the store and the colony's stock levels (P2.4).

- **Public API:** `index.ts`: `goodsSystem`, `GOODS_TABLES`, `itemsOf(world)`, `itemsAt(world, x, y, z)` (stacks lying on a tile, not carried), `layItem(world, fields, {owner, state})` (a new stack), `takeFrom(world, item, count)` (the stack goes when emptied), `loadOf`, `stackSize(content, kind)` (life.json `stacks`, else `default`). Also exported: clothes `garmentOf`, `wornOf`, `wornIn`, `putOn`, `leftOf`, `lifeOf`, `worthFor`, `sparesOf`, `shortOf` (`docs/CLOTHES_AND_LOOM.md`); store kinds `DEPOT_TABLES`, `depotTakes`, `onDepot` and the rest of `depots.ts`; walk counts `TRIP_CELL`, `TRIP_TABLES`, `tripsOf` (`trips.ts`); the store, tools, marks and metal helpers are described in their files' headers.
- **Arrival:** in `init`, after the people, the cart is unloaded on the open tiles joined to the camp nearest the start, ring by ring: the cart itself, its goods (life.json `cart.goods`) in stacks of at most the stack size, and one bedroll a person, laid out (`state: 'laid'`) and owned by them. Each also arrives wearing a wool cloak (`cart.cloak`, V-092): held and owned, `state: 'worn'`, citing the unloading; not a load (`loadOf` is what someone carries, `cloakOf` what they wear).
- **Store (P2.4):** `store.ts`: after unloading, the band sets up the store: the first free block of `work.store` width by height open tiles joined to the camp, in rings around the cart. One stack a tile. `slotFor(world, content, kind, count, from)` is a matching stack with room for the load, else an empty store tile, nearest `from`; `growStore(ctx, cause)` adds the free open tile beside the store nearest its first tile, up to `work.store.most` tiles.
- **Stores by kind (5B Bundle 1, V-129):** `depots.ts`, table `depot` (store, x, y, z, w, h, state `plan`/`built`, line, built, builtTick): `depotsOf`, `storeKindOf` (an item kind's store kind, `buildings.stores.kinds`), `insideDepot`, `depotAt` (a built store's inside), `depotTiles`, `depotsFor`. `store.ts`: `inStore` is the home store or a built store's inside; `depotSlot` (nearest store of the kind with room), `slotFor` (that, else the home store), `inHomeStore`. `plates.ts`: `storedEnough` says every store together holds a good's plate. `trips.ts`, table `trip` (kind, use, x, y, n: a 28-slot daily ring in 4-tile squares): `noteTrip`, `rollTrips` (daily), `tripsFor` (gathered and used points over `days`), `noteUse` (a carried good used up off the store). Stacks per kind: `life.stacks`.
- **Stock levels (P2.4, the player's lever, V-015):** a `stock` row per `work.stock` good holding a level id (`none`, `some`, `plenty`: 0, 50 or 200; wood starts at `some`). The `setStock` command `{ good, level }` changes it. `wantOf` is the level's count; `haveOf(world, content, good)` counts the good wherever it is (stored, lying, carried), by item kind or by the item's `good` in items.json. A food row starts at plenty.
- **Plans (P3.1):** `raiseStock(ctx, good, part, cause)`: a plan needing a good kept at a zero level raises it to the first non-zero level, with a `goods.stockRaised` line citing the plan. `freeTile` is false where a part is planned or built, on a store of a kind or a field.
- **Test marks (`?debug` only, V-015):** `marks.ts`: `markFell {x, y}` adds a `mark` row on a surface tree or fallen log, else is refused; `markedTree` gives the nearest to a free feller, `unmark` clears it. `markStore {x, y}` adds a store tile on free open ground (`goods.storeMarked`), else is refused.
- **Seed (P4.7, V-089):** item `seed` (not food), 80 in the cart and a Stock plate row (start some); harvest keeps seed back to its want. Life.json `lastResort` foods (seed) are eaten only when hungry with nothing else to reach and do not count towards `goods.foodGone`.
- **Food (P2.6):** `food.ts`: `eatFrom(ctx, item, eater)` takes one from a food stack; with no food left lying anywhere it writes `goods.foodGone` (notable), kept in state `foodGone` and read by `foodGoneLine(world)`, which hunger cites.
- **Metal (Bundle 3):** `metal.ts`: `ingotWant(world, content, metal)` (plate want less held, plus the inputs short bronze needs), `plannedRuns`, `charcoalNeeded`, `wantedBy`. Item `trail` (JSON steps): `trailOf`, `trailText`, `withStep`, `mergeTrails`, `oreTrail`. TODO: miners stamp the ore tile through `withStep`.
- **Tools:** `tools.ts` (`toolOf`, `speedOf`, `freeTools`, `heldBy`, `layTool`, `restOf`, `HELD`...), `docs/TOOLS_AND_SMITHY.md`.
- **Events:** emits `goods.unloaded` (goods, beds), citing the `people.arrived` line (every item from the cart cites it as `made`); `goods.storeSet` (width, height) and the first `goods.stockSet` (word, good), citing the unloading; `goods.stockSet` for each level change; `goods.storeGrew` (tiles), citing the load's line.
- **State owned:** tables `item` (kind, count, quality, x, y, z, holder (who carries it, or 0), owner (whose it is, or 0), state, wear, worked, trail, made), `store` (x, y, z, made) and `stock` (good, level, line). No system state; `every: day` with no `run`, since stacks change through the people who use them.
- **Invariants:** one stack of a kind a tile from the unloading; counts never above the stack size; a used-up item is removed (entity MODULE.md).
- **Tests:** `src/engine/needs/needs.test.ts` (the cart unloads once, a bedroll each, the rations go down as the band eats); `src/engine/people/jobs.test.ts` (the store is set up; the stock level's logs reach it with no order; the lever and its refusal).
