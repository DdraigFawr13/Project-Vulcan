# law

Laws the colony keeps and how each person answers them (P4.8, V-091): the work-hours law (Short days, Customary, Long days), Strangers (P5.9), and Rations, Punishment and Sickness (P6.5, V-157, V-171).

- **Public API:** `index.ts`: `lawSystem`, `LAW_TABLES`, `settingOf(world, content, law)`, `lawDef`, `lawRow`, `hoursOf(world, content, person)` (their own hours: the law's, or the customary ones when they refuse), `lawHours`, `customaryHours`, `hoursFor`, `isWinter`, `weigh(world, content, person, setting)` (willingness, answer, what weighed for and against, the memory lines), `leanOf(setting, memory)`, `WORK_HOURS`; Rations: `RATIONS`, `rationed(world, content, person)` (keeps the law and has eaten today's share; people `mind.ts` asks before going to eat), `ateFood(ctx, person, gives)` (people `eat.ts` after each meal).
- **Lever:** `setLaw {law, setting}` (V-041: the only say; nobody is ordered). A new setting writes `law.set`, then everyone answers with `law.answered` (citing it and the memories that weighed); The law's `start` setting (the custom) clears the answers; a `split` law (Strangers) is answered only at a split. A grumbler gains the setting's `grumbled` memory.
- **Weighing (content `laws.json`):** `start` − the setting's `ask` + (mood − half the scale) ÷ `moodPer` + `low` per low need + trait and memory leans + the leans of held roles and an unfinished ambition (settings `roles`, `ambitions`; engine self, V-170) + `winterLean` in winter + a setting's `store` lean (the store lasts to the harvest or not; goods `storeLasts`) + the colony's leans (`leans.ts`, P6.6: a faction's and the elder's first, standing after; engine colony; `weigh(…, {alone})` leaves the faction and elder out); a need in the setting's `expects` (hunger under rations) is not held against it; `obey` or more obeys, `grumble` or more grumbles, else refuses (keeps the customary hours).
- **Settling (P6.5):** someone who changed their answer holds it `answer.settle` days before changing again (the answer's `at`), unless they have just come to stand with a faction (P6.6). Someone who joins while a law stands answers it within the hour, citing the line that set it. A change between two settings carries each person's `left` (capped at the new share) and `flouted` over; a supper or a carer's feeding comes off the share but is never a full-plate line (`eat(…, chose=false)`).
- **Rations (P6.5, `rations.ts`):** a setting with `share` gives each answer `left`: the share per cent of a full day's food (the food need's daily fall), filled each midnight (a keeper's overspend carries; a refuser's does not). A keeper eats only while some is left; held back and hungry they keep the setting's `kept` memory (a grumbler also `grumbled`). A refuser eating past the share writes `law.keptCustom` "took a full plate" at most once in `answer.every` days, seen as for hours (`flout.ts`).
- **Punishment and Sickness (P6.5):** answered like the rest; Punishment's effects come with theft and brawls (Bundle 6). `sickness.ts`: `sickRule(world, content)` (the law in force whose setting has `sick`: `house` or `away`, its setting and set line) and `answerTo(world, law, person)`; people `apart.ts` acts on it and build `sickhouse.ts` sites the house.
- **The Strangers law (P5.9):** settings with `lets` and no hours, answered through `answerNow` when it settles a split: see `docs/STRANGERS_AND_HOMES.md`.
- **Hours:** Customary is the colony's day (content `work.hours`; no lever changes it, V-099), in winter the custom's `winter` hours (V-092). Other settings have `hours` and `winter`. Bed stays at the colony's bedtime. people `mind.ts` reads `hoursOf` per person.
- **Hourly:** at the hour a refuser's day and the law's part (the customary end under Long, the law's end under Short), an awake refuser writes `law.keptCustom`; awake people on the same level within `sight` tiles, or in the same house, write `law.saw` and gain the setting's `saw` memory about them. Anyone holding a memory that weighs on the setting and not yet weighed answers again; a changed answer is a line (`now`). Grumblers' memory is refreshed at the law's end of day.
- **State owned:** tables `law` (law, setting, line, changed) and `answer` (person, law, answer, will, pro, con, cites, mems, line, at, left, flouted). No system state.
- **Invariants:** reads people rows, traits, needs, mood and memories; writes only its tables and memories through needs `addMemory`. No RNG.
- **Tests:** `laws6.test.ts` (every answer to a new Rations, Punishment or Sickness setting is a line citing it; a keeper is held to the share, a refuser's full plate is seen); `law.test.ts` (two people answer the same law differently, each citing its memories; a refusal seen spreads a memory; Customary clears the answers; the warmth line moves with traits and memories).
