# wild

Game herds and the valley's one named predator pack (P5.4a, V-110, V-111), the prints they leave on snow and mud (V-122) and what a killed animal leaves. Hunting, fishing, fleeing and attacks are the people's side (`people/hunt.ts`, `fish.ts`, `flee.ts`, `fight.ts`), which uses the API below.

- **Public API:** `index.ts`: `wildSystem` (after `weatherSystem` in `SYSTEMS`), `WILD_TABLES`, `herdsOf(world)`, `animalsOf(world)`, `herdOf(world, id)`, `packOf(world)` (the pack row while it is in the valley), `predatorsNear(world, x, y, r)` (wolf or bear rows within `r` tiles, the larger axis distance), `markAttack(ctx, line)` (sets `pack.attacked`, counts it), `spareCount(content)` and `huntable(world, content)` (herds with more than the spare, largest first), `killAnimal(ctx, animal, byPerson, cause, type = 'wild.killed', item = 'game')` (removes it; lays `item` x meat in stack-sized stacks, its leftovers and one `offal` where it fell, owned by nobody, each citing the new `wild.killed` line; adds to the herd's `taken` when `byPerson` is a person; returns the line; a kept animal, a flock's, writes no `wild.killed`: the goods cite `cause`, which is returned), `tameAnimal(ctx, animal, keptHerdId, fields, line = 0)` (moves a wild animal into a kept herd, applies `fields`, counts it in the wild herd's `taken`, sets that herd's line to `line` and runs its low check), `wildHerdsOf(world)`, `speciesOf(content, id)`, `printsAt(world, content, i)` ({kind, name, ageHours, dir} or undefined), `setLures(ctx, from, lures)` (replaces source `from`'s list of `{tile, where, rank}`, `where` a key of `wild.pack.where`: `midden`, `flock`; empty takes them away), `packArrival(world, content)` ({tile, where} of the lure the pack reached on this visit, `where` the words a line uses, else undefined; cleared at dawn and by `feedPack`), `feedPack(ctx, cause)` (the pack has eaten: hunger 0, `lastKill` now, line `cause`; it goes home as at dawn), `scareHerd(ctx, herd)` (a failed stalk: every animal runs), `woundPredator(ctx, animal, by, cause)` (a blow from a person: the first hurts the animal (`hurt`, it runs `folk.limp` minutes, `wild.predatorWounded`), the `folk.hits`th kills it through `killAnimal` as `wild.predatorKilled` and lowers the pack's size), `drawOff(ctx, cause)` (the pack goes home: `wild.packDrewOff`), `approach(ctx, animal, x, y)` (one step nearer), `membersOf`, `speciesOf`, `withoutWild(content)` (for tests of other systems: no hunting or fishing, a pack that never visits or attacks). `markAttack` also records `attackedAt`.
- **Kept animals:** a `herd` row with `kept` set to a pasture's id and its `animal` rows (`sex`, `born`, `young`, `tamedBy`, `cull`, `shorn`, `dies`) belong to the flock module. The wild system skips them in movement and flight, snow loss, breeding, strays, `herdLow`, the pack's in-valley eating (and so `gameWords`) and `huntable`; placement never makes one. Wild never imports flock.
- **Content:** all numbers are `wild.json` (the people's side is its `folk` block; hunting and fishing dials are `work.json` `hunt`, `fish`) (schema `schemas-wild.ts`): species (herds = weighted biome tiles / `perHerd`; deer have art kinds `hind` and `stag` in a pattern), predators, prints, soft grounds, spare, placement, movement, winter months and the pack's dials. Items `hide`, `bone`, `fat`, `offal`; `seasons.json` `snowMelt` and `snowShare`.
- **Placement (`init`, `wild` stream):** herds on open ground their species stands on, reachable from the start and at least `place.start` tiles from it, `place.apart` from each other; the pack is the first predator whose creature (`creatures.json`) lives in a biome of the valley, else the last (a cave bear); named from the creature's `names` with `{near}` the nearest neighbour (`valleyOf`), or `pack.near` on a plain seed; its den is the open tile at least `den.from` away with the most trees about it.
- **Movement:** `move.ts`, every `move.every` minutes. Within its range an animal walks straight at a point and stops where the ground stops it (`stepCost`; never water or built walls); one that strayed finds a way home (`findPath`). A person within the species' flee distance sets a herd's animals running (`flee`, a step every `fleeEvery`, for `fleeFor`). The pack idles within `pack.roam` of the den.
- **Daily (midnight):** the snow takes animals on snow-lying tiles (`snowLoss` x herd/cap), the pack eats beyond the valley (`offMap`, less in winter months and on `harsh` days) or among the herds (`inValley` x game/`gameFull`, more under snow) or `hunger` rises; `wild.packHungry` at `hungry` days and each `hungryEvery` after; a pack below `least` leaves; on the first of `returns.month` a new one may come. Monthly: births in a species' breeding months and a stray to a herd under its start outside winter (no lines; state only).
- **Visits:** at `visit` days hungry, between `night.from` and `night.to`, at most every `visitEvery` days, the pack trots (`move.trot`) to the lowest-ranked lure (nearest the den among equals), else the town ring's edge on the line from the green; the goal is kept for the night. The first to arrive writes `wild.packAtEdge` with the lure's `where` words, and `packArrival` then reports it. At dawn they go home.
- **Prints:** `prints.ts`. A step onto ground that holds prints (snow lies, or a wet day on a soft ground) writes `map.printKind`, `printAt`, `printDir`. Chunk versions are raised once an hour for chunks that took prints, and at midnight for chunks whose prints went; prints past `printHours` are not returned and are swept at midnight.
- **Events:** emits `wild.packNamed`, `wild.herdLow`, `wild.packKilled` (quiet), `wild.packHungry`, `wild.packAtEdge` (notable), `wild.packLeft`, `wild.killed`, `wild.predatorWounded`, `wild.predatorKilled`, `wild.packDrewOff`; reads none. The people's side adds `wild.tracksFound`, `wild.sighted`, `wild.attacked` (notable). Each cites the line before it in the herd's or pack's story, or the weather spell.
- **State owned:** tables `herd`, `animal`, `pack` (`wild.ts`); system state `open`, `fleeing`, `trot`, `dirtyN`, `lure_<from>_n` / `lure_<from>_<k>` / `lurew_…` / `lurer_…` (each source's lures: tile plus one, word index, rank), `goal`, `goalW`, `goalLure`, `reached` (the night's goal and whether the pack is there); `map.printKind`, `printAt`, `printDir`.
- **Invariants:** whole-number maths and the `wild` stream only; one pack row; an animal is on open ground; a herd's `start` is its starting size.
- **Tests:** `wild.test.ts` (slow tier): herds and a named pack on `anvil/1`; a print on snow gone after a day; `killAnimal`'s goods and line; a hard winter with no meals (hungry lines citing a low herd or the spell, the pack at the edge); snow lies and melts; a pack down to one leaves and a new one returns.
