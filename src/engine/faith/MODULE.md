# faith

Faith, shrines and feast days (6B Bundle 4, P6.8, V-143, V-187): everyone holds the faith of their home people with a devotion; the faithful raise a shrine at the colony's first reason to gather, the most devout keeps it as priest, prayer there lifts the faithful, feast days are asked for from ledger lines and kept under the Feasts law, and what a faith holds sacred leans law answers. No lever but the Feasts law (V-041).

- **Public API:** `index.ts`: `faithSystem` (in `SYSTEMS` after family, hourly, stream `faith`), `FAITH_TABLES`, table reads (`creedsOf`, `creedOf`, `faithOf`, `holdersOf`, `holydaysOf`, `mainCreed`, `shrineOf`, `isShrine`, `devotionWord`, `faithWords`), `praying(ctx, p)` (people mind, at leisure), `holyHours` (a feast day's work ends at noon), `holyToday`, `keptDays`, `dayWords`, `FEASTS` (the law id), `holyPlace` and `keepHoly` (people feast), `shrineBuilt` (crafts carpentry).
- **Content:** `worship.json` (schema `schemas-worship.ts`): `devotion` (`spread`, `trait`, `devout`, `words`), `shrine` (`kind`, `faiths` with `kind`/`water`, `near`, `least`), `pray` (`minutes`, `memory`, `every`), `lean`, `grieved`, `feast` (`noon`, `leanDays`, `lean`), `why`, `days` (kinds `arrival`, `death`, `wedding`: name, words, `feast`, `rite`, `remember` memories); `faiths.json` (`priestTitle`, `sacred`, `abhors`); law `feasts` in `laws.json` (`feasts` per setting) and setting `values` tags; furniture `shrine-stone`, `shrine`; events `faith.*`.
- **Belief (`believe.ts`):** a person with no faith row gets their culture's `religion`: devotion its value, ± `spread` (stream `faith`), + `devout` with the trait, 0 to 10. A faith's first holder copies it into a `creed` row, so a later cult or schism is one more row.
- **Shrine (`shrine.ts`, daily):** once `least` living people hold a creed and the colony has had its first death, wedding or harvest home, the creed sites its shrine kind on open ground nearest the first hearth (else the green) within `near`, by water first for a faith that wants it, reachable from the green, as a builders' plan (`faith.shrine`, citing that reason; a later creed cites its holders' arrivals). Carpentry's last stroke calls `shrineBuilt` (`faith.shrineBuilt`). Hourly a creed with a shrine and no living priest names its most devout grown holder (`faith.priest`, citing the old priest's death).
- **Prayer (`pray.ts`):** at leisure, a holder whose last prayer is `every` days old for their devotion walks beside a built shrine and prays `minutes` (`doing` `pray`, which feeds recreation), then gains `pray.memory`. The lukewarm never go.
- **Feast days (`days.ts`):** the arrival, the first death and each later one closely mourned by `grieved`% of the living and the first wedding are noted as `unasked` rows; the priest of the main creed asks for each (`faith.dayAsked`, citing it). The Feasts law keeps the first `feasts` asked days (by line) beside the harvest home. On the day at `noon` (`faith.feastDay`), work stops (unless one refuses the law), and build `callHoly` calls the supper; an empty store gives `faith.noFeast`, under `leanDays` of food the day is lean (`faith.leanDay`).
- **The rite (`rite.ts`):** the gathering is round the main shrine; each creed whose priest is there has its rite (`faith.rite`, its `rite` memory for holders there), then the supper (`faith.feast`, the day's `feast` memory, or `feast.lean` when lean); on a death's day those who were here then gain `remember`.
- **Leans:** law `faith.ts` reads these tables as data (law does not import faith): a devout holder leans `lean` × devotion / 10 for a setting whose `values` their faith holds sacred, against for one it abhors.
- **State owned:** tables `creed`, `faithful`, `holyday`; feast columns `holy`, `lean` are build's.
- **Invariants:** stream `faith` only; whole numbers; one faith row per person; a holy day is kept at most once a year.
- **Tests:** `faith.test.ts` (shrine and priest citing the reason; prayer lifts only the faithful; a cart day kept with the priest's rite reaching back to its asking; a death becomes a day and an empty store skips it; the Wells' kin lean on Sickness).
