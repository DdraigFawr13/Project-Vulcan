# defy

Defiance (P6.7, V-160): a grievance against a law climbs a ladder one step at a time (grumble, work slowly, refuse, take from the store, brawl, leave the valley), and every act's line cites the memory and the belief behind it.

- **Public API:** `index.ts`: `defySystem`, `coolOf`, `DEFY_TABLES`, `defyOf`, `stepOf`, `stepAt`, `atLeast(world, content, person, step)`, `slacks`, `sentenceOf(world, content, person, day)`, `leavingOf`, `stashOf`, `takesWith`, `pilfer(ctx, p, food)`, `grievanceOf`, `reasonsOf(world, content, person, setting)` (the memory and belief), `whyWords`.
- **Content:** `defy.json` (`steps` with `answer` or `bar`, `hold` and `traits`; `belief`, `hold`, `cool`; `slow`, `take`, `brawl`, `leave`, `starving`; `memories`; `punish` sentences; the `standing` cause for leaving); laws.json settings' `beliefs` (what carries a grievance into acts) and Punishment's `punish` (`work`, `stocks`, `away`); tales.json tales `full-plate`, `theft`, `brawl`, `left`.
- **Grievance (`why.ts`):** the law a person is furthest short of keeping (laws.json `answer.obey` less their answer's `will`); its memory is the held one weighing most against the setting; its belief the strongest at `belief` or more among the setting's `beliefs`. Act lines cite the answer, the memory and the belief.
- **Ladder (`ladder.ts`, daily at `hour`):** answer steps follow the law answer at once (grumbles, refuses); an act step needs the grievance at its `bar` (traits move it), the memory and the belief, `hold` days (or the step's own) on the step below, and for leaving a faction (colony) or `leave.misery` days under `leave.mood`, with nobody else leaving in `leave.every` days. `cool` days under a step's bar step them down one (`defy.cooled` from an act); `coolOf`: a close friend of a calling holder with a `cool` push (the peacemaker, engine calling) cools in that many days.
- **Acts (`acts.ts`):** `defy.slow` on reaching it (people idle one work minute in `slow.every`); `pilfer`: after eating from a store stack, at the `take` step, or (`hungerWhy`, no law behind it) holding the `starving.memory` and the `starving.belief` at `belief` while the store will not last to the harvest, at most once in `take.every` days, `take.count` more pocketed as a held stash (`state: 'hid'`, not a load) eaten first (`defy.took`); hourly `brawls`: at a meal, leisure or work, once in `brawl.every` days, with the one the memory is about or the nearest keeper within `reach` (`defy.brawled`, health `wound` for both, `deep` per cent deep, the `brawled` memory). Anyone awake who sees it (needs `seenBy`) catches them (`defy.caught`; the `sawTheft` or `sawBrawl` memory).
- **Punishment (`punish.ts`):** caught, the setting's `punish` sentences them (`defy.punished`) with a defy.json `punish` entry, so a new sentence is data: `day` `work` (tomorrow worked to bedtime) or `green` (tomorrow's working day there, the stocks), its `memory`, or `leave` (leaves now). A sentence that is not `leave` steps them one step down the ladder (`stepDown`). Refusers of the law who saw the act gain the `unfair` memory, which leans them against it.
- **Leaving (`leave.ts`):** `defy.left` names the road, who goes with them (housemates who share the grievance: at `take` or more, the same law their biggest grievance, holding its memory and belief) and what they take (clothes, bedroll, stash; tools are left); the rest gain the `left` memory; standing falls by `standing` (colony `addCause`).
- **People side:** people `defy.ts`, from `mind.ts`: `departing` (walk to the edge, then status `gone`), `eatStash`, `extraWork`, `inStocks` (`doing: 'stocks'`), `slacking`.
- **State owned:** tables `defy` (person, law, step, since, under, misery, line, mem, why, belief, took, brawled), `sentence` (person, kind, day, line), `leaving` (person, line); state `left`.
- **Invariants:** draws only from the `combat` stream (brawls); reads answers, memories, beliefs, traits, moods, factions and items; writes its own tables, memories, wounds and stash items. Must not import `people`.
- **Tests:** `defy.test.ts` (a hard winter on anvil: works slowly, takes, brawls; every act cites the answer, a memory and a belief and walks back to a full plate; a thief seen is remembered); `hard.test.ts` (the store out lowers standing; the starving take at full plates; a housemate without the belief stays).
