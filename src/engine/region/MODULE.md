# region

The lighter world map (P1.5, DECISIONS V-017): the region around the valley, its named neighbours and the three valleys on offer.

- **Public API:** `index.ts`: `makeRegion(seed, content, withRaster = false)` (pure; the player's seed without a valley number; draws from its own `region` stream state made from that seed, never from a world's streams), `siteOf(seed)` (`<seed>/<n>` gives the region seed and valley `n`; a plain seed gives undefined), `valleyOf(seed, content)` (the region and the valley a world seed settles), `regionSystem`, and the types `Region`, `RegionCell`, `RegionRange`, `RegionSite`, `RegionValley`, `RegionNeighbour`.
- **Generator:** `region.ts` lays an 80 by 56 grid (`region.json` `grid`) of height from two value-noise octaves, warmth falling from south to north and with height, rain from noise and heavier in the west, biomes from the forked temperature-by-rainfall table (lakes below `lake`, alpine above `alpine`). Up to two rivers run downhill from the wettest high cells to the edge or a lake, refused if they twist too much. Joined mountain cells of `ranges.min` or more are named ranges; those of `ranges.label` or more are written on the map where they clear the sites. Three valleys sit in low inland ground walled by heights on `valleys.walls` of eight rays and away from lakes, one per terrain.json climate where the region has ground for it (otherwise the best walled ground left, then any low ground), each given the closest climate. Six to eight sites (`sites.count`) are spaced apart; each draws a culture weighted by its preferred biomes and site preferences (river, heights, plains, forest, deposit), repeats discouraged, and a name from that culture's place syllables, unique in the region; its kind follows the race (`kinds`). Each valley is named in its nearest site's tongue (`vales`) and lists its nearest three sites with a compass word and walking days (long side plus half the short, times `daysPerCell`). `land.ts` holds the noise, rivers and ranges, `names.ts` the names and bearings. No roots or trigonometry (V-006). With `withRaster`, the fields are sampled four times per cell for drawing.
- **World seeds:** a world seed `<seed>/<n>` is valley `n` of region `<seed>`: every stream derives from that whole string, so the three valleys differ in everything, and the map generator takes the valley's climate (`valleyOf` in `map.ts`). A plain seed (tests, tools, worlds from before P1.5) has no region.
- **Events:** emits `region.named` (the written ranges and the rivers, and the site count) and `region.settled` (the valley, its range, climate and nearest neighbours), both citing `world.begun` (no "why?"), in `init`, only for a valley seed; reads none.
- **State owned:** none. The region is made again from the seed when asked (about 20 to 40 ms with the raster), so saves do not carry it.
- **Invariants:** the same seed and content give the same region; site names are unique within a region, valley names unique among the three; every valley's climate is a terrain.json climate id.
- **Tests:** `region.test.ts`: the P1.5 done-when (the map seeds give distinct, named neighbourhoods of six to eight sites, three named valleys each), a valley seed settles with its climate and its two lines, a plain seed has none.
