# people

The band and the people of the valley: who they are (P2.1) and how they walk (P2.3) what they choose to do each minute (P2.2) and the work they take up themselves (P2.4).

- **Public API:** `index.ts`: `peopleSystem` (draws the band in `init`), `strangersSystem` (P5.9, right after it in `SYSTEMS`, stream `strangers`), `PEOPLE_TABLES`, `drawBand(rng, content, homes)` (pure: the band from a list of homes), `tradeOf(band, skills)`, `dayOf(world, content)` (the colony's day), `levelOf(world, content, person, job)` and `declinedLine(world, person, job)` (priorities), `progressOf(content, skillRow)` (practice towards the next level), types `Person`, `Home`, `Day`.
- **Band:** `band.ts` draws `band.size` people (V-016) from the valley's neighbours (`valleyOf(...).valley.near`, nearer ones more often), or `band.homeCulture` for a plain seed. Each gets the culture's race, a given name from its list, no family name, an age from maturity + `minAdult` to `ageShare`% of the lifespan, attributes from the race's means plus dice, two traits by weight that do not exclude each other, looks, and for each `band.skills` entry a potential and a level. One skill drawn towards high potentials is raised as the one they made a living by; `trade` is the best skill now. Whole bands are redrawn up to `band.tries` times until someone has every `band.must` skill at `band.able` or more, then the likeliest person is trained up to it. No two given names alike.
- **Strangers (P5.9):** `strangers.ts`, `edge.ts`, `welcome.ts`, `verdict.ts`, `row.ts`: see `docs/STRANGERS_AND_HOMES.md`.
- **Walking (P2.3):** `walk.ts`: each minute a person on a way gains `walking.speed` stride and takes the next step once it covers the step's cost, so water and marsh slow them; a way blocked since it was found is found again from where they stand, or they stop. Someone with nothing to do stands `band.wander.rest` minutes, then strolls to an open tile within `band.wander.radius` of the camp, drawn from the `people` stream.
- **Mind (P2.2):** `mind.ts`: after the minute's steps each person chooses, in order: eat when food is under its `seek` (walk to the nearest food stack lying on the ground, eat one there; if none can be reached, look again in an hour), sleep from the colony's bedtime until work starts (`day.ts`) or when rest is under its `seek` (at their own laid-out bedroll, else where they stand) until it is morning and rest has reached `sleep.wake`, take their leisure from the end of work or when recreation is under its `seek` (strolling; under it a free person takes up no new work, `jobs.ts`), else stroll; work hours and bedtime are the person's own from law `hoursOf` (P4.8: the law's, or the customary ones for a refuser). The choice is the `doing` field (`eat`, `sleep`, `relax`, ''), which the needs system reads; none of it is a ledger line.
- **Work (P2.4, V-015):** `jobs.ts`: no one is ordered. In the colony's work hours, after eating and sleep, a free person works: someone carrying a load takes it to the store (`slotFor`, else `growStore`; onto a matching stack, else its own tile); someone able to fell (the `jobs.json` job `work.fell.job`: its attribute requirements, strength 8) takes it up while the colony's wood (`haveOf`, plus the trees others are felling) is under its stock level (`wantOf`): a fallen log first, then the standing tree nearest the store within `fell.reach`, never within `fell.camp` of the start, worked from one of its four sides for `minutes × 2 × skilled ÷ (skilled + woodcutting level)` minutes; the feature goes and its logs lie around its tile. Anyone else carries the nearest load outside the store (`haul.load`; a plated good only until the store holds its level, `storedEnough`; then food goes to a granary).
- **Priorities, the colony's day and learning (P2.5, V-015):** `priority.ts`: the player's `setPriority {person, job, level}` lever sets what matters more to someone, 1 first to 4 last, for each `work.priority.jobs` kind (a `priority` row; no row is the `start` level); a person looking for work tries the kinds in their own order (ties in content order), leaves felling to someone free and able who puts it higher, and when not able for work raised above the start level declines once per change with a `work.declined` line naming the attribute. Setting a priority makes them look again at once. `day.ts`: `dayOf` is the colony's customary day from content (`work.hours`, `life.sleep.from`); the work-hours law sets everyone's hours from it (V-099: no lever changes the day). `learn.ts`: each minute of felling practises woodcutting (`xp` on the skill row); `work.learn.start + perLevel × level` minutes raise it by one, never past potential, with a `skill.rose` line citing the taking-up line.
- **Foraging and the day's tally (P2.9, V-015):** `forage.ts`: the same shape as felling, for food. While food is under its stock level, a free person takes up foraging: the nearest ripe bush within `forage.reach` of the store that they can get beside, worked for `minutes × 2 × skilled ÷ (skilled + foraging level)` minutes; the bush stays on the map, its `amount` of berries lie on the open tiles around it for haulers; a `bush` row records the picking. A bush is ripe when the month is in `forage.months` and it was never picked or not for `regrowDays` days; out of season none is. `tally.ts`: per-day counts of logs felled, items hauled, berries picked and food eaten, moved to `yesterday` (`yesterdayOf`) at midnight. No ledger lines.
- **Quarrying and building (P3.1, V-041):** quarrying is felling's twin (`jobs.ts` `Gather`): while stone (`work.quarry.good`) is under its stock level, someone able for the `quarry` job breaks up the boulder nearest the store within `quarry.reach` for `quarry.minutes` (shorter with stoneworking) and its stone lies around for haulers. `build.ts`: a free person able for the `buildings.job` takes up the nearest free plan, fetches its material, brings it, builds it from beside the tile, or takes a part down that was ordered down; the `plan` field holds the plan's id.
- **Mining (P4.1, V-086):** `mine.ts` (+ `shaft.ts`, `clay.ts`, `minetask.ts`), the Mine row after Quarry (`work.mine`). While `oreShort` > 0 a free able person takes up the nearest vein tile they can get beside (`vein` minutes), else the metal's shaft: sinking a level (`shaft` minutes; the first takes the `ladder` logs, fetched until held) or, once done, tunnelling (`nextDig`, `dig` minutes a tile); one of each a shaft. Else clay for a wall plan (`mine.clayDug`). Ore and rubble are hauled up (`haul.kinds`). More in mine `MODULE.md`.
- **Carpentry and beds (P3.4, P3.5, V-041, V-061 to V-066):** `carpentry.ts`, a Work sheet kind after Building (`carpentry`, the crafting skill). A free able person takes up the bench plan, else a piece left part-made, else build `nextWant`, fetches the logs (`stackOf`), works beside the bench `minutes × skilled ÷ (skilled + level)`, and the piece is set at the spot chosen at the start; quality = crafting level ± up to `roll` from the `craft` stream (`work.tookUpCarpentry`, `furniture.made`, `build.waiting` when wood cannot be reached). `claim.ts`: at bedtime someone with no bed takes the free bed nearest their bedroll, in their own house first (`people.claimedBed`); the bedroll goes back to the store.
- **Cooking (V-090, V-041):** `cook.ts`, the Cook row (after Forage; the cooking skill; content `work.cook`). While meals anywhere plus those being made are under `perPerson` × people alive, and a lit fire, firewood in the store and a stack with a batch's worth of raw food (`rawFor`: the meals' food value over `gain` times the raw food's; `from` order, grain first) can be reached, a free able person takes that food and one firewood, stands beside the nearest lit hearth, works `minutes × 2 × skilled ÷ (skilled + level)`, and lays `batch` meals for haulers (`work.tookUpCooking` citing the hearth and priority; `food.cooked`). Otherwise the band eats raw. `mind.ts` walks to a meal up to `cook.prefer` tiles further than the nearest food, eats a meal first on a shared tile, and a meal adds `ate-well` (needs). A worn cloak is held but not a load: use goods `loadOf` for what someone carries.
- **Farming (P4.7, V-015, V-089):** `farm.ts`, a Work sheet kind after Forage (`farm`, jobs.json, the farming skill; content `work.farm` with minutes a tile to `sow` and `reap` at skill `skilled`). A free person takes up reaping a ripe field, else sowing a field waiting in a month its crop is sown in when seed can be reached (`work.tookUpReaping` citing the field's `field.ripe`; `work.tookUpSowing` citing its last line). A sower fetches seed from the store, then works tile by tile from beside each, one seed a tile, shorter with farming skill (practising it); the `plan` person field holds the field's id. Each reaped tile lays its yield on it for haulers: seed first, until the colony's seed (`haveOf`) reaches the Seed plate's want, then the crop's item. The last tile sown or reaped ends in build `finishSowing` / `finishReaping`. Seed grain is life.json `lastResort`: `mind.ts` eats it only under the food warning line with no other food to reach, one `goods.ateSeed` line a person a day (person field `seeded`).
- **The wild valley, the people's side (P5.4a, V-041, V-072, V-109 to V-112, V-122):** `hunt.ts`, `fish.ts` (Wild heading, Hunt and Fish rows after Forage; content `work.hunt`, `work.fish`, `wild.folk`). While food is under `from` per cent of its Food plate a free able person takes up hunting (`work.tookUpHunting`; at most `crew`): the largest herd worth the hunt (`huntable`: never one at its last `spare`), nearest the green among equals, stalked games before hares; they walk to it and, within `near` tiles of a calm herd, stalk it: every `every` minutes a kill chance of `chance` + `perLevel` per level of hunting, times the spear's speed (`killAnimal`, the meat and leftovers lie for haulers), else the herd bolts (`scareHerd`). A stalking hunter does not frighten a herd (wild leaves `job: hunt` persons out of the frightened). Hares are snared, one an `snare.every` minutes. A hunter on fresh hoof prints (`follow.hours`) turns to the herd they lead to (`work.followedTracks`, once a hunt). If the pack attacked within `tracks.packDays`, two who put Hunt above `pack.level` and see fresh paw prints near the town go after it (`work.trackingPack`; task `pack`). Hunting and fishing end at dusk and when the plate is full. Fishing: the nearest unfrozen stream, river or lake tile reachable, a catch chance each `every` minutes (`fish.chance` + `perLevel`), the fish laid by the angler (state `fished` counts them); frozen water ends it. `tracksMinute` (each minute, anyone on the surface): fresh wolf prints within the ring + `tracks.ring` write `wild.tracksFound` (once a game day, citing the pack's hungry line) and give `saw-tracks`.
- **Fight or flee, and the pack's attacks (V-109, V-111):** `flee.ts` `predatorRung`, the first thing `care.ts` `danger` asks. Awake on the surface, with a predator not at its den (`folk.den`) in sight (`folk.sight`, day or night): stand if bold (a `folk.bold` trait, no `timid` one), armed (`folk.armed` tool kind held) and another within `together` tiles is standing or bold and armed; else flee to the nearest closed house (`roomAt`) or away. `doing` is `stand` or `flee` while one stays in sight. Everyone newly in sight decides together; one `wild.sighted` line an hour names them (citing the pack's edge or hungry line); the standers get `stood-together`. `fight.ts`: with pack hunger at `attackAfter` and night, a wolf within `prowl` tiles walks towards, and at one tile falls on, a person who is alone in the open or the last fleeing to shelter (`exposed`): up to `rounds` blows; each blow standing armed people beside the target strike first (`strike`, by hunting level and weapon), a hit hurts the wolf (the second kills it, wild `woundPredator`); a miss, and it may bite (`bite`: part and depth by weight, into `health` `wound`). One `wild.attacked` line citing the sighting, `markAttack`, `saw-attack` for the awake within `witness`, then `drawOff` (`wild.packDrewOff`). `syncLures` tells wild where the midden is.
- **The midden (V-072):** haulers carry `offal` (`work.haul.midden`) to `build` `middensOf(world)` and nowhere else; it is used up there with no line (`haul.ts`).
- **Herding (Phase 5 Bundle 3, V-041):** `herd.ts` + `herdtask.ts`, a Work sheet kind after Farming (`herd`, the farming skill; content `flock`). Nobody is ordered; the Herding row is the only lever. Two jobs are the day's own and come before the herder's other work, even outside work hours (the hook is the first line of `working`, so after eating, sleep, being hurt and warming in `mind.ts`, which are unchanged): *fold* from `night.from` less `fold.before` minutes while a flock has animals outside the fold and is not folded (shut it in, to `night.to` plus `fold.open`), and *let out* after that while it is folded. The free herder who puts Herding highest (ties in person order; not carrying a load or a body, not at work they rank above Herding) takes it; from `fold.wait` minutes into the duty's time any free herder may; either only with a way to beside the fold gate. They walk there, work `fold.minutes` and call `foldFlock` / `openFlock`. At `night.from`, before the flock system, `herdWatch` writes `noteLeftOut` once with the reason of whoever holds the fold duty, else the best-placed herder (`why`: abed, warming, eating, toFold (on the way), busy ({work} the Work sheet kind's name), noWay (free but no way to the gate), none). At most `perWorkplace` (jobs.json `herd`) herders work one flock at once (the fold duty aside). Work-hours tasks in order: butcher a marked animal (`cull.minutes`; whoever tamed it gets `memories.cull`), shear (`shear.minutes`), carry cured hay to the rick (the `hay` person field, `hay.carry` a trip), mow (`hay.mow` minutes shorter with farming skill), tame (at most `tame.crew` at once on a flock; walk to `flee + 2` tiles of the herd's nearest animal, sit `tame.session` minutes a sitting, then look again later like any finished work; mid-sitting only the herd and its nearest animal are checked, the room and month at a sitting's start and before `takeIn`; the total `sat` for the herd `herdId` reaching `tame.youngMinutes` in a `tame.young` month or `tame.adultMinutes` calls `takeIn`; another herd starts again). Fields `task` (`fold` `unfold` `butcher` `shear` `hay` `mow` `tame`), `plan` (the flock), `target` (gate tile, animal, tile or wild herd), `hay`, `herdId`, `sat`. When a flock's `takenLine` changes every herder (Herding above the lowest level) and each tamer of the flock gets `memories.taken` (system state `taken`). The flock API writes the lines; no `work.tookUp` line is written for herding.
- **Metal and tools (Bundle 3):** `station.ts`, `charcoal.ts`, `smelt.ts`, `smith.ts`, `tools.ts`, `toolFactor`: `docs/TOOLS_AND_SMITHY.md`.
- **Hearths, tending and warmth (P3.6, V-044 to V-048):** `hearth.ts`: a free builder takes up the nearest planned hearth piece they can get stone for (`work.tookUpHearth`), made through carpentry's `furnishing` (plain), then fire `layHearth`. `tend.ts`, the Tend row: (a) a lit fire under `tend.low` minutes, (b) an unlit hearth in use or with someone cold near, (c) splitting logs into firewood while the Firewood plate is short, (d) topping a used fire under `tend.topBelow` up to `tend.top`; only where the unheated tile is under `warmth.comfort`; a fire unused `tend.idle` minutes burns down (`work.tookUpTending`, `fire.lit`); also in the evening. `warm.ts`: below the warmth line an awake person leaves work (it resumes), stands where a fire warms to comfort (`doing: warm`) until `low + again`, or lights one (`people.warming` once). Bedtime banks the fire in reach (`fire.banked`). `haul.ts` holds carrying.
- **Test marks (`?debug`):** before choosing a tree, a free person able to fell takes the nearest marked tree they can get beside (`goods` `markedTree`), with a `work.tookUpMarked` line citing the mark's order.
- **Homes (P5.14):** `claim.ts`, `home.ts` move a household into its own home: see `docs/STRANGERS_AND_HOMES.md`.
- **Events:** every line is a template in content `events.json` (`people.*`, `work.*`, `skill.rose`, `food.cooked`, `goods.ateSeed`, `furniture.made`, `farm.harvestHome`, and the digging lines `mine.sank`, `mine.dug`, `mine.veinMined`, `mine.clayDug`, `mine.noLadder`); the causes each cites are given with its system above, and `work.tookUp*` lines also cite the player's priority when one is set. Trips are not lines.
- **State owned:** tables `person` (name, sex, race, culture, home, homeDays, homeDir, born year, seven attributes, trade, hair, hairColour, clothes, x, y, z, status, arrived; since P2.3 `path` (the nodes still to step onto, an `ints` field), `stride` and `rest`; since P2.2 `doing` and `retry`; since P2.4 `job`, `target`, `work`, `took` and `next`; since P3.1 `plan`; since P4.1 `task` (`sink`, `tunnel`, `mine`, `clay`) and `what` (metal or part); since P5.9 the `row.ts` newcomer fields), `bush` (x, y, picked, line; P2.9), `skill` (person, skill, level, potential, xp since P2.5), `trait` (person, trait) and `priority` (person, job, level, line, declined; P2.5). System state: the colony's day once changed, the day's tally (P2.9) and `idle` (carpentry's last empty search).
- **Invariants:** draws only from the `people` stream: in `init`, after the map (it stands people on open start tiles: not water, rock or a blocking feature; the band's `spots` first, then rings out to 12), and in `wander` each minute; a level never passes its potential; a person who dies stays a row with a new status.
- **Tests:** `strangers.test.ts` (slow tier, see the doc); `wild.test.ts` (slow tier: a hunter takes a deer and the herd keeps its last three; fishing and frozen water; a bold armed pair stands and a timid person runs for a house; an attack's line leads through the sighting to the pack's hunger; tracks; the midden), `smelt.test.ts`, `tools.test.ts` (Bundle 3); `cook.test.ts` (a week of cooked meals with `ate-well` memories raising the mood, the player's priority moving who cooks, no cooking without a lit fire); `forage.test.ts` (foraging within a day when the cart is low, berries stocked by day 35, no picking out of season or while regrowing, the tally matching the lines); `priority.test.ts` (a raised priority changes who takes up felling within an hour; declining once with a reason; skills by use; the colony's day); `src/engine/paths/paths.test.ts` and `npm run paths` cover the ways walked; `people.test.ts` (ten seeds give ten distinct bands, each able to build and cook; ages, potentials and trade; the band arrives on open ground in one ledger line). Farming has no test of its own: build `fields.test.ts` runs it end to end. `herd.test.ts` (slow tier: the herder folds before dusk and lets out after dawn; a flock left out with the reason; butchering with the tamer's memory; a lamb tamed in Bloommonth; mowing and hay to the rick).
