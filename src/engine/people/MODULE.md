# people

The band and the people of the valley: who they are (P2.1) and how they walk (P2.3) what they choose to do each minute (P2.2) and the work they take up themselves (P2.4).

- **Public API:** `index.ts`: `peopleSystem` (draws the band in `init`), `PEOPLE_TABLES`, `drawBand(rng, content, homes)` (pure: the band from a list of homes), `tradeOf(band, skills)`, types `Person`, `Home`.
- **Band:** `band.ts` draws `band.size` people (V-016) from the valley's neighbours (`valleyOf(...).valley.near`, nearer ones more often), or `band.homeCulture` for a plain seed. Each gets the culture's race, a given name from its list (or start + end syllables, redrawn when the seam reads badly), no family name (content has none; the UI says "Idris of Hyddog"), an age from maturity + `minAdult` to `ageShare`% of the lifespan, attributes from the race's means plus dice, two traits by weight that do not exclude each other, a hair style, hair and clothing colours, and for each `band.skills` entry a potential (from the skill's two `learnAttributes`) and a level (a share of it that grows with the share of adult life lived). One skill drawn towards high potentials is raised as the one they made a living by; `trade` is the best skill now (ties: higher potential, then list order). Whole bands are redrawn up to `band.tries` times until someone has every `band.must` skill at `band.able` or more, then the likeliest person is trained up to it. No two given names alike.
- **Walking (P2.3):** `walk.ts`: each minute (`every: minute`) a person on a way gains `walking.speed` stride and takes the next step once it covers the step's cost (`src/engine/paths`), so water and marsh slow them; a way blocked since it was found is found again from where they stand, or they stop. Someone with nothing to do stands `band.wander.rest` minutes, then strolls to an open tile within `band.wander.radius` of the camp, drawn from the `people` stream. Steps are never ledger lines.
- **Mind (P2.2):** `mind.ts`: after the minute's steps each person chooses, in order: eat when food is under its `seek` (walk to the nearest food stack lying on the ground, eat one there; if none can be reached, look again in an hour), sleep from `sleep.from` to `sleep.to` or when rest is under its `seek` (at their own laid-out bedroll, else where they stand) until it is morning and rest has reached `sleep.wake`, take their leisure from `relax.from` or when recreation is under its `seek` (strolling), else stroll. The choice is the `doing` field (`eat`, `sleep`, `relax`, ''), which the needs system reads; none of it is a ledger line.
- **Work (P2.4, V-015):** `jobs.ts`: no one is ordered. In `work.hours`, after eating and sleep, a free person works: someone carrying a load takes it to the store (`slotFor`, else `growStore`; onto a matching stack, else its own tile); someone able to fell (the `jobs.json` job `work.fell.job`: its attribute requirements, strength 8) takes it up while the colony's wood (`haveOf`, plus the trees others are felling) is under its stock level (`wantOf`): a fallen log first, then the standing tree nearest the store within `fell.reach`, never within `fell.camp` of the start, worked from one of its four sides for `minutes × 2 × skilled ÷ (skilled + woodcutting level)` minutes; the feature goes, the chunk's version rises and the logs lie in stacks on its tile and the reachable open tiles around. Anyone else carries the nearest load lying outside the store, up to `haul.load`. Finding nothing, they look again in 30 minutes.
- **Test marks (`?debug`):** before choosing a tree, a free person able to fell takes the nearest marked tree they can get beside (`goods` `markedTree`), with a `work.tookUpMarked` line citing the mark's order.
- **Events:** emits `people.arrived` (names, valley, homes), citing `region.settled` (the player's choice of valley) or `world.begun` for a plain seed; `work.tookUp` (name, good, have, want) when someone takes up felling, citing the stock level's line; `work.felled` (name, verb, what, count, good) per tree, citing their taking-up line. Trips are not lines.
- **State owned:** tables `person` (name, sex, race, culture, home, homeDays, homeDir, born year, seven attributes, trade, hair, hairColour, clothes, x, y, z, status, arrived; since P2.3 `path` (the nodes still to step onto, an `ints` field), `stride` and `rest`; since P2.2 `doing` and `retry`; since P2.4 `job`, `target`, `work`, `took` and `next`), `skill` (person, skill, level, potential) and `trait` (person, trait). No system state.
- **Invariants:** draws only from the `people` stream, in `init`, after the map (it stands people on open start tiles: not water, rock or a blocking feature; the band's `spots` first, then rings out to 12); a level never passes its potential; a person who dies stays a row with a new status.
- **Tests:** `src/engine/paths/paths.test.ts` and `npm run paths` cover the ways walked; `people.test.ts` (ten seeds give ten distinct bands, each able to build and cook; ages, potentials and trade; the band arrives on open ground in one ledger line).
