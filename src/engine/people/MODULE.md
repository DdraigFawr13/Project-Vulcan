# people

The band and the people of the valley: who they are (P2.1) and how they walk (P2.3) what they choose to do each minute (P2.2) and the work they take up themselves (P2.4).

- **Public API:** `index.ts`: `peopleSystem` (draws the band in `init`), `PEOPLE_TABLES`, `drawBand(rng, content, homes)` (pure: the band from a list of homes), `tradeOf(band, skills)`, `dayOf(world, content)` (the colony's day), `levelOf(world, content, person, job)` and `declinedLine(world, person, job)` (priorities), `progressOf(content, skillRow)` (practice towards the next level), types `Person`, `Home`, `Day`.
- **Band:** `band.ts` draws `band.size` people (V-016) from the valley's neighbours (`valleyOf(...).valley.near`, nearer ones more often), or `band.homeCulture` for a plain seed. Each gets the culture's race, a given name from its list (or start + end syllables, redrawn when the seam reads badly), no family name (content has none; the UI says "Idris of Hyddog"), an age from maturity + `minAdult` to `ageShare`% of the lifespan, attributes from the race's means plus dice, two traits by weight that do not exclude each other, a hair style, hair and clothing colours, and for each `band.skills` entry a potential (from the skill's two `learnAttributes`) and a level (a share of it that grows with the share of adult life lived). One skill drawn towards high potentials is raised as the one they made a living by; `trade` is the best skill now (ties: higher potential, then list order). Whole bands are redrawn up to `band.tries` times until someone has every `band.must` skill at `band.able` or more, then the likeliest person is trained up to it. No two given names alike.
- **Walking (P2.3):** `walk.ts`: each minute (`every: minute`) a person on a way gains `walking.speed` stride and takes the next step once it covers the step's cost (`src/engine/paths`), so water and marsh slow them; a way blocked since it was found is found again from where they stand, or they stop. Someone with nothing to do stands `band.wander.rest` minutes, then strolls to an open tile within `band.wander.radius` of the camp, drawn from the `people` stream. Steps are never ledger lines.
- **Mind (P2.2):** `mind.ts`: after the minute's steps each person chooses, in order: eat when food is under its `seek` (walk to the nearest food stack lying on the ground, eat one there; if none can be reached, look again in an hour), sleep from the colony's bedtime until work starts (`day.ts`) or when rest is under its `seek` (at their own laid-out bedroll, else where they stand) until it is morning and rest has reached `sleep.wake`, take their leisure from the end of work or when recreation is under its `seek` (strolling), else stroll. The choice is the `doing` field (`eat`, `sleep`, `relax`, ''), which the needs system reads; none of it is a ledger line.
- **Work (P2.4, V-015):** `jobs.ts`: no one is ordered. In the colony's work hours, after eating and sleep, a free person works: someone carrying a load takes it to the store (`slotFor`, else `growStore`; onto a matching stack, else its own tile); someone able to fell (the `jobs.json` job `work.fell.job`: its attribute requirements, strength 8) takes it up while the colony's wood (`haveOf`, plus the trees others are felling) is under its stock level (`wantOf`): a fallen log first, then the standing tree nearest the store within `fell.reach`, never within `fell.camp` of the start, worked from one of its four sides for `minutes × 2 × skilled ÷ (skilled + woodcutting level)` minutes; the feature goes, the chunk's version rises and the logs lie in stacks on its tile and the reachable open tiles around. Anyone else carries the nearest load lying outside the store, up to `haul.load`. Finding nothing, they look again in 30 minutes.
- **Priorities, the colony's day and learning (P2.5, V-015):** `priority.ts`: the player's `setPriority {person, job, level}` lever sets what matters more to someone, 1 first to 4 last, for each `work.priority.jobs` kind (a `priority` row; no row is the `start` level); a person looking for work tries the kinds in their own order (ties in content order), leaves felling to someone free and able who puts it higher, and when not able for work raised above the start level declines once per change with a `work.declined` line naming the attribute. Setting a priority makes them look again at once. `day.ts`: `setDay {workFrom, workTo, sleepFrom}` changes the colony's day (state `workFrom`, `workTo`, `sleepFrom`, `dayLine`; content `work.hours` and `life.sleep.from` until then): work hours, free time from `workTo`, bed at `sleepFrom`, up at `workFrom`. `learn.ts`: each minute of felling practises woodcutting (`xp` on the skill row); `work.learn.start + perLevel × level` minutes raise it by one, never past potential, with a `skill.rose` line citing the taking-up line.
- **Foraging and the day's tally (P2.9, V-015):** `forage.ts`: the same shape as felling, for food. While food (`haveOf` the `work.forage.good`, plus the berries of bushes others are walking to) is under its stock level, a free person takes up foraging (`work.forage.job`, the `foraging` skill; one of the Work sheet's kinds, so priorities, outranking and the order work the same): the nearest ripe bush (features of kind `bush` with `yields`) within `forage.reach` of the store that they can get beside, worked for `minutes × 2 × skilled ÷ (skilled + foraging level)` minutes (practising the skill); the bush stays on the map, its `amount` of berries lie on the open tiles around it for haulers (`haul.kinds` lists berries), and a `bush` row (x, y, picked tick, line) records the picking. A bush is ripe when the month is in `forage.months` and it was never picked or not for `regrowDays` days; out of season none is. `spots.ts` holds what felling and foraging share (standing beside a tile, laying a yield out, who is on what). `tally.ts`: system state counting per game day logs felled, items carried into the store, berries picked and food eaten (units); at midnight today moves to `yesterday` with that day's index and resets; `yesterdayOf(world, content)` gives it with the day of the month and month name. No ledger lines.
- **Quarrying and building (P3.1, V-041):** quarrying is felling's twin (`jobs.ts` `Gather`): while stone (`work.quarry.good`) is under its stock level, someone able for the `quarry` job breaks up the boulder nearest the store within `quarry.reach` for `quarry.minutes` (shorter with stoneworking) and its stone lies around for haulers (`work.felled` with the verb "broke up"). `build.ts`: a free person able for the `buildings.job` takes up the nearest free plan (`src/engine/build`), fetches its material, brings it, builds it from beside the tile, or takes a part down that was ordered down; the `plan` field holds the plan's id. Plans whose material nobody can reach share one `build.waiting` alert per material. Both are Work sheet kinds (`work.priority.jobs`), so priorities, outranking and declining work the same.
- **Carpentry and beds (P3.4, P3.5, V-041, V-061 to V-066):** `carpentry.ts`: a Work sheet kind after Building (`carpentry`, jobs.json, the crafting skill). A free person able for it takes up the bench plan, else a piece started and left, else the next want (`src/engine/build` `nextWant`; a search that found nothing is not repeated until `wantSignature` changes, system state `idle`), with wood they can reach. They fetch the logs (`build.ts` `stackOf`), bring them beside the bench (the bench's own plan, for the bench), work there `minutes × skilled ÷ (skilled + level)` (practising crafting), and the piece is set at the spot chosen when it was taken up (nobody carries it). Quality score = their crafting level + a roll of up to `roll` either way from the `craft` stream. A plan whose wood cannot be reached raises `build.waiting` once. `claim.ts`: at bedtime (before moving in) someone with no bed takes the free bed nearest their laid bedroll, else themselves, in their own house first; the bed's `owner` is them and the bedroll goes owner-less in their hands, so the next load takes it back to the store. They sleep in their bed (`mind.ts` `goToBed`).
- **Hearths, tending and warmth (P3.6, V-044 to V-048):** `hearth.ts`: a free person able to build takes up the nearest planned hearth piece they can get stone for (job word `hearth`, `work.tookUpHearth`), made where it stands through carpentry's `furnishing` (no quality roll: plain), then `fire` `layHearth`. `tend.ts`, the Tend row (after Haul): in order (a) a lit fire under `tend.low` minutes, (b) an unlit hearth in use or with someone cold near, (c) splitting logs in the store into firewood while the Firewood plate is short, (d) topping up a lit used fire under `tend.topBelow` to at most `tend.top`; fires are only fed where the unheated tile is under `warmth.comfort`, and a fire unused for `tend.idle` minutes is left to burn down. Taking up cites the priority and the plate or fire line; lighting writes `fire.lit`. Also taken up in the evening before bed. `warm.ts`: below the warmth warning line an awake person leaves work (the job stays and resumes), goes to the nearest reachable tile a fire warms to comfort and stays (`doing: warm`) until warmth is `low + again`; with none they light or feed a fire themselves if free (`people.warming` once). Going to bed at night banks the lit fire in reach (`fire.banked`). `haul.ts` holds carrying (split out of `jobs.ts`); firewood is hauled like logs.
- **Test marks (`?debug`):** before choosing a tree, a free person able to fell takes the nearest marked tree they can get beside (`goods` `markedTree`), with a `work.tookUpMarked` line citing the mark's order.
- **Events:** emits `people.arrived` (names, valley, homes), citing `region.settled` (the player's choice of valley) or `world.begun` for a plain seed; `work.tookUp` (name, work, good, have, want) when someone takes up felling or foraging, citing the stock level's line; `work.picked` (name, count, what, x, y) per bush, citing their taking-up line; `work.felled` (name, verb, what, count, good) per tree, citing their taking-up line; `work.prioritySet` and `work.daySet` citing the lever; `work.declined` citing the priority; `skill.rose` citing the work; since P3.4 `work.tookUpHearth`, `work.tookUpTending` and `people.warming` (P3.6), `work.tookUpCarpentry` (name, kind, room, why; cites the bench, the house built and the priority), `furniture.made` (name, what, room; cites taking it up and the bench) and `people.claimedBed` (name, room; cites the bed made). `work.tookUp` also cites the person's felling priority when the player set one. Trips are not lines.
- **State owned:** tables `person` (name, sex, race, culture, home, homeDays, homeDir, born year, seven attributes, trade, hair, hairColour, clothes, x, y, z, status, arrived; since P2.3 `path` (the nodes still to step onto, an `ints` field), `stride` and `rest`; since P2.2 `doing` and `retry`; since P2.4 `job`, `target`, `work`, `took` and `next`; since P3.1 `plan`), `bush` (x, y, picked, line; P2.9), `skill` (person, skill, level, potential, xp since P2.5), `trait` (person, trait) and `priority` (person, job, level, line, declined; P2.5). System state: the colony's day once changed, the day's tally (P2.9) and `idle` (carpentry's last empty search).
- **Invariants:** draws only from the `people` stream: in `init`, after the map (it stands people on open start tiles: not water, rock or a blocking feature; the band's `spots` first, then rings out to 12), and in `wander` each minute; a level never passes its potential; a person who dies stays a row with a new status.
- **Tests:** `forage.test.ts` (foraging within a day when the cart is low, berries stocked and nobody hungry by day 35, no picking in the cold months or inside the regrowing days, yesterday's tally matching the day's lines); `priority.test.ts` (a raised priority changes who takes up felling within an hour; declining once with a reason; skills by use; the colony's day); `src/engine/paths/paths.test.ts` and `npm run paths` cover the ways walked; `people.test.ts` (ten seeds give ten distinct bands, each able to build and cook; ages, potentials and trade; the band arrives on open ground in one ledger line).
