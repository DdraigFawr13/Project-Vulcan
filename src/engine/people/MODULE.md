# people

The people of the valley as they live each minute (P2.2, P2.4): the people system and its tables, the mind's choice of what to do, the job table that runs every kind of work, and daily life: drinking, warming, beds and homes, feasts (faith `keepHoly` and `praying`), winter evenings and defiance's acts. The work itself is in `herding`, `crafts`, `land` and `care`, the band's body and hands in `band`.

- **Public API:** `index.ts`: `peopleSystem` (draws the band in `init`), `strangersSystem` (P5.9, right after it in `SYSTEMS`, stream `strangers`), `PEOPLE_TABLES`, `byAFire`, `winterEvening`, and from `band`: `drawBand(rng, content, homes)`, `tradeOf(band, skills)`, `dayOf(world, content)`, `levelOf`, `declinedLine`, `progressOf`, `yesterdayOf`, `toolFactor`, `setLineHook`, `lineOf`, `todayLine`, types `Person`, `Home`, `Day`, `Tally`, `LineHook`, `LineNeed`.
- **Mind (P2.2):** `mind.ts`: after the minute's steps each person chooses, in order: eat when food is under its `seek` (walk to the nearest food stack lying on the ground, eat one there; if none can be reached, look again in an hour), sleep from the colony's bedtime until work starts (`day.ts`) or when rest is under its `seek` (at their own laid-out bedroll, else where they stand) until it is morning and rest has reached `sleep.wake`, take their leisure from the end of work or when recreation is under its `seek` (strolling; under it a free person takes up no new work, `jobs.ts`), else stroll; work hours and bedtime are the person's own from law `hoursOf` (P4.8: the law's, or the customary ones for a refuser). The choice is the `doing` field (`eat`, `sleep`, `relax`, ''), which the needs system reads; none of it is a ledger line. At leisure after work, the tavern first once it stands (inn `atTheInn`, 6B); a hangover loses a minute in a few at work (inn `hungOver`).
- **Afield (6W Slice 5, V-206):** `afield.ts`: someone packed for a trip eats from the pack, drinks where they are, warms by a camp fire, camps at night and goes on with the trip at any hour; home with the job done, the pack is put down (docs/TRIPS.md).
- **Drinking (P5.2):** `drink.ts`: under water's `seek`, drink to full beside the nearest of `waterSpots` (`doing: drink`; `noteDrink`). In warm weather a holder of `sick.lakeBelief` passes the lake by for a well or running water (`people.passedLake`, once a year; V-208).
- **Work (P2.4, V-015, refactor proposal 7):** no one is ordered. `jobs.ts` runs the job table (`jobtable.ts`: per kind its `takeUp`, minute of `work`, `atTarget`, `holds`; fields `jobfields.ts`). In the work hours, after eating and sleep, a free person goes on with the work in hand, else delivers a load (unless the work `holds` it), else by daylight works at its chosen target, else takes up the Work sheet's kinds in their priority order (`band` `priority.ts`), a cold room's hearth first; after dusk only work the dark or a light allows (`dusk.ts`).
- **Warmth and beds:** `warm.ts`: below the warmth line an awake person leaves work (it resumes), stands where a fire warms to comfort (`doing: warm`) until `low + again`, or lights one (`people.warming` once). `claim.ts`: at bedtime someone with no bed takes the free bed nearest their bedroll, in their own house first (`people.claimedBed`); the bedroll goes back to the store.
- **Winter evenings (P6.3, V-162):** `evening.ts` `gathering`, from the mind's free time: on a winter evening (`winterEvening`: winter and dark) someone at leisure on the surface walks to the nearest tile a lit fire warms (warm.ts `nearestWarm`, a look every 15 minutes) and stays, relaxing; each hour there their worn garments gain tales.json `evening.mend` hours of wear, up to new. `byAFire` is what engine tales reads for fireside tellings. No lines. `practising` (P6.4): one free minute in self.json `ambition.every` goes to the skill of an unfinished ambition (`learn.ts` `practise`).
- **Homes (P5.14):** `claim.ts`, `home.ts` move a household into its own home: see `docs/STRANGERS_AND_HOMES.md`.
- **Events:** every line is a template in content `events.json` (`people.*`, `work.*`, `skill.rose`); the causes each cites are given with its system, and `work.tookUp*` lines also cite the player's priority when one is set. Trips are not lines.
- **State owned:** `PEOPLE_TABLES`: table `person` (name, sex, race, culture, home, homeDays, homeDir, born year, seven attributes, trade, hair, hairColour, clothes, x, y, z, status, arrived, plus the fields of `band` `row.ts` and `walk.ts`, `mind.ts` (`doing`, `retry`), `drink.ts` and `jobfields.ts` (`job`, `target`, `work`, `took`, `next`, `plan`, `task`, `what`)), `trait` (person, trait), and `band`'s `skill`, `priority` and tool tables and `land`'s `bush`. System state: the colony's day once changed, the day's tally and `idle` (carpentry's last empty search).
- **Invariants:** draws only from the `people` stream: in `init`, after the map (it stands people on open start tiles: not water, rock or a blocking feature; the band's `spots` first, then rings out to 12), and in `wander` each minute; a person who dies stays a row with a new status. Nothing below `people` imports it: `band` < `herding` < `crafts` < `land`, `care` < `people`.
- **Tests:** `jobs.test.ts`; `evening.test.ts`; `lake.test.ts` (the lake tale, and a believer drinking at the well in warm weather). The other people-side tests sit with their files in `band`, `herding`, `crafts`, `land` and `care`.
