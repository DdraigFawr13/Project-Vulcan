# flock

The tame flock (P5.4b, Phase 5 Bundle 3) and its hay (proposal P3): kept sheep and goats in the pasture the colony sites (build `pasture.ts`), their year (lambing, growing, shearing, the autumn cull), the hay meadow's mowing, drying and rick, folding at dusk and the pack taking from a flock left out. Control stays indirect (V-041): no lever names an animal or a tile; herders (people `herd.ts`) choose the work.

- **Public API:** `index.ts`: `flockSystem` (after `wildSystem` in `SYSTEMS`, stream `flock`), `FLOCK_TABLES`, `flocksOf(world)`, `flockOf(world, pastureId)`, `keptOf(world, flock)` (its animal rows), `headOf(world, flock)`, `kindDef(content, species)`, `animalWord(content, animal)` (`ewe`, `lamb`...; line name), `canTame(world, content, flock)` (room left and a taming month), `tameTargets(world, content, flock)` (wild herds of `tame.species` with more than `wild.spare` animals within `tame.reach`, nearest first), `takeIn(ctx, flock, wildAnimal, byPerson, cause)` (wild `tameAnimal`, then `flock.tamed` / `flock.full`; returns the line), `foldFlock(ctx, flock, byPerson)`, `openFlock(ctx, flock, byPerson)`, `mowable(world, content, flock)` (meadow tiles not mown this year, today a mowing day), `mowTile(ctx, flock, tile, byPerson)`, `readySwaths(world, content, flock)` (tiles whose cut hay has dried), `takeHay(world, flock, tile, most)` (returns hay taken), `addToRick(ctx, flock, hay)`, `shearable(world, content, flock)` (grown fleece animals not shorn this year, in the shearing month), `shear(ctx, flock, animal, byPerson)` (lays `shear.item` on the animal's tile), `marked(world, flock)` (animals marked to cull, oldest mark first), `butcher(ctx, flock, animal, byPerson)` (wild `killAnimal` with item `cull.item`; `flock.butchered`; returns the line), `noteLeftOut(ctx, flock, why, byPerson)` (writes `flock.leftOut`, sets `outLine`).
- **Table `flock`** (`flock.ts`), one row per built pasture, made by the system the hour after its pasture is built: `pasture` (entity), `hay` (in the rick), `swaths` / `swathHay` / `swathDry` (`ints`, parallel: a cut tile, its hay left, its dry days), `mown` (`ints`, meadow tiles mown this year), `mowDay` (the day number on which mowing is allowed: set at 06:00 when the night was dry, in a hay month), `spoiled` (hay lost this year), `folded` (1 while shut in), `outLine` (tonight's `flock.leftOut`, 0 at dawn), `takenAt` (tick of the pack's last taking), `takenLine`, `onHay` (1 from the first lying snow on the pasture to the first of `hay.thaw`), `cullLine` (the latest `flock.cullSet`/`flock.cullEarly`), `full` (1 once `flock.full` is said, until head drops below `holds`), `line` (the flock's latest line), `year` (the year the yearly counters belong to).
- **Animals** are wild `animal` rows in a kept wild `herd` row (`kept` = the pasture's id; one per species per pasture, made on the first taming of that species). Fields added for kept animals: `sex` (`f` / `m`), `born` (tick, 0 for a tamed grown animal), `young` (1 until the first of `lambing.grown`), `tamedBy` (person), `cull` (tick marked to cull, 0 for none), `shorn` (year last shorn + 1), `dies` (tick a weak young dies, 0 for none). `kind` is the art kind from `flock.kinds` (`ewe`, `ram`, `lamb`...). The wild system skips kept herds and their animals everywhere (movement, flight, snow loss, breeding, strays, `herdLow`, the pack's in-valley eating, `huntable`).
- **Movement** (`move.every` minutes): a kept animal with a path steps along it on the people's terms (`stepCost`: it wades a ford the way crosses, passes a gate, never a hurdle); else, every `move.wander` minutes, it steps to a random open tile inside its place: the fold while `folded`, else the pasture's grazing tiles (inside the hurdles, outside the fold). An animal outside its place (just tamed, or let out) is given a way there (`findPath`).
- **Daily (midnight):** wet day (`world.systems.weather.wet`): each swath loses `ceil(swathHay × spoil)`, one `flock.haySpoiled` for the day's loss; dry day: swaths gain a dry day. On hay: `hay -= head`; with too little hay to reach the first of `hay.thaw` the youngest marked first, extra animals are marked (`flock.cullEarly`); with none, one animal (marked first) starves (`flock.starved`, its carcass laid by `killAnimal` with no person). First lying snow on most of the pasture's grazing tiles: `onHay` (`flock.onHay`); first of `hay.thaw`: grass (`flock.onGrass`). First of `lambing.month`: each grown female with a grown male in the pasture lambs on a day of the month drawn now with chance `lambing.chance` (one young, sex even odds; it dies within `diesWithin` days with chance `dies`); one `flock.lambed` a day with births, `flock.youngDied` for each death. First of `lambing.grown`: young grow (`flock.grown`). First of the month after the last hay month: `flock.rick`. First of `cull.month`: keep `min(head, floor(hay / hay.plan))`: one grown male per `ramsPer` grown females (at least one while any female is kept), then females youngest first, then males; the rest are marked (`flock.cullSet`, citing the rick's or spoil's line).
- **Dusk and the pack:** the people side folds before dusk (`night.from` of `wild.pack` less `fold.before` minutes) and lets out at `night.to` plus `fold.open`; at `night.from` an unfolded flock with animals and no `outLine` gets `noteLeftOut` with `why.none`. While `outLine` is set and not folded, the flock lures the pack (wild `setLures(ctx, 'flock', [{tile, where: 'flock', rank: 0}])`; cleared when folded or at dawn). When the pack has arrived at that lure (wild `packArrival`), at most once a night: the animal nearest is removed (no goods; one tuft is not a good), `flock.taken` cites the pack's hunger line and `outLine`, wild `feedPack(ctx, line)`, and `takenLine` is set (the people side gives herders `memories.taken`).
- **Events:** `flock.*` in `events.json`; `pasture.*` are build's.
- **Invariants:** whole-number maths and the `flock` stream only; a kept animal is inside its pasture's rectangle unless it has a path; head never counts the dead.
- **Tests:** `flock.test.ts`.

## As built (differences from the contract above)

- `flock` has two more fields: `mowLog` (the day number `flock.mowing` was last written, so one line a day) and `births` (`ints`, four per drawn lamb: day, species index, sex, days the weak one lives).
- `lambed` and `grown` are written once per species a day, not once a day; `grown` plurals add an `s`.
- Lures are set from the folding time (`night.from` less `fold.before`) while the flock is unfolded with animals, not only once `outLine` is set: the wild system sends the pack on the tick of `night.from`, before this system runs that tick. Taking still needs `outLine`.
- `packArrival(world, content)` takes content and returns the `where` words; `killAnimal` on a kept animal writes no `wild.killed` (see wild MODULE).
- Dry night: the weather's wet flag covers a whole day, drawn at midnight. This system keeps yesterday's and today's (`wetPrev`, `wetNow`); the night before 06:00 is dry when both are dry.
- `onHay` can begin only in a month after the last hay month; hay is eaten by every animal, marked or not, until it is butchered. `flock.tamed`'s `{species}` is the kind's species id (`sheep`).
- `syncLures` keeps system state `lured` (lures set last minute) and skips the wild call while there are none, then or now. `keptOf` answers `[]` without scanning the animals while a flock has no kept herd.
- Males are kept across species by sex alone at the cull; kept animals leave no prints and `foldFlock` / `openFlock` write no line.
