# calling

Callings (6B Bundle 6, P6.13, V-175, V-189 q8 and q9): rare roles with a high bar, at most one holder each, held for life, each pushing the colony or one person towards greatness.

- **Public API:** `index.ts`: `callingSystem` (in `SYSTEMS` after teach, hourly, no RNG), `CALLING_TABLES`, `callingsOf(world)`, `callingOf(world, person)` (the one held now), `barsOf(world, content, person)` (how they stand against each first bar: tests and `npm run callings`).
- **Content:** `callings.json` (schema `schemas-callings.ts`): `every`, `hour`; `callings` entries with `name`, `titles` by trade skill, `stages` (`bar` tests, `sign` for the person sheet), `best` (the test that ranks) and `push`. Events `calling.taken`, `calling.masterwork`; tale `masterwork`, belief `valley-masters`; annals lines for both.
- **The frame (V-189 q8):** a calling is stages, each a bar of tests and a sign. Only the first stage is read now; 6E's dark paths add later stages, turning points and endings to the same entries. Tests (`bar.ts`): `trait`, `trusted` (colony `trustOf`, among the `top` grown), `friends` (told or heard a tale lately, colony `talkersOf`, with `share` per cent of the other living), `tales` (held), `lines` (ledger lines naming them at an actor place, tallied hourly in state by `tally.ts`), `attribute`, `ambition` (fulfilled), `skill`, `memory` (held), `role` (held), `either` (one of `or`). Each has `word` for the line.
- **Taking (`take.ts`, every `every` days at `hour`):** holders who died or left give theirs up (`ended`, no line). Each calling with no living holder goes to the grown-up holding no other calling who passes every test and ranks highest on `best` (ties to the lower id): `calling.taken` ("Aldith has become the master carpenter: crafting 12 and taught an apprentice to the end."), citing the memory, role, ambition or tallied lines behind it that the ledger still holds, else their arrival. A calling may never come on a seed.
- **Pushes (read where they bite, through engine entity `callings.ts`, so nothing imports this module):** `cool` (defy `coolOf`: a friend's act step cools in that many days, the peacemaker); `told` and `keep` (tales: a heard telling by them feeds that many belief steps, and their hearers keep it that per cent as long, the lorekeeper); `apprentice` (band `learn.ts`: their own apprentice's pace, the master); `masterwork` (`masterwork.ts`: the next line of one of its `events` in their calling's skill with them first among its actors is their masterwork: the piece or tool it made takes `quality`, a name from `names`, a `calling.masterwork` line the tales copy); `watch` (herding `watcherFor`: they sit the watch fire first, the champion); `fade` (tales: others' `belief` fades in `by` per cent of the days).
- **State owned:** table `calling` (person, calling, stage, skill, title, line, since, ended, work, named); state `seen` (last ledger id read) and the tallies `<event>@<actor>#<person>` with `…!` the last line.
- **Invariants:** no RNG; whole numbers; one calling a person; reads trust, traits, bonds, tales, attributes, ambitions, skills, memories, roles and the ledger; writes its table, its state and (a masterwork) a furniture piece's or tool's quality. Must not import `people`.
- **Tests:** `calling.test.ts` (none unearned; each of the four taken at its bar with its line, and its push: a friend cools in 4 days, a lorekeeper's telling feeds 2 and is kept past forgetDays, a master teaches their apprentice at 400 and names a masterwork, the champion's valley forgets its fear of the woods in half the days).
