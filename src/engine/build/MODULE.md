# build

Plans and built parts (P3.1, P3.2): walls, floors, doors and stairs, a tile each; houses the colony sites itself, rooms and growth (P3.7, P3.3, P3.8).

- **The midden (P5.4a, V-072):** `midden.ts`, table `midden` (x, y, line). Hourly, once an item of a `work.haul.midden` kind (offal) lies about, the colony sites a one-tile midden `work.midden.past` tiles past the town ring, the nearest such tile to the green on flat dry ground (walking at most `walk`) that is not within `apart` tiles of a house, a field or water, reachable from the green (`build.middenSited`, citing the offal's making line). Sited once, never sent back. `middensOf(world)` returns `[{x, y, z, line}]` for the screens and haulers; `atMidden(world, x, y)`. The pack is told of it by the people system (`setLures`).
- **Public API:** `index.ts` (furniture: `piecesOf`, `furnitureOf`, `bedOwnedBy`, `benchOf`, `qualityOf`, `nextWant`, `roomName`, `wantSignature`, `siteFor`): `buildSystem`, `BUILD_TABLES`, `plansOf(world)`, `planAt(world, x, y, z = 0)`, `partOf(content, id)`, `planPart(content, plan)`, `stillNeeded(content, plan)`, `nextNeed(content, plan)`, `refundOf(content, plan)` (what taking it down gives back, per item), `finishPlan(ctx, plan, cause)`, `takeDownPlan(ctx, plan, cause)`.
- **Parts:** content `buildings.json`: the build `job` (jobs.json, building skill), the skill dial `skilled`, `downShare` (per cent of a part's minutes to take it down) and one entry per part: kind (`wall` blocks the way, `door` and `floor` do not, `stair` cuts a way down), material (an item), cost, optional `also {material, cost}` (brought after the first; built only when both are in), minutes and art. `wall-daub`: clay then logs; art is the wood wall for now. A built part is `map.built` (1 + its index); a stair is a tile kind instead, so stairs keep working as before.
- **Placing (test mode, V-041, V-049):** `placeBuilding {part, x, y}` plans a part on a free buildable surface tile, else `command.refused` with the reason; a plan of a good kept at none raises its stock level (goods `raiseStock`). `deconstruct {x, y}` drops an untouched plan (`build.dropped`), else orders it down (`build.downOrdered`; state `down`); a cut stair stays.
- **Work:** done by people (`src/engine/people/build.ts`), unordered: a builder takes the nearest free plan, fetches its material (the store before ground), and builds from beside the tile once nobody stands on it, moving anything lying on a wall or door tile aside. A wall that would shut someone off from a store tile is left (`build.shutIn` once, retried hourly); plans with unreachable material share one `build.waiting` a material.
- **Houses (P3.7, V-049 to V-054, V-097):** content `buildings.house` (sizes, the added-room `stage`, wall materials, `near`, `reach`). Hourly, while anyone sleeps outside a built house and none is planned, `houses.ts` `planHouse` sites one (`build.needed`, then `build.sited`): `site.ts` `chooseHouse` takes the largest size sleeping no more than the homeless that some material can finish (stock plus trees, logs and boulders within `reach` of the store), walled in the MOST PLENTIFUL such material (clay: known level 1 tiles within `reach` a miner could dig (`digsFor`), each its `digs.count`); then the best free rectangle within `near` of the green. No ground: `build.noGround`, a look again daily.
- **Growth (P3.8):** `grow.ts` `growHouses` runs first: a built house with more sleepers than beds adds a 5 by 4 room on its flattest side (`build.grows`), walled in the house's own wall material (row `wall`; its plans wait if it cannot be had); only with no ground for a room is another house sited. Goods lying on a footprint do not block it (`clearing.ts`): one `build.goodsAside` line; haulers carry them off whatever the Stock level (to the store, else beside the plans), and the part waits until its tile is clear. `finishHouses` writes `build.houseBuilt` / `build.roomAdded`.
- **Homes and the hall (P5.14):** `homes.ts`, `beds.ts` (with `houseAt`, `mainOf`, `rowsOf`, `bedSpot`): see `docs/STRANGERS_AND_HOMES.md`.
- **Rooms (P3.3):** `rooms.ts` `roomAt(world, content, x, y)`, worked out on demand, tiles flood-filled inside walls and doors (one door at least); `beds` (beds and bedrolls), `sleepers`, role (`rooms.roles`), beauty (floors, crowding, pieces' quality), look word, crowding, doors and impressiveness.
- **Moving in:** people `home.ts`: at bedtime a homeless person, or one in a crowded room that others leaving would not uncrowd, carries their bedroll to a free floor (`bedSpot`, which counts the tiles others are carrying theirs to, person field `bedTo`); `people.movedIn` cites the house built.
- **Furniture (P3.4, P3.5, V-061 to V-067):** `furniture.ts` table `furniture` (kind, x, y, z, facing, quality, maker, owner, state `plan` / `built`...). Content `buildings.furniture` (job `carpentry`, material, `stream` `craft`, `roll`, `kinds`, `quality` words) and `rooms.wants`. A piece blocks no way and leaves `map.built` alone. `wants.ts`: `nextWant` (a bed first, else what the busiest room lacks), `siteFor` (the `place` hint), `roomName`, `planBench` (hourly; `furniture.benchPlaced`). Made in people/carpentry.ts.
- **Hearths (P3.6, V-041, V-049):** `hearths.ts`: `planCampfire` plans a hearth piece (kind `hearth`, built by builders in stone, `centre` hint) by the green if none stands within 6 tiles (`fire.planned`); `planRoomHearths` plans one in a used house room whose unheated temperature is under `rooms.hearthBelow` (`fire.plannedCold`, citing the cold person or the spell). beds come first in a room.
- **Town ring and fields (P4.7, V-049 to V-053, V-089):** `ring.ts`: content `buildings.town` (`ring`, `step`, `most`). On `build.noGround` the ring widens a step (`town.ringWidened`); fields it now covers are left to grass at once if nothing grows on them, else after their next harvest (`field.leftToGrass`). `fields.ts` table `field`. Hourly, while field tiles are short of `field.perMouth` a person and none is planned, `planFields` sites one (`field.needed`, `field.sited` + reason; `fieldsite.ts`: one compact block, a rectangle as near square as fits, of good soil, the nearest to the ring's edge, `weigh.near` over fertility). `cropNow` picks the crop: its own, else the rotation's (`fieldsoil.ts`; none under `soil.rest`); `soillife.ts` draws soil at harvest, adds winter gain and muck (V-130). `fieldlife.ts` `tickFields`: a frost under the crop's `frost` loses it (`field.frostKilled`, citing the spell), else `field.ripe` after `days`; `field.noSeed` once a season; people/farm.ts ends in `finishSowing` / `finishReaping`. `blight.ts`: blight (stream `blight`), `tileYield`, dry-spell loss.
- **Stores by kind (5B Bundle 1, V-129):** `stores.ts`, content `buildings.stores`. Daily `planStores` (homes first): the first kind whose store, within `near` of the green (`eatenNear` for food), saves `save` tiles of walking a day, or fills a full home store, gets a `depot` row, `build.storeSited` and edge plans (`house` = depot id). `finishStores`: `build.storeBuilt`. No send-back.
- **The well (P5.2, V-131):** `well.ts`, table `drink` (day, tile, drinks, minutes; `noteDrink`). Daily `planWell`: with no well, drinking walks over `well.walk` minutes a person a day (mean of `well.days`) or the most-drunk water frozen plan one `well` piece by the first hearth (`build.wellNeeded`); builders make it (`build.wellBuilt`). No Send back. `wellsOf`, `waterSpots(world, content)`.
- **Levers (Build sheet):** `setBuild {kind, level}` (1 to 4; `build.wantSet`); `setBuild {kind: 'field', level}` is the Fields row; `sendBack {house}` drops a plan nothing is built on yet, once: material brought is laid back, the site is never chosen again (state `sent`) and the colony chooses again at once (`build.sentBack`); `sendBack {field}` likewise for a field with nothing sown (`field.sentBack`).
- **Pasture (P5.4b, Phase 5 Bundle 3, V-041):** `pasture.ts`, `pasturesite.ts`, `pasturerows.ts`. Content `flock.pasture`. API: `PASTURE_KIND`, `planPasture`, `wildHerds` (raw `herd` / `animal` rows of `flock.tame.species`, `!kept`), and from `pasturerows.ts`: `pasturesOf`, `livePastures`, `hurdleTiles`, `foldRect`, `foldTiles`, `grazingTiles`, `meadowTiles`, `onPastureGround`. Hourly, while no pasture is live, a tameable wild herd grazes and nobody sleeps in the open: `pasture.needed` once, then `choosePasture` sites one (`pasture.sited`): a `w` by `h` rectangle of open surface ground outside the ring (bushes cleared, trees marked to fell, row `mark`), centre within `reach` of the green, water within `water`, the `meadow` (along a side the fold touches, away from the green first) and the rick's tile clear, touching nothing built, planned or claimed. Lowest `weigh` score wins (ring gap, water, herd, trees; tile order; best `weigh.tries` tried). The fold is the inside corner nearest the green; the outer gate faces the green, the fold gate is in the fold's inner wall. One `plan` row a hurdle (`house` = the pasture; `hurdle`, `gate-hurdle`), built unordered. All standing: `pasture.built`. No ground: `pasture.noGround` once, a look daily. `sendBack {pasture}` (once, before a hurdle stands; logs back, trees unmarked, state `sent`, the colony chooses at once). `setBuild {kind: 'pasture', level}` is the Pasture row. `groundOf` (site.ts) counts live pasture ground as taken; burial and smelter checks use `onPastureGround`.
- **Roads (P5.10, V-054, V-124):** `roads.ts`, table `road` (state `plan` / `built` / `next`). Daily `planRoads`: between each pair of `placesOf` (green, homes, fields, pasture, smithy, burial ground), the way's most worn run of `roadable` tiles (`buildings.roads`); while another plan waits or spare stone is short it is kept as the one `next` row, else `build.roadPlanned` and a `plan` row a tile. `finishRoads`: `build.roadLaid`. No Send back. Builders rank road plans by the Roads row against the Houses row (people/build.ts). API: `roadsOf`, `roadAt`, `nextRoad`, `placesOf`, `ROAD_KIND`.
- **Metal works (Bundle 3):** `station.ts`, `metalsite.ts` (smelter, clamp), `smithy.ts`: `docs/TOOLS_AND_SMITHY.md`.
- **Checks:** each hour, while a plan waits and no living person meets the build job's requirements, one `build.nobody` alert (system state `nobody`, cleared when that changes).
- **Events:** templates in content `events.json`: `build.*`, `town.ringWidened`, `field.*`, `pasture.*`, `farm.harvestHome`, `fire.planned`, `fire.plannedCold`, `furniture.benchPlaced`.
- **State owned:** table `plan` (part, x, y, z, have, also, work, state `plan` / `built` / `down`, line, done, waited, shut, held, house); tables `house` (with `wall`, its wall part) and `want` (`houses.ts` `HOUSE_TABLES`), `field` (`fields.ts` `FIELD_TABLES`), `pasture` (`pasturerows.ts` `PASTURE_TABLES`), `road` (`roads.ts` `ROAD_TABLES`), `furniture` (`furniture.ts` `FURNITURE_TABLES`); system state `nobody`, `green`, `need`, `noGround`, `tried`, `ring`, `ringLine`, `fieldNeed`, `fieldNoGround`, `fieldTried`, `pastureNeed`, `pastureNoGround`, `pastureTried`, `roadTried`. `map.built` is written only through map `setBuilt`.
- **Invariants:** one plan row a tile; a row in state `built` matches `map.built` (or a stair tile).
- **Tests:** `build.test.ts`; `furniture.test.ts`; `walls.test.ts` (wall material, with the reason); `houses.test.ts` (an unordered cottage, then a room; goods carried aside; one send-back); `fields.test.ts` (a field, sowing to harvest, frost, ring, send-back); `stores.test.ts` (a store sited from need, built and filled, unordered); `pasture.test.ts` (siting, hurdles and gates, built and walkable to every grazing and fold tile, send-back); `roads.test.ts` (wear stages and fading; a road planned on the worn way from the green, named, laid, and cheaper to walk).
