# build

Plans and built parts (P3.1, P3.2): walls, floors, doors and stairs, a tile each. Houses the colony sites itself, rooms and growth (P3.7, P3.3, P3.8).

- **Public API:** `index.ts` (furniture: `piecesOf`, `furnitureOf`, `bedOwnedBy`, `benchOf`, `qualityOf`, `nextWant`, `roomName`, `wantSignature`, `siteFor`): `buildSystem`, `BUILD_TABLES`, `plansOf(world)`, `planAt(world, x, y, z = 0)`, `partOf(content, id)`, `planPart(content, plan)`, `stillNeeded(content, plan)` (material still to bring, both materials; 0 once all is in or down), `nextNeed(content, plan)` (material to bring next: the part's own, then `also`), `refundOf(content, plan)` (what taking it down gives back, per item), `finishPlan(ctx, plan, cause)` (sets the part on the map: `setBuilt`, or `cutStairsAt` for a stair, which reaches the level below), `takeDownPlan(ctx, plan, cause)` (clears it from the map, removes the row and returns the material to lay back, as `refundOf`).
- **Parts:** content `buildings.json`: the build `job` (jobs.json, building skill), the skill dial `skilled`, `downShare` (per cent of a part's minutes to take it down) and one entry per part: kind (`wall` blocks the way, `door` and `floor` do not, `stair` cuts a way down), material (an item), cost, optional `also {material, cost}` (brought after the first; built only when both are in), minutes and art. `wall-daub`: clay then logs; its art shows as the wood wall for now. A built part is `map.built` (1 + its index); a stair is a tile kind instead, so stairs keep working as before.
- **Placing (test mode only, V-041, V-049):** `placeBuilding {part, x, y}` plans a part on a free buildable surface tile, else `command.refused` with the reason; a plan of a good kept at none raises its stock level (goods `raiseStock`). `deconstruct {x, y}` drops an untouched plan (`build.dropped`), else orders it down (`build.downOrdered`; state `down`); a cut stair stays.
- **Work:** done by people (`src/engine/people/build.ts`), unordered: a builder takes the nearest free plan, fetches its material (the store before ground), and builds from beside the tile once nobody stands on it, moving anything lying on a wall or door tile aside. A wall that would shut someone off from a store tile is left (`build.shutIn` once, retried hourly); plans whose material nobody can reach share one `build.waiting` per material.
- **Houses (P3.7, V-049 to V-054, V-097):** content `buildings.house` (sizes, the added-room `stage`, wall materials, `near`, `reach`). Hourly, while anyone sleeps outside a built house and none is planned, `houses.ts` `planHouse` sites one (`build.needed`, then `build.sited`): `site.ts` `chooseHouse` takes the largest size sleeping no more than the homeless that some material can finish (stock plus trees, logs and boulders within `reach` of the store), walled in the MOST PLENTIFUL such material (clay: known level 1 tiles within `reach` a miner could dig (`digsFor`), each its `digs.count`), with the reason in `why`; then the best free rectangle within `near` of the green. No ground: `build.noGround`, a look again daily.
- **Growth (P3.8):** `grow.ts` `growHouses` runs first: a built house with more sleepers than beds adds a 5 by 4 room on its flattest side (`build.grows`), walled in the house's own wall material (row `wall`; its plans wait if it cannot be had); only with no ground for a room is another house sited. `finishHouses` writes `build.houseBuilt` / `build.roomAdded`.
- **Rooms (P3.3):** `rooms.ts` `roomAt(world, content, x, y)`, worked out when asked: tiles flood-filled inside walls and doors (one door at least); `beds` (beds and bedrolls), `sleepers`, role (`rooms.roles`), beauty (floors, crowding, pieces' quality), look word, crowding, doors and impressiveness.
- **Moving in:** people `home.ts`: at bedtime a homeless person, or one in a crowded room that others leaving would not uncrowd, carries their bedroll to a free floor (`bedSpot`, which counts the tiles others are carrying theirs to, person field `bedTo`); `people.movedIn` cites the house built.
- **Furniture (P3.4, P3.5, V-061 to V-067):** `furniture.ts` table `furniture` (kind, x, y, z, facing, quality score, maker, owner, made, line, state `plan` / `built`, have, work, waited). Content `buildings.furniture` (job `carpentry`, material, `stream` `craft`, `roll`, `kinds`, `quality` words) and `rooms.wants`. A piece blocks no way and leaves `map.built` alone. `wants.ts`: `nextWant` (a bed where most sleepers lack one, else what the busiest room lacks), `siteFor` (the `place` hint; never just inside a door or on a piece or item; other pieces leave room for missing beds), `roomName`, `planBench` (hourly: the first time a built room lacks furniture, `furniture.benchPlaced`). Making it is people/carpentry.ts.
- **Hearths (P3.6, V-041, V-049):** `hearths.ts`, hourly: `planCampfire` plans a hearth piece (kind `hearth`, built by builders in stone, `centre` hint) by the green if none stands within 6 tiles (`fire.planned`); `planRoomHearths` plans one in a used house room whose unheated temperature is under `rooms.hearthBelow` (`fire.plannedCold`, citing the cold person or the spell). beds come first in a room.
- **Town ring and fields (P4.7, V-049 to V-053, V-089):** `ring.ts`: content `buildings.town` (`ring`, `step`, `most`); house `near` is the larger of the ring and `house.near`. On `build.noGround` the ring widens a step (`town.ringWidened`) and the colony looks again; fields it now covers are left to grass at once if nothing grows on them, else after their next harvest (`field.leftToGrass`). `fields.ts` table `field` (state `plan` / `sown` / `ripe` / `fallow` / `sent`). Each hour, while field tiles are short of `field.perMouth` a person and none is planned, `planFields` sites one (`field.needed`, `field.sited` with a reason; `fieldsite.ts`: open good soil outside the ring near water, grown over connected tiles; trees marked like a house's). `cropNow` picks the crop sown in this month, so an off-season field waits. `fieldlife.ts` `tickFields`: a frost under the crop's `frost` loses it (`field.frostKilled`, citing the spell), else `field.ripe` after `days`; `field.noSeed` once a season; people/farm.ts ends in `finishSowing` / `finishReaping`.
- **Levers (Build sheet):** `setBuild {kind, level}` (1 first to 4 last; `build.wantSet`); `setBuild {kind: 'field', level}` is the Fields row (start level `field.start`); `sendBack {house}` drops a plan nothing is built on yet, once (a replacement cannot be sent back): material brought is laid back, the site is never chosen again (state `sent`) and the colony chooses again at once (`build.sentBack`); `sendBack {field}` does the same for a planned field with nothing sown (`field.sentBack`).
- **Checks:** each hour, while a plan waits and no living person meets the build job's requirements, one `build.nobody` alert (system state `nobody`, cleared when that changes).
- **Events:** templates in content `events.json`: `build.*`, `town.ringWidened`, `field.*`, `farm.harvestHome`, `fire.planned`, `fire.plannedCold`, `furniture.benchPlaced`.
- **State owned:** table `plan` (part, x, y, z, have, also, work, state `plan` / `built` / `down`, line, done, waited, shut, held, house); tables `house` (with `wall`, its wall part) and `want` (`houses.ts` `HOUSE_TABLES`), `field` (`fields.ts` `FIELD_TABLES`), `furniture` (`furniture.ts` `FURNITURE_TABLES`); system state `nobody`, `green`, `need`, `noGround`, `tried`, `ring`, `ringLine`, `fieldNeed`, `fieldNoGround`, `fieldTried`. `map.built` is written only through map `setBuilt`.
- **Invariants:** one plan row a tile; a row in state `built` matches `map.built` (or a stair tile).
- **Tests:** `build.test.ts` (a stone wall from hauled stone; taking down; `build.shutIn`; refusals; a stair; every command is a lever or test tool); `furniture.test.ts` (quality, bench, beds, owners unordered); `walls.test.ts` (the most plentiful wall material, with the reason; wattle and daub); `houses.test.ts` (an unordered cottage, then a room; moving in; one send-back); `fields.test.ts` (a field outside the ring; sowing to harvest; frost loss; the ring widening; one send-back).
