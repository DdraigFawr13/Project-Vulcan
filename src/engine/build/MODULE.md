# build

Plans and built parts (P3.1, P3.2): walls, floors, doors and stairs in wood and stone, a tile each. Houses the colony sites itself, rooms and growth (P3.7, P3.3, P3.8).

- **Public API:** `index.ts` (furniture: `piecesOf`, `furnitureOf`, `bedOwnedBy`, `benchOf`, `qualityOf`, `nextWant`, `roomName`, `wantSignature`, `siteFor`): `buildSystem`, `BUILD_TABLES`, `plansOf(world)`, `planAt(world, x, y, z = 0)`, `partOf(content, id)`, `planPart(content, plan)`, `stillNeeded(content, plan)` (material still to bring; 0 once all is in or for a part to come down), `refundOf(content, plan)` (what taking it down gives back), `finishPlan(ctx, plan, cause)` (sets the part on the map: `setBuilt` for a wall, floor or door, `cutStairsAt` for a stair, which reaches the level below), `takeDownPlan(ctx, plan, cause)` (clears it from the map, removes the row and returns the material to lay back: all of it for a built part, what was brought for a plan).
- **Parts:** content `buildings.json`: the build `job` (jobs.json, building skill), the skill dial `skilled`, `downShare` (per cent of a part's minutes to take it down) and one entry per part: kind (`wall` blocks the way, `door` and `floor` do not, `stair` cuts a way down), material (an item), cost, minutes and art. A built part is `map.built` (1 + its index); a stair is a tile kind instead, so stairs keep working as before.
- **Placing (test mode only, V-041, V-049):** `placeBuilding {part, x, y}` puts a plan on a buildable surface tile with no feature, plan, built part or store on it (and, for all but a floor, nothing lying there); otherwise `command.refused` with the reason. A plan of a good the colony keeps none of raises that good's stock level to its first non-zero level (goods `raiseStock`, a `goods.stockRaised` line citing the plan); the level stays raised after the plans are done or dropped, so the store keeps some for repairs until the player sets it back. `deconstruct {x, y}` drops a plan nothing was brought for at once (`build.dropped`), else orders the plan or built part down (`build.downOrdered`; state `down`); a cut stair stays.
- **Work:** done by people (`src/engine/people/build.ts`): nobody is ordered. A builder takes up the nearest free plan, fetches its material from wherever it lies (the store before ground at the same distance), brings it, then builds from beside the tile; a part finishes only when nobody stands on its tile, and anything lying on a wall or door tile is moved beside the builder first. A wall that would cut the builder off from someone or a store tile they reach now (a ring closed without a door) is left unbuilt: one `build.shutIn` alert a plan, and nobody tries it again for an hour. Plans whose material nobody can reach, and none of which is being carried or fetched, share one `build.waiting` alert per material until some is brought. Nothing is laid on a planned tile (people `layAround`, goods `freeTile`).
- **Houses (P3.7, V-049 to V-054):** content `buildings.house` (sizes lean-to, cottage, longhouse; the added-room `stage`; walls in preference order; `near`, `reach`). Each hour, while anyone sleeps outside a built house and no house is planned, `houses.ts` `planHouse` sites one (`build.needed` once, then `build.sited`): `site.ts` `chooseHouse` takes the first wall material there is some size of within `reach` of the store (stock plus trees, logs and boulders), the largest size that sleeps no more than the homeless (start small), and the best-scoring free rectangle within `near` of the green (the cart's tile): rise, distance, trees on the footprint and on the way in from the green (`way.ts`, a 0-1 search; a site whose way in crosses it is rejected). Its trees are marked (goods `mark` with `house`), cleared as building work in its place on the Work sheet (people `work.tookUpClearing`). No ground: one `build.noGround`, a look again daily.
- **Growth (P3.8, "cottage, then grow"):** `grow.ts` `growHouses` runs first: a built house with more sleepers than beds (its first house counts the homeless too) adds a 5 by 4 room sharing a wall on its flattest side (`build.grows`, citing the house built), walls of stock material, else of material within reach; only when no built house has ground for a room is another house sited. `finishHouses` marks a house or room built when all its plans stand (`build.houseBuilt`, `build.roomAdded`).
- **Rooms (P3.3, V-043):** `rooms.ts` `roomAt(world, content, x, y)`, worked out when asked: the tiles flood-filled without crossing a wall or door, closed off with at least one door; places to sleep (`beds`: the most of the built bed pieces and the `sleepers`, bedrolls laid in it plus beds owned), a role (content `rooms.roles`), beauty (floors, crowding, plus the beauty of each built piece's quality word), look word, crowding (`crowd.tiles` per bed), door tiles and impressiveness. Nothing stored.
- **Moving in:** people `home.ts`: at bedtime a homeless person, or one in a crowded room that others leaving would not uncrowd, carries their bedroll to a free floor (`bedSpot`, which counts the tiles others are carrying theirs to, person field `bedTo`); `people.movedIn` cites the house built.
- **Furniture (P3.4, P3.5, V-061 to V-067):** `furniture.ts` table `furniture` (kind, x, y, z, facing, quality score, maker, owner (a bed's), made, line, state `plan` / `built`, have, work, waited); `piecesOf`, `bedOwnedBy`, `benchOf`, `qualityOf(content, score)`. Content `buildings.furniture` (job `carpentry`, material, `stream` `craft`, `roll`, `kinds` with cost, minutes, art, beds, seats, `place` hint; `quality` words with `at` cut and beauty) and `rooms.wants` (what a room of a role lacks, in order). A piece is not a wall: it blocks no one's way and `map.built` is untouched. `wants.ts`: `nextWant` (a bed in the room with most sleepers lacking one, else the first thing the busiest room lacks that has a free tile), `siteFor` (the `place` hint; never the tile just inside a door, a piece or a lying item), `roomName` ("cottage", "cottage's second room"), `planBench` (hourly, from the build run: once a built room lacks furniture and no bench exists, the best crafter sets a bench plan against a wall of the largest room, `furniture.benchPlaced`). Making it is people/carpentry.ts. `homeless`, `bedsOf` and `bedSpot` count bed pieces and owned beds as well as bedrolls.
- **Hearths (P3.6, V-041, V-049):** `hearths.ts`: hourly in the build run, `planCampfire` plans a hearth piece (kind `hearth`, built by builders: its own `job` and `material` stone in `buildings.furniture.kinds`, sited by the `centre` hint) on the nearest free open tile to the green if none stands within 6 tiles of it (`fire.planned`, citing the world's beginning); `planRoomHearths` plans one in a built house room someone is in when its unheated temperature (weather `tileTemperature`) is under `rooms.hearthBelow` and it has none, citing the cold person's line or the weather spell (`fire.plannedCold`). Planned or built, it stays. Carpentry's `nextWant` skips kinds with a `job`. Beds now come before everything else in their room and may stand where a sleeper's bedroll lies.
- **Levers (Build sheet):** `setBuild {kind, level}` (1 first to 4 last; `build.wantSet`); `sendBack {house}` drops a plan nothing is built on yet, once (a replacement cannot be sent back): material brought is laid back, the site is never chosen again (state `sent`) and the colony chooses again at once (`build.sentBack`).
- **Checks:** each hour, while a plan waits and no living person meets the build job's requirements, one `build.nobody` alert (system state `nobody`, cleared when that changes).
- **Events:** `fire.planned`, `fire.plannedCold`; `furniture.benchPlaced` (cites the house built); `build.planned`, `build.dropped`, `build.downOrdered` (cite the order), `build.nobody` (cites the plan); from people: `work.tookUpBuilding` (cites the plan and the builder's priority if set), `build.waiting` and `build.shutIn` (cite the plan), `build.built` (cites taking it up; a stair's `map.stairsCut` cites it), `build.takenDown` (cites taking it up and the order).
- **State owned:** table `plan` (part, x, y, z, have, work, state `plan` / `built` / `down`, line, done, waited, shut, held, house); tables `house` and `want` (`houses.ts` `HOUSE_TABLES`), `furniture` (`furniture.ts` `FURNITURE_TABLES`); system state `nobody`, `green`, `need`, `noGround`, `tried`. `map.built` is written only through map `setBuilt`.
- **Invariants:** one plan row a tile; a row in state `built` matches `map.built` (or a stair tile); surface only for now.
- **Tests:** `build.test.ts` (a planned stone wall is built from quarried, hauled stone with only the placing orders, its tiles block the way and its lines lead back to the plan; taking it down gives the stone back; the last wall of a ring round a store tile stays unbuilt with one `build.shutIn`; refusals; a wooden stair reaches level 1; every command is an indirect lever or a test-mode tool); `furniture.test.ts` (quality follows the maker's crafting level; an unordered bench, beds and owners on three seeds; an owned bedroom beats the barracks over a week; carpentry is an indirect lever); `houses.test.ts` (an unordered cottage with a reason, then a room citing the house; everyone moves in once; the same site on the same seed; a room from its walls; the Build level and one send-back).
