# inn

The tavern (6B, V-188).

- **Public API:** `index.ts`: `innSystem` (in `SYSTEMS` after faith, hourly, stream `inn`), `atTheInn(ctx, p, evening)` (people mind, at leisure), `highestOn` (keeper and bard), `hungOver(ctx, p)` (people mind, a lost minute at work), reads `tavernOf`, `tavernName`, `inTavern`, `keeperOf`, `cupsTonight`, `kegsOf`, `cupsHeld`, and `fillCask` (crafts brew).
- **Content:** `tavern.json`: `build` (the Tavern row's `start`, house `size`, `names`, `wants`), `brew`, `stock` (the Ale row), `bard` (tales songs), `evening`, `keeper`, `bend`, `brawl`, `hungOver`; memories `drank-ale`, `hung-over`; tales.json `pass.tavern`, `places.tavern`, tale `tavern-brawl`.
- **Naming and keeper (`keeper.ts`):** a built tavern house with no row is named from `build.names` (`tavern.named`, citing the house built). The innkeeper is the living grown-up who puts Inn-keeping highest, ties to the sociable, then the lowest id (`tavern.keeper` on each change, citing the priority).
- **Evenings (`evening.ts`):** once an evening, as each grown-up's work ends, `evening.go` per cent plus traits say whether they go; the keeper always does, and on a winter evening everyone at leisure does, in place of the gathering by a lit hearth (they still mend by its fire). In the tavern they relax until bed, on a chair first. Each hour the keeper, when there, pours a cup from the fullest ready cask to each with fewer than `evening.cups` (`lowCups` when mood is under `low`): the `drank-ale` memory. When the ale runs out or no keeper is there they leave after `evening.leave` minutes, and once gone get no more.
- **Rowdy (`rowdy.ts`):** a `brawl.trait` drinker on their second cup throws the dice once an evening; at `brawl.chance` per cent they come to blows with the nearest drinker (`tavern.brawled`, citing the cask's brew; light wounds both ways, the `brawled` memory). Over `hungOver.after` cups: next morning the `hung-over` memory and until `hungOver.until` o'clock a minute in `idle` lost at work.
- **Tales (engine tales):** both inside the tavern is the `tavern` place (`pass.tavern` per cent an hour); the keeper hears `keeper.hears` new tales a day, others `pass.perDay`; a teller who drank tonight bends `bend` per cent more.
- **Events:** `tavern.named`, `tavern.keeper`, `tavern.brawled`; crafts brew's `tavern.tookUp`, `tavern.brewed`, `tavern.noGrain`.
- **State owned:** tables `tavern` (house, name, line, keeper, kept, noGrain), `keg` (piece, drink, cups, ready, line) and `night` (person, day, go, came, going, cups, leave, brawled, hung, line).
- **Invariants:** a keg's cups only fall by pouring and are set by a brew; reads people, traits, moods and the build tables, writes only its own tables, memories and wounds.
- **Tests:** `inn.test.ts` (sited after the harvest home citing it, named and kept, nobody's home; waits at "last"; ale poured and most tellings at the tavern; the dry tavern left within the hour; a brew fills a cask, grain kept while food is short; a brawl; a hangover till noon).
