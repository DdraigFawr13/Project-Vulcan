# health

Wounds, blood, healing, festering, marks, sickness and death (P5.6 to P5.8). Content: `health.json`. Realism rule (Morgan): healing takes weeks, wounds fester, bones heal crooked.

- **Public API:** `index.ts`: `healthSystem`, `HEALTH_TABLES`, `wound(ctx, p, {part, kind, severity, what, where, place}, causes, mortal)`, `die(ctx, p, cause, causes)`, `stopFor`, `woundsOf`, `openWounds`, `bodiesOf`, `bodyOf`, `scarsOf`, `isDown`, `keepsDown`, `armOut` (an open deep arm wound: no work), `markPercent(world, content, person, 'walk' | 'work')`, `woundName`, `woundWords`, `worstOf`, `severityOf`, `pronouns`, `bedWords`, `whereWords`, `dailyChance(total, days)`; sickness (P5.7): `expose(ctx, p, ill, how, causes, {sure, water, from, shows})`, `boutOf`, `illDef`, `shunned(world, content, tile)`.
- **Bites (P5.4a):** the wound kind `bite` (health.json `wounds`) has its own `fester` chances `[33, 50]` (cared, uncared) for its deep and grave wounds, read by `heal.ts` in place of the severity's; other kinds keep the severity's. Drawn by the people system's wolf fights (`people/fight.ts`).
- **Wounds (`wounds.ts`):** a `wound` row on one of six parts: kind, severity (light, deep, grave by `share`), `bleed` now (0, 1 slow, 2 fast), `mortal` (a grave wound that bleeds out whatever is done, `mortal` per cent), `bound` (1 rough, 2 healer), `dressed`, `progress` (hundredths of an hour), `fester`. One that bleeds slow on someone still standing is bound roughly by themselves. `body` row per person once hurt: `blood`, `down`, `carrier`, `healer`, `finder`/`found`, `bed`/`roll`, `died`/`diedAt`, `alerted`, `missed`. Down: a `down` severity, or a bleeding wound to a `downLimb`, until `upAt` per cent healed; a fever; blood under `faint`. Hurt: they put down what they carried and drop their work; down, they lie (`doing: hurt`). A finder or healer who is hurt, dies or is called away is replaced; one found with no free bed is carried once a bed frees (checked hourly).
- **Each hour (`heal.ts`):** blood drains `slow`/`fast` a bleeding wound (a healer-bound one a step less each hour), else regains `regain`; at 0 they die. Progress per hour: `heal.bed` abed or `up`, times 100 dressed within `dressEvery` hours, `undressed` if not, `rough` if never healer-bound; a light wound always 100. Festering: a daily roll in the first `fester.days`, so the whole chance is `fester[dressed ? 0 : 1]`; a fever lasts `fever` days with a daily roll to `dies` per cent. Healed: a mark (`markFor` the part) with `mark[healer ? 0 : 1]` per cent. Daily chances by halving, no logarithms (V-006).
- **Herbs (Bundle 6):** wound fields `herbed` and `herbless` (ticks); `herbedNow` is true within `herbs.every` days of a herb dressing and `festerChance` (`heal.ts`) then takes `herbs.fester` per cent of the chance (whole numbers). Soap (V-135): a dressing washes a wound once (`washed`, the tick) when the store has soap, one soap per `lamp.chandler.soap.washes` washings, and `festerChance` takes `soap.fester` per cent of what is left. The healer's side is `people/nurse.ts`; the Health tab adds `words.herbed` or `words.noHerbs` after "Bound by".
- **Sickness (`sick.ts`, P5.7):** illnesses are content `ills` (catch and spread in `sick.per`, days, `shows` days after catching, `abed`, `grave` and `dies` per cent, `immune` days or -1, `world` for strangers' diseases, `mark`). Table `ill` (a bout: `coming`, `sick`, `over`, `closed`; how, water tile, from, lines, healer, draught, `cut`, `herbless`) and `chilled` (hours today at no warmth). Hourly: `sick.coldHours` at warmth 0 in a day is one exposure to the chill; a bout shows with `health.sick` (or `health.spread` when given), an abed one sets body `down` and `found`, so care.ts beds and feeds them; a flux from a water says `health.shunned`; a grave case turns halfway (`health.grave`) and at its end dies at `dyingChance` (`dies` less the healer's `cut`, `die` naming `graveName`), else `health.passed` (with any `mark`). At `sick.night` each sick person gives a catching illness to each other person in their room at `spread`. Exposures from elsewhere: people `foul.ts` (drinks), `draught.ts` (nursing), `strangers.ts` (a neighbour's fresh disease, region `sick`). Healer side: people `draught.ts` and `surgery.ts`; wound `sewn` (per cent off festering for `kit.stitch` kinds and off a crooked mark for `kit.set` kinds) and `sewnBy`.
- **Sickness, the people's side (P5.7):** `foul.ts` `drankAt`: a drink within `sick.foulNear` of the midden or a fold, or from a lake at `sick.warmLake`°, is a flux exposure (`drink.ts` passes over shunned water). `draught.ts`: Healing work before other new work (`draughtFirst`, unless Healing is last), a draught (one herb) to the nearest abed sick person due one (`health.draught` / `health.noDraught` the first time; sets the bout's `cut`, the first shortens it; may give the healer a catching illness). `surgery.ts` `sew`: binding or dressing stitches cuts and bites (kit only) and sets breaks and crushes (by hand at `kit.hand`), `health.stitched`; the healer's kit is a `tools.json` kind serving Healing (`tools.ts` `equip`, `bestHeld`), worn by binding, dressing and draughts. `strangers.ts`: each stranger from a neighbour with a fresh disease is an exposure to it.
- **Soap in the homes (`soap.ts`, P5.7):** at each week's first midnight, one `lamp.chandler.soap.item` from the store per built home (table `soap`: `until`, `cut`); `sick.soap.ills` and roommates' spread then catch `sick.soap.cut` per cent less, by the share of homes served; `health.soap` / `health.noSoap` when that starts or stops.
- **Herb garden (build `herbs.ts`, P5.7):** table `garden` (x, y, w, h, line, fed). Hourly in a `health.garden.sow` month, when a draught or dressing found no herbs within `garden.within` days (the health tables' `herbless`), lays out `w` by `h` `herb-bed` parts (plan rows, Build row builders) beside the sickroom, else the first house (`build.herbGarden`); once. In each herb month every laid bed with nothing on it gets one herb for the haulers.
- **Death (`death.ts`):** `health.died` cites the wound, its care and the fever; status `dead`; tools back to the cart, bed freed; `mourn.close` for the same home or house, `mourn.band` for the rest. Hourly an unburied body gives `mourn.saw` to the awake within `near` and is said after `unburied` days. At `work.hours.to` a dug grave (build `grave`) is filled: status `buried`, `mourn.burial` for everyone above ground and awake.
- **Elsewhere:** accidents, finding, carrying, binding, dressing, feeding and digging are the people's (`people/accident.ts`, `care.ts`, `nurse.ts`, `burial.ts`); the sickroom and burial ground the build system's (`build/sickroom.ts`, `burial.ts`). Marks slow walking (`people/walk.ts`) and work (`people/tools.ts` `scaled`).
- **Commands:** `hurt {person, part, severity}` and `sicken {person, ill}`: test-mode tools (V-041).
- **Events:** `health.hurt` (notable when deep or grave), `health.festered`, `health.feverBroke`, `health.healed`, `health.died`, `health.unburied`, `health.buried`, `health.sick`, `health.spread`, `health.grave`, `health.passed`, `health.shunned`, `health.soap`, `health.noSoap`.
- **Invariants:** draws only from the `health` stream (with people's accident rolls); the dead keep their rows; a wound is `open`, `healed` or `closed` (at death).
- **Tests:** `health.test.ts` (a shaft fall carried to bed and bound; a death with its causes, buried with mourners; the bled-to-death wound named after binding stopped it; a sickroom); `sick.test.ts` (flux abed, passing, immune; draughts at start levels; turning food; soap; grave chill kills; cold brings the chill).
