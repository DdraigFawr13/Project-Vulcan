# health

Wounds, blood, healing, festering, lasting marks and death (P5.6, P5.8, V-114 to V-116). Content: `health.json`. Realism rule (Morgan): healing takes weeks, wounds fester, bones heal crooked.

- **Public API:** `index.ts`: `healthSystem`, `HEALTH_TABLES`, `wound(ctx, p, {part, kind, severity, what, where, place}, causes, mortal)`, `die(ctx, p, cause, causes)`, `stopFor`, `woundsOf`, `openWounds`, `bodiesOf`, `bodyOf`, `scarsOf`, `isDown`, `keepsDown`, `armOut` (an open deep arm wound: no work), `markPercent(world, content, person, 'walk' | 'work')`, `woundName`, `woundWords`, `worstOf`, `severityOf`, `pronouns`, `bedWords`, `whereWords`, `dailyChance(total, days)`.
- **Bites (P5.4a):** the wound kind `bite` (health.json `wounds`) has its own `fester` chances `[33, 50]` (cared, uncared) for its deep and grave wounds, read by `heal.ts` in place of the severity's; other kinds keep the severity's. Drawn by the people system's wolf fights (`people/fight.ts`).
- **Wounds (`wounds.ts`):** a `wound` row on one of six parts: kind, severity (light, deep, grave by `share`), `bleed` now (0, 1 slow, 2 fast), `mortal` (a grave wound that bleeds out whatever is done, `mortal` per cent), `bound` (1 rough, 2 healer), `dressed`, `progress` (hundredths of an hour), `fester`. One that bleeds slow on someone still standing is bound roughly by themselves. `body` row per person once hurt: `blood`, `down`, `carrier`, `healer`, `finder`/`found`, `bed`/`roll`, `died`/`diedAt`, `alerted`, `missed`. Down: a `down` severity, or a bleeding wound to a `downLimb`, until `upAt` per cent healed; a fever; blood under `faint`. Hurt: they put down what they carried and drop their work; down, they lie (`doing: hurt`). A finder or healer who is hurt, dies or is called away is replaced; one found with no free bed is carried once a bed frees (checked hourly).
- **Each hour (`heal.ts`):** blood drains `slow`/`fast` a bleeding wound (a healer-bound one a step less each hour), else regains `regain`; at 0 they die. Progress per hour: `heal.bed` abed or `up`, times 100 dressed within `dressEvery` hours, `undressed` if not, `rough` if never healer-bound; a light wound always 100. Festering: a daily roll in the first `fester.days`, so the whole chance is `fester[dressed ? 0 : 1]`; a fever lasts `fever` days with a daily roll to `dies` per cent. Healed: a mark (`markFor` the part) with `mark[healer ? 0 : 1]` per cent. Daily chances by halving, no logarithms (V-006).
- **Herbs (Bundle 6):** wound fields `herbed` and `herbless` (ticks); `herbedNow` is true within `herbs.every` days of a herb dressing and `festerChance` (`heal.ts`) then takes `herbs.fester` per cent of the chance (whole numbers). Soap (V-135): a dressing washes a wound once (`washed`, the tick) when the store has soap, one soap per `lamp.chandler.soap.washes` washings, and `festerChance` takes `soap.fester` per cent of what is left. The healer's side is `people/nurse.ts`; the Health tab adds `words.herbed` or `words.noHerbs` after "Bound by".
- **Death (`death.ts`):** `health.died` cites the wound, its care and the fever; status `dead`; tools back to the cart, bed freed; `mourn.close` for the same home or house, `mourn.band` for the rest. Hourly an unburied body gives `mourn.saw` to the awake within `near` and is said after `unburied` days. At `work.hours.to` a dug grave (build `grave`) is filled: status `buried`, `mourn.burial` for everyone above ground and awake.
- **Elsewhere:** accidents, finding, carrying, binding, dressing, feeding and digging are the people's (`people/accident.ts`, `care.ts`, `nurse.ts`, `burial.ts`); the sickroom and burial ground the build system's (`build/sickroom.ts`, `burial.ts`). Marks slow walking (`people/walk.ts`) and work (`people/tools.ts` `scaled`).
- **Commands:** `hurt {person, part, severity}`: a test-mode tool (V-041).
- **Events:** `health.hurt` (notable when deep or grave), `health.festered`, `health.feverBroke`, `health.healed`, `health.died`, `health.unburied`, `health.buried`.
- **Invariants:** draws only from the `health` stream (with people's accident rolls); the dead keep their rows; a wound is `open`, `healed` or `closed` (at death).
- **Tests:** `health.test.ts` (a shaft fall carried to bed and bound; a death with its causes, buried with mourners; the bled-to-death wound named after binding stopped it; a sickroom).
