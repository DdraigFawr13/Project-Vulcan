# contest

Contests at the harvest home (6C Slice 7 part 3, V-236, V-241): who wins, a byname after three, rivals.

- **Public API:** `index.ts`: `contestSystem` (every minute, before `lordSystem`, stream `contest`), `CONTEST_TABLES`, `contestsAt(world, feast)` (the contests held at a supper, in order; the lord reads it for what pleases a bold lord and his prize), `contestsOf(world)`, `rivals(world, a, b)`.
- **Content:** `contests.json` (schema `schemas-contests.ts`): `each` a harvest home, `lord` with the lord there, `least` contestants; `enter` (`base`, `traits`, `rival`, `won`, in 100); `roll`; `wins` for a byname; `beaten` for a rival; `memories` (`contest-won`, `contest-lost`, `contest-rival`); `kinds` (needs `none` / `field` / `brewer`, `skill` and `least`, `attribute`, the winner's words, `byname`, `m`, `spares`, the lord's `prize` tool kind).
- **Holding (`hold.ts`):** the minute a harvest-home supper (a `feast` row with no couple and no feast day) is eaten, `each` contests are drawn from those the valley can hold (a field laid out, a cask brewed, the trial of strength always), those not held last time first; `lord` of them when the reeve's row says the lord rode in for it, the last one his. A supper let go holds none.
- **Who stands up:** the grown-ups at the supper, up and about and not strangers waiting, with the contest's skill at `least`, each at `base` plus their traits, plus `rival` while their rival can stand too and `won` if they won it before. Fewer than `least`, and it is not held.
- **Winning:** skill level plus attribute plus 0 to `roll`, ties to the earlier person. `contest.held` (all who stood, winner first, citing the eaten supper); the winner keeps `won`, the rest `lost` about the winner. The `wins`th win of one contest gives its byname (`m` for a man), else a spare no living person holds, unless they have one (tales `bestow`, a `tale.byname` line with no story). Runner-up `beaten` times to the same winner: `contest.rival`, a `rival` row and `rival` for the beaten.
- **Events:** emits `contest.held`, `contest.rival`, `tale.byname`; reads the `feast` rows (build) and the `reeve` row's `lord` and `feast` (lord) as data.
- **State owned:** tables `contest` (kind, year, feast, winner, entered, line, lord) and `rival` (person, of, line); state `done`, the last supper seen.
- **Invariants:** no lever picks a contestant or a contest (V-041); draws only from stream `contest`; at most one set of contests a supper.
- **Tests:** `contest.test.ts` (a supper's contest, its lines and memories; two with the lord and his prize; a third win's byname; a rival).
