# teach

Teaching (V-189).

- **Public API:** `index.ts`: `teachSystem` (in `SYSTEMS` after faith, hourly, no RNG), `helping` and `takeUpHelping` (the job table's `help` row), `takeUpLessons`, `teaching`, `atLessons` (rows `teach`, `lesson`), `scribing`, `reading` (people mind), `standingSchool`, `pupil`, `booksOf`, `keptBooks`, `bookName`, `schoolOf`, `apprenticesOf`, `taughtOf`, `skillWord`, `withoutApprentices` (test content with no masters). Inside: bond.ts `APPRENTICES` (the law id) and `wayOf` (`all`, `choice` or `none`; a refuser keeps the custom).
- **Content:** `teach.json` (schema `schemas-teach.ts`): `school` (build, teacher, from, lesson, book, read, words); job `teach` and work.json's School heading; events `build.school`, `school.*`, `skill.read`; `master`, `ahead`, `reach`, `bondReach`, `beside`, `apprentice`, `past`, `slow`, `traits`, `least`, `hour`, `friend`, `choose`, `end`, `child`, `near`, `memories`, `words`; law `apprentices` in `laws.json` (settings' `apprentices`); events `skill.taught`, `teach.*`; memories `taught-apprentice`, `finished-apprentice`; tale `lost-art` and belief `arts-lost`.
- **Pace (band `learn.ts`):** practice is kept in hundredths of a minute. Traits add their per cent (never under `least`); alone, levels from `slow.from` take `slow.times` as long; beside a master (level `master`+, `ahead`+ levels ahead, awake at the same work within `reach`) `beside` per cent, beside one's own master (within `bondReach`) `apprentice` per cent. Taught, a learner can rise `past` levels beyond their potential, never above the teacher less 1. A taught rise is `skill.taught` naming the teacher.
- **Apprentices (`bond.ts`, daily at `hour`):** each grown master with no bond weighs the law, in the skill at `master`+ they have practised most (skill row `used`, so the one they work at): `all` a keeper takes one; `none` takes none and parts with one; `choice` (custom, and refusers) only with a `choose` trait or for their child or a friend at `friend` closeness. Pick: own child, closest friend, highest gift, among the grown `ahead`+ behind, able for a Work sheet kind of the skill, who can still learn from them (`teach.apprentice`, citing the law line or the child's birth). Ends `teach.finished` (within `end` levels, or as far as this master can take them; both gain a memory) or `teach.parted` (death, leaving, None).
- **Working beside (`help.ts`):** a free grown apprentice whose master is at a Work sheet kind of the bond's skill, and who puts that kind level with their best Priorities (V-041) and is able for it, takes up `help` (the bond's line is the why): walks beside the master and practises the skill each minute there; a cold room's hearth at the same priority comes first. It adds nothing to the master's work. It stops when the master leaves that work.
- **Watching (`watch.ts`, hourly):** a child within `child.reach` of a parent at work gains `child.rate` per cent of an hour's practice in it, up to `child.upTo` (a `skill.taught` naming the parent).
- **Record (`record.ts`, hourly):** each `skill.taught` line makes or extends a `taught` row.
- **Loss (`loss.ts`, hourly):** state notes each living master's level by skill (`m<id>.<skill>`); one gone with nobody living within `near` levels writes `teach.lost`, citing the death or leaving. Nobody then teaches past the gift in that skill until someone reaches master level again, since teaching needs a teacher at `master`+ and at most the teacher less 1 (rule 6). Masters are noted hourly, so one who rises and dies within the hour is missed.
- **The schoolroom (`school.ts`, `books.ts`, V-189 q7):** sited by build `school.ts` (the Build sheet's Schoolroom row). Hourly `chooseTeacher`: the teacher is `highestOn` the `school.teacher.job` (Teaching) row, a `school.teacher` line on each change citing the schoolroom built and their priority. In the first `school.lesson.hours` of the working day, with a child of school age (`school.from` per cent of their coming of age, `pupil`), the teacher takes up `teach` before other work (people `jobs.ts`) and each such child `lesson`: each minute both are inside, the child's highest-potential skill below `lesson.upTo` gains a practice minute (trait pace), a `skill.taught` naming the teacher. With no child of school age, in their free time (people mind, before the tavern) the teacher `scribing`: picks the living master with the highest level whose skill has no kept book at it (`school.writing`), and each minute within `book.near` of them while their `doing` is in `book.free` is a sitting; `book.minutes` write it (`school.book`, kept on the `book.shelf`). `reading`: in free time, a grown person reads the highest kept book in a skill they have worked at (`used`), inside the schoolroom, up to `read.minutes` a day at `read.pace` per cent, up to min(potential + `past`, the book's level less `read.less`): a `skill.read` naming the book and its master (recorded as taught). Hourly `loseBooks`: with no shelf standing in the schoolroom, kept books are lost (`school.bookLost`). A kept book within `near` of a dead master's level keeps their skill (`loss.ts`).
- **State owned:** tables `apprentice` (master, learner, skill, line, since, ended), `taught` (learner, teacher, skill, from, to, first, last), `school` (house, teacher, kept), `book` (master, skill, level, scribe, state `writing` / `kept` / `lost`, minutes, begun, line, lost) and `reader` (person, day, minutes); state `seen` (last ledger id read) and the masters.
- **Invariants:** no RNG; whole numbers; one bond per person; reads skills, traits, bonds, answers and the ledger; writes its tables, memories and (through band `gain`) skill rows.
- **Tests:** `teach.test.ts` (an apprentice beside their master passes their gift and outpaces one alone, the ledger names the teacher; a keeper of Every master takes one, citing the law; a master's death with nobody near takes the skill, citing it); `school.test.ts` (siting, teacher, lessons, a book written and read, kept and lost).
