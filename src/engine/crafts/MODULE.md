# crafts

Building and the workshop trades (P3.1 to P3.6, P5.3, P5.5, P5.13, 5B Bundles 3 to 5): building plans, carpentry, hearths and tending, cooking, the mill, the larder rows, the loom's trades, charcoal, smelting and smithing, beekeeping and candles. Each file gives rows to the job table (`people/jobtable.ts`). Uses `band` and `herding` (the watch fire's wood).

- **Public API:** `index.ts`: `LARDER_JOBS`, `MILL_JOBS`, `HEARTHS`, `TRADE_JOBS`, `banks`, `beekeeping`, `building`, `burning`, `chandling`, `clayWanted`, `coldHearth`, `coldPick`, `cooking`, `furnishing`, `hearthFirst`, `markUsed`, `reachableStacks`, `smelting`, `smithing`, `stackOf`, `stationing`, `takeUpBeekeeping`, `takeUpBuilding`, `takeUpCarpentry`, `takeUpChandling`, `takeUpChange`, `takeUpCharcoal`, `takeUpCooking`, `takeUpHearth`, `takeUpSmelting`, `takeUpSmithing`, `takeUpStation`, `takeUpTending`, `tendForWarmth`, `tendInEvening`, `tendRule`, `tending`.
- **Building (P3.1, V-041):** `build.ts`: a free person able for the `buildings.job` takes up the nearest free plan, fetches its material, brings it, builds it from beside the tile, or takes a part down that was ordered down; the `plan` field holds the plan's id.
- **Carpentry and beds (P3.4, P3.5, V-041, V-061 to V-066):** `carpentry.ts`, a Work sheet kind after Building (`carpentry`, the crafting skill). A free able person takes up the bench plan, else a piece left part-made, else build `nextWant`, fetches the logs (`stackOf`), works beside the bench `minutes × skilled ÷ (skilled + level)`, and the piece is set at the spot chosen at the start; quality = crafting level ± up to `roll` from the `craft` stream (`work.tookUpCarpentry`, `furniture.made`, `build.waiting` when wood cannot be reached). Beds: `people` `claim.ts`.
- **Cooking (V-090, V-041, P5.3):** `cook.ts`, the Cook row (content `work.cook`). While meals (made and in the making) are under `perPerson` × people alive and firewood is stored, a free able person takes the nearest lit hearth (else an unlit one, lit on arrival: `fire.lit` "a meal was to be cooked") and the best `recipes` entry whose main (`rawFor`) and side food can be had, else plain fare from `from`; fetches both, works `minutes × 2 × skilled ÷ (skilled + level)`, lays `batch` graded by level plus a `roll` (`grades`: plain, good, fine; `food.cooked`, `food.firstFine` once a person). Eating leaves the grade's memory; `mind.ts` prefers a meal up to `cook.prefer` tiles further. Fields `what`, `lit`, `fineMeal`. A worn cloak is held but not a load: use goods `loadOf`.
- **Clothes and the loom (P5.5):** `change.ts`, `weave.ts`, `tan.ts`, `tailor.ts`, `trades.ts`: `docs/CLOTHES_AND_LOOM.md`.
- **Larder rows (5B Bundle 3):** `cure.ts`, `cooper.ts`, `potter.ts` (`larder.ts`; `work.cure`, `cooper`, `potter`): a curer smokes or salts fresh meat; a cooper casks the stack with most food beyond its barrels; at a built kiln a potter pots the grain most beyond its pots or fires wall bricks (the miner digs clay). One each.
- **Metal and tools (Bundle 3):** `station.ts`, `charcoal.ts`, `smelt.ts`, `smith.ts`, `tools.ts`, `toolFactor`: `docs/TOOLS_AND_SMITHY.md`.
- **Hearths, tending and warmth (P3.6, V-044 to V-048):** `hearth.ts`: a free builder takes up the nearest planned hearth piece they can get stone for (`work.tookUpHearth`), made through carpentry's `furnishing` (plain), then fire `layHearth`. In the cold a slept-in room's planned hearth wins ties (`coldHearth`, `hearthFirst`). `tend.ts`, the Tend row: (a) a lit fire under `tend.low` minutes, (b) an unlit hearth in use or with someone cold near, (c) splitting logs into firewood while the Firewood plate is short, (d) topping a used fire under `tend.topBelow` up to `tend.top`; only where the unheated tile is under `warmth.comfort`; a fire unused `tend.idle` minutes burns down (`work.tookUpTending`, `fire.lit`); also in the evening. Warming oneself: `people` `warm.ts`. Bedtime banks the fire in reach (`fire.banked`). `hearthlaw.ts` (6B, V-184): each tender's `tendRule` from their Hearths answer: a keeper uses the setting's `hearth` numbers (`below` moves the line, `bank` the bedtime banking), a refuser the custom with a weekly `law.keptCustom`, a cold grumbler under a banking setting stokes the old way (`fire.stoked`). `haul.ts`: carrying.
- **Beekeeping (P5.13, V-135):** `beekeep.ts`, the Beekeeping row (after Candlemaking; job `beekeep`, outdoor work so it stops at dusk; content `lamp.bees`). A free able person takes up a skep to be made, else the honey of a skep with bees in the harvest month, else in a swarm month the looking over of an empty skep (once a day each); works from beside it and the bees module changes the skep and writes the line (`work.tookUpBees` for making and honey only). Honey and wax lie round the skep for haulers. Rules and lines: `src/engine/bees/MODULE.md`.
- **Chandler (V-135):** `chandle.ts`, the Candlemaking row (Lamp heading; the crafting skill; content `lamp.chandler`). The first recipe wanted with its inputs in the store: soap while soap stored or carried is under `soap.keep`, else candles while all candles (kept ones too) plus those being made are under the Candles plate (wax before tallow). A free able person fetches the first input, stands beside the nearest lit hearth with fuel for `minutes × 2` (one in a room first), works `minutes × 2 × skilled ÷ (skilled + level)`, draws the other inputs as the batch is made (ash from the hearths' pits, fire `takeAsh`, else the store) and lays the result for haulers (`lamp.tookUp`, `lamp.dipped`). Dressing (`nurse.ts`) washes a wound with store soap (health MODULE.md). Recipe id in `make`.
- **Thatching (6B):** `thatch.ts`, `docs/ROOFS_AND_STAGES.md`.
- **Events:** `work.tookUp*`, `food.cooked`, `food.firstFine`, `furniture.made`, `lamp.tookUp`, `lamp.dipped`, `fire.lit`, `fire.banked`, `build.waiting` (content `events.json`).
- **Tests:** `smelt.test.ts`; `cook.test.ts` (cooked meals raise the mood; priority moves who cooks); `cure.test.ts` (smokehouse from need, curing, coopering); `larder.test.ts`; `potter.test.ts`; `loom.test.ts` (P5.5); `chandle.test.ts`.
