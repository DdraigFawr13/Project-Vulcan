# map

The valley's ground: every surface tile and what it is made of, and the four levels below.

- **Public API:** `index.ts`: `mapSystem` (generates the surface in `init` when a world begins from a seed, never on load, from the `worldgen` stream; handles the `cutStairs {x, y, z}` and `sinkShaft {x, y, z, to}` orders), `setTile(ctx, x, y, kind, cause, z = 0)` (the only writer of a tile afterwards, on any level: it records `cause`, the event the caller emitted to explain the change, and raises the chunk's version; below the surface a tile that stops being rock loses its vein), `layerOf(map, z)` (level `z`'s tiles, causes, features and chunk versions: the map itself for 0, undefined for an unreached level), `CHUNK` (16), `chunksAcross(map)`, `chunksDown(map)`, `chunkTiles(map, cx, cy, z = 0)` (one chunk's ground, features and snow as `Uint8Array`s, for the worker's snapshots). `tiles.ts` holds these helpers.
- **Generator:** `terrain.ts` lays the ground (climate drawn by weight, a trough along one of 16 written-out axes roughened by value noise, spurs from ridged noise, rock from `rock.line` metres above the valley floor (held between the `rock.wall` shares, so rock share varies by seed), scree and crags by percentile, the valley walls of one rock drawn by weight, rock colder than `rock.snowline` in the top `rock.snowTop` of the heights marked in the `snow` layer (the tile keeps its rock), biome from the forked temperature-by-rainfall table, fertility); `mountains.ts` raises massifs of drawn radius, height and stretch, each of one rock, bent by a warp field, placed by the layout `generator.layout` names in `generator.layouts` (`valley`: 5 to 8 ranges on the rims, mostly along the axis; `highlands`, kept for later: massifs inside the valley too); `water.ts` then carves the water (the floor tilted `water.tilt` metres down the axis so it drains one way; hollows flooded to their brim by `flood.ts`'s priority flood; each tile drains to a lower neighbour drawn by its drop from a position hash, so courses wind; rain gathered downhill, heavier on the ranges; streams below the rock line, rivers past `riverFlow`, widened with flow and never deep (Morgan, 2026-10-17); every course runs on to the edge or a lake; hollows past `lakeDepth` and `lakeArea` become lakes, deep past `deepDepth`; stream heads are springs; shores of sand, scree or marsh in patches) and the map turns water into the `water.shallow`, `water.deep` and `water.spring` kinds; `checks.ts` reads the water done-whens (`waterNearStart`, `strandedWater`; tests and map-stats import it directly, `index.ts` does not export it); `features.ts` places trees in groves and old-growth stands, fallen logs, bushes and herbs in glades,, herbs and boulders from `features.json` and picks the start tile (nearest the middle with the start floors within `start.radius`, a square, counting only ground joined to the start without crossing rock: `reachableAround`); `noise.ts` is value noise on an integer lattice from the stream, smoothstep only, no transcendental math or square roots (V-006). Every dial, and every kind, biome and item id the generator names, is in `terrain.json` `generator`; features that clump into groves say so with `clumps`. The surface takes its draws from `worldgen` only in `init`; P1.3's lazy levels need their own per-level streams, so reaching levels in a different order cannot change them. `npm run map:stats` and `map:render` run it over `seeds.ts`.
- **Levels below (P1.3):** `levels.ts` reaches a level the first time stairs or a shaft get to it: `underground.ts` `makeLevel(seed, content, map, depth)` lays it out from sub-states of the `veins` stream and the surface rock as generated (`map.rock`, never changed by digging) alone (ARCHITECTURE RNG), so any order of reaching gives the same level: each range's rock spread out beneath it along a warped cost (`roots`), bedrock bodies drawn once per seed elsewhere (`bodies`), earth, clay and groundwater on level 1 (`earth`), tunnels dug by worms that snake and stop, and caverns (`caves`), then each `features.json` vein of that level in clusters through solid rock (its `near` clusters within the start radius). Dials in `terrain.json` `generator.underground`. The two orders are test levers until colonists dig (stairs in P3.2, mining in P4.1); a refused order writes `map.digRefused` with its reason (off the map, from an unreached or the deepest level, under water, a way down already there, a tree or other blocking feature in the way).
- **Events:** emits `map.formed` (cites `world.begun`) once, at world creation, with the climate and main biome, then `map.watered` (cites `map.formed`) with the river's exit edge and the lake and spring counts; `map.stairsCut` and `map.shaftSunk` (cite the order's `command.applied`), `map.levelReached` (cites the stairs or shaft; its rocks and ores in words; every tile of the new level names it as its cause) and `map.digRefused`; reads none. Layers are written whole under that one line, never per tile.
- **State owned:** `world.map`: `width`, `height`, `tiles` (index into `content.terrain.kinds`, row by row), `tileCauses` (the id of the ledger line that last changed each tile, `map.formed` at first; the inspector's "last changed") and `chunkVersions` (one counter per chunk, raised when a tile in it changes, so the worker sends only changed chunks); the generator's layers as typed arrays (`elevation` Int16 metres, `biomes`, `fertility` 0 to 100, `features` 0 or 1 + index, `snow` 0 or 1, `water` 0 or a body code from core `WATER` (stream, river, lake, spring), all Uint8), `riverExit`, `startX`, `startY`, `climate`, `temperature`, `rainfall`; `levels` (one per level below, null until reached, else `tiles` Uint8, `tileCauses` Int32, `features` Uint8 and `chunkVersions`). Features have no writer yet; the first milestone that fells a tree adds one beside `setTile`, raising the chunk's version.
- **Invariants:** size and starting ground come from `content.terrain.surface`; every tile names an existing kind (`setTile` checks; content allows at most 256 kinds, as tiles travel as bytes). Tiles are kind indices, so a save is only valid with the content it was made with (P0.5 `load` refuses a different `contentHash`). The surface is generated in `init`; later generation (V-014's underground levels, made when first reached) will go through one generator entry point that `init` also calls and that emits a line citing its trigger. Level `z` is 0 for the surface and 1 to `underground.depth` below; `MapState`, `setTile`, `ChunkView` and `inspect` all carry it (P1.3). A level not yet reached stores nothing. Which picture a tile shows is the renderer's choice, never state.
- **Tests:** `src/engine/engine.test.ts` (the determinism test also compares the map); `map.test.ts` (the P1.1 start done-when and P1.2's water near the start and no inland course ends, on every seed in `seeds.ts`); `levels.test.ts` (P1.3: copper and tin within the start radius on level 2 on every seed; levels made only when reached, the same in any order; stairs and refusals); the save size before and after a level is reached is in `src/worker/save.test.ts`.
