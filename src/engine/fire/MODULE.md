# fire

Hearths and fires (P3.6): a lit fire burns fuel, warms its room or its radius, is banked for the night and goes out with a line saying why.

- **Public API:** `index.ts`: `fireSystem`, `FIRE_TABLES`, `heatAt(world, content, i)` (whole degrees lit fires add at surface tile `i`; `needs` `feltAt` adds it to the weather), `heatMap`, `firesOf`, `fireOf(world, pieceId)`, `pieceOfFire`, `fuelOf`, `reachOf` (a fire's room tiles, else the tiles within `fire.radius`), `placeOf` ("in the cottage", "on the green"), `bankFire(ctx, person, spot)`, `layHearth(ctx, builder, piece)` (a finished hearth piece: line, plain quality, an unlit fire).
- **Read by the screens (P3.6):** the engine's index also exports `fireOf`, `placeOf`, `heatAt` and (from needs) `feltAt`, so the worker's views read a fire's state, its place in words and the felt temperature without working any of them out again.
- **Content:** `work.json` `fire`: `fall` degrees per tile, `radius`, `fuels` (item, `minutes` a piece burns, `heat`, `banked` heat and `rate` times slower), `split` (a log into pieces), `tend` numbers (`low`, `top`, `topBelow`, `idle`, `near`). A hearth is a furniture kind with its own `job` (Building) and `material` (stone) in `buildings.json`.
- **Heat (V-043):** a lit fire gives its fuel's `heat` at its tile less `fall` per tile of Chebyshev distance, over the closed room it stands in or the open tiles within `radius`; a tile takes the most from any one fire. Worked out when asked and kept in a cache keyed by the next line id and the lit fires (never saved).
- **Hourly:** a lit fire used by anyone in its reach keeps `used`; banked fires unbank at dawn (the people's `workFrom`); fuel falls 60 minutes (60 / `rate` banked); at 0 the fire goes out (`fire.out`), citing the empty Firewood plate when the colony wants none, else its last line; "nobody has used the room since {time}" when idle for `tend.idle`.
- **Ash (V-135):** each hour a lit fire adds the fuel minutes it burned to its pit (`ash`, at most `lamp.ash.keep` × `every`); `ashIn` counts whole ash, `takeAsh` takes it for the chandler's soap (this hearth first). Nothing is laid or hauled.
- **Events:** `fire.built`, `fire.lit` and `fire.banked` (written from people code through these helpers), `fire.out`, `fire.unbanked`. Planning lines are the build system's; tending ones the people's.
- **State owned:** table `fire` (piece, lit, fuel, kind, banked, used, line, ash). No system state. It reads build rooms (a fire's reach) but never people: the people mark `used` hourly (people/hearth.ts `markUsed`), and people code writes the table when they light, feed, bank or build a fire (people/tend.ts, `bankFire`, `layHearth`).
- **Invariants:** whole numbers; a fire exists only for a built hearth piece; fuel is 0 when unlit.
- **Tests:** `fire.test.ts` (three seeds in a hard winter with no orders: campfire lit, every used room warm, nobody warm for two hours under the warning line; no room hearth in warm weather; heat falls with distance; the out line leads to the Firewood plate; tending is an indirect lever).
