# mine

Shafts sited by an ore stain when a metal is wanted (P4.1).

- **Public API:** `index.ts`: `mineSystem` (hourly, no RNG stream), `MINE_TABLES`, `shaftsOf(world)`, `shaftFor(world, metal)` (the live shaft of a metal: not `sent` or `spent`), `SHAFT_STATES`, `veinOpen(world, content, reach, f, i)` (the one rule for an open vein that can feed a metal), `metalsOf(content)` (vein features whose `${id}-ingot` has a Stock plate), `oreShort(world, content, metal)` (ore still to find: goods `ingotWant` times `work.mine.perIngot` less the ore held anywhere), `nextDig(world, content, foot, metal, taken)` (the next tile to dig on the vein level and the open tile to dig it from).
- **Planning:** once a day and when an ingot plate changes, for each metal with `oreShort` > 0 and no vein tile of it the band can get beside from the green (`site.ts` `openVein`, built on `veinOpen`: veins glint through the fog once their level is reached, V-030 and V-094, so the tile need not be known), `site.ts` takes the nearest tile (larger of its two offsets from the green, then tile index) whose `map.stains` is that metal's, past the town ring (build `ringOf`), on ground walking at no more than `work.mine.walk` times the plain pace, with no feature, plan or building, no shaft, at least `work.mine.avoid` tiles from a plan sent back or a shaft spent, and reachable from the green. `mine.shaftPlanned` cites the plate's `goods.stockSet` and carries `stain` (the colour word), `x`, `y` and a `why` (the sentence `work.mine.why` filled with the wanted ingots, the stain's colour word, tiles and direction). No tile: `mine.noStain` once per metal per plate change. A shaft that is `done` while no way to a vein opens from its foot (`nextDig` finds none) and no vein is open is `spent`: `mine.noVein` (notable, citing the plate line) once per plate change, and the same pass sites another away from it. A replacement plan has `sentBack` 1 only when the last shaft row of the metal is the `sent` one it replaces.
- **Lever:** `sendBackShaft {shaft}` (normal; V-041): a shaft still `planned`, with no digging begun (`depth` 0 and nobody has taken up its sink task; else the reason "digging has begun") and not itself a replacement; the plan goes to `sent`, `mine.shaftSentBack`, and the colony plans again elsewhere with `sentBack` 1. Otherwise `command.refused` with a reason.
- **Tunnelling:** `tunnel.ts` `nextDig` is a Dijkstra over the vein level from the shaft foot, planned on what miners know: known open ground costs 1, a vein tile `work.mine.vein` minutes, a known rock or earth tile (map `diggable`) `dig`, an unknown tile `dig` whatever it really is; known water that cannot be walked is never entered; ties go to the lower tile. The way runs through known open ground first and the first tile on it that is not known open is next. If that tile is really open (a cave, water) the miner digs nothing: standing beside it shows it (`openAround`) and the way is found again.
- **Events:** emits `mine.shaftPlanned`, `mine.shaftSentBack`, `mine.noStain`, `mine.noVein`; the digging lines (`mine.sank`, `mine.dug`, `mine.veinMined`, `mine.clayDug`, `mine.noLadder`) are the people's (`src/engine/people/mine.ts`). Reads `goods.stockSet` lines by plate.
- **State owned:** table `shaft` (x, y, metal, to, state one of `SHAFT_STATES`: `planned` / `sinking` / `done` / `sent` / `spent`, depth, sentBack, line, why: the reason kept for the tile page); system state `sig`, `day` (the last look), `no-<metal>` and `novein-<metal>` (the plate line a `mine.noStain` or `mine.noVein` was said for). Writes no tile: sinking and digging go through map `sinkShaftAt`, `setTile` and `openAround`.
- **Digging (people side):** a sinker takes up the first level only when the logs held plus the stacks they can reach cover the `ladder` (else `mine.noLadder`, once per plate change), fetches stack by stack until the count is held, and `stop` lays down a load still held. `work.tookUp` for sinking and tunnelling cites the plate and the shaft's plan line. A vein tile is taken up whether or not it was shown. What digging gives lies on the free tiles round the dug one (`layYield`), and what finds none on the dug tile itself: nothing vanishes. Clay: the plan part's material that a terrain kind digs, from the tiles `digsFor` (build) allows, the rule a house counts clay by. Every vein's `yields` must be a `work.haul.kinds` entry (content check).
- **Invariants:** at most one live (not `sent` or `spent`) shaft per metal; `depth` never above `to`; the save check holds each shaft row to a known state, `depth` <= `to` <= the level count.
- **Tests:** `mine.test.ts`: siting past the ring on plain ground with the reason; iron ore in the store with only the plate raised, each line tracing to it; send back once and the refusals (a replacement; sinking taken up); `mine.noStain` once; a shaft with no vein left spent, `mine.noVein` once, another sited; a ladder from stacks of one log; `mine.noLadder` once; a vein seen unshown, tunnels planned on known ground; clay dug and hauled up; determinism. `people/mining.test.ts`: ore never lost, a held ladder laid down, an unseen open tile shown not dug.
