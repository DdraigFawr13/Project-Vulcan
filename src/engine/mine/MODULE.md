# mine

Shafts sited beside a found seam when a metal is wanted (P4.1, V-215).

- **Public API:** `index.ts`: `mineSystem` (hourly, no RNG stream), `MINE_TABLES`, `shaftsOf(world)`, `shaftFor(world, metal)` (the live shaft of a metal: not `sent` or `spent`), `SHAFT_STATES`, `veinOpen(world, content, reach, f, i)` (the one rule for an open vein that can feed a metal), `metalsOf(content)` (vein features whose `${id}-ingot` has a Stock plate), `oreShort(world, content, metal)` (ore still to find: goods `ingotWant` times `work.mine.perIngot` less the ore held anywhere), `saltShort(world, content)` (salt still to dig for the cure), `saltCause(world)` (the line a salt shaft plan cites), `smokehouseOf(world)` (the built smokehouse, if any), `nextDig(world, content, foot, metal, taken)` (the next tile to dig on the vein level and the open tile to dig it from).
- **Planning:** once a day, when an ingot plate changes or a seam is found, for each metal with `oreShort` > 0 and no known vein tile of it the band can get beside from the green (`site.ts` `openVein` on `veinOpen`: known tiles only, V-215), `siteShaft` takes the nearest found seam (`liveSeams`: `map.places` with its `what`, none within `work.mine.avoid` of a plan sent back or a shaft spent) and beside it (`siteBy`, within `work.mine.near`) the nearest tile walking at no more than `work.mine.walk`, with no feature, plan, building or shaft, reachable from the green. `mine.shaftPlanned` cites the plate, worn tools and the seam's line, with `seam` (colour word), `x`, `y` and `why` (`work.mine.why`). No seam: no plan (prospecting is land's `prospect.ts`). A `done` shaft with no way to a vein is `spent`: `mine.noVein` once per plate change, and the next seam is tried. A replacement plan has `sentBack` 1 only when the last shaft row of the metal is the `sent` one it replaces.
- **Lever:** `sendBackShaft {shaft}` (normal; V-041): a shaft still `planned`, with no digging begun (`depth` 0 and nobody has taken up its sink task; else the reason "digging has begun") and not itself a replacement; the plan goes to `sent`, `mine.shaftSentBack`, and the colony plans beside another found seam with `sentBack` 1. Otherwise `command.refused` with a reason.
- **Tunnelling:** `tunnel.ts` `nextDig` is a Dijkstra over the vein level from the shaft foot to a vein tile miners know of (shown, or behind a found seam: `seamTiles`): known open ground costs 1, a known vein tile `vein`, a known rock or earth tile `dig`, an unknown tile `dig` whatever it is; known water not walked is never entered; ties to the lower tile. The first tile on the way not known open is next; if it is really open the miner only shows it. **Search tunnels** (`search.ts`, V-215): with a `done` or `spent` shaft at an ore's level, that ore wanted and no seam of it sitable, one tunnel at a time of up to `work.mine.search` tiles from a known cave or water edge the foot reaches (farthest first), else the foot, the way (of 8, a slant as a stair) with most known liked rock (`vein.rocks`); `mine.searchBegun` when taken up (`beginSearch`); state `search` keys `<metal>-<shaft>` and tried ways.
- **Events:** emits `mine.shaftPlanned`, `mine.shaftSentBack`, `mine.noVein`; the digging lines (`mine.sank`, `mine.dug`, `mine.veinMined`, `mine.veinShown`, `mine.searchBegun`, `mine.clayDug`, `mine.noLadder`) are the people's (`src/engine/land/mine.ts`). Reads `goods.stockSet` lines by plate.
- **State owned:** table `shaft` (x, y, metal, to, state one of `SHAFT_STATES`: `planned` / `sinking` / `done` / `sent` / `spent`, depth, sentBack, line, why: the reason kept for the tile page); system state `sig`, `day` (the last look), `novein-<metal>` (the plate line a `mine.noVein` was said for). Writes no tile: sinking and digging go through map `sinkShaftAt`, `setTile` and `openAround`.
- **Digging (people side):** a sinker takes up the first level only when the logs held plus the stacks they can reach cover the `ladder` (else `mine.noLadder`, once per plate change), fetches stack by stack until the count is held, and `stop` lays down a load still held. `work.tookUp` for sinking and tunnelling cites the plate and the shaft's plan line. A vein tile is taken up whether or not it was shown. What digging gives lies on the free tiles round the dug one (`layYield`), and what finds none on the dug tile itself: nothing vanishes. Clay: the plan part's material that a terrain kind digs, from the tiles `digsFor` (build) allows, the rule a house counts clay by. Every vein's `yields` must be a `work.haul.kinds` entry (content check).
- **Invariants:** at most one live (not `sent` or `spent`) shaft per metal; `depth` never above `to`; the save check holds each shaft row to a known state, `depth` <= `to` <= the level count.
- **Tests:** `mine.test.ts` (seams planted by `plantSeam`): siting beside a seam with the reason; iron ore in the store with only the plate raised; send back once and the refusals; no seam, no plan; a spent shaft and the next seam; a ladder from stacks of one; `mine.noLadder` once; a vein dark until known, tunnels on known ground; clay; determinism. `search.test.ts`: a vein dark until a search tunnel passes beside it. `land/mining.test.ts`: ore never lost, a held ladder laid down, an unseen open tile shown not dug.
- **Salt (`salt.ts`):** with a smokehouse, no salt in a store and `cure.salt.want` fresh meat in the stores, a found white `salt` seam gets a shaft like a metal, citing the curer's take-up or the smokehouse. A cellar's stair is a cellar-kind `shaft` (`sendBackShaft` refuses it).
