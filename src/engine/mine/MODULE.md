# mine

Shafts sited by an ore stain when a metal is wanted (P4.1).

- **Public API:** `index.ts`: `mineSystem` (hourly, no RNG stream), `MINE_TABLES`, `shaftsOf(world)`, `shaftFor(world, metal)` (the live shaft of a metal), `metalsOf(content)` (vein features whose `${id}-ingot` has a Stock plate), `oreShort(world, content, metal)` (ore still to find: goods `ingotWant` times `work.mine.perIngot` less the ore held anywhere), `nextDig(world, content, foot, metal, taken)` (the next tile to dig on the vein level and the open tile to dig it from).
- **Planning:** once a day and when an ingot plate changes, for each metal with `oreShort` > 0, no live shaft and no known vein tile the band can already reach (`site.ts` `openVein`), `site.ts` takes the nearest tile (larger of its two offsets from the green, then tile index) whose `map.stains` is that metal's, past the town ring (build `ringOf`), on ground walking at the plain multiplier, with no feature, plan or building, no shaft, at least `AVOID` (6) tiles from a plan sent back, and reachable from the green. `mine.shaftPlanned` cites the plate's `goods.stockSet` and carries a `why` (wanted ingots, the nearest stain and its colour word from `work.mine.stains`, tiles and direction). No tile: `mine.noStain` once per metal per plate change.
- **Lever:** `sendBackShaft {shaft}` (normal; V-041): a shaft still `planned` and not itself a replacement; the plan goes to `sent`, `mine.shaftSentBack`, and the colony plans again elsewhere with `sentBack` 1. Otherwise `command.refused` with a reason.
- **Tunnelling:** `tunnel.ts` `nextDig` is a Dijkstra over the vein level from the shaft foot: open ground costs 1, a rock or earth tile `work.mine.dig` minutes, a vein tile `vein`; ties go to the lower tile. The way runs through open ground first and the first tile on it that is not open is dug next.
- **Events:** emits `mine.shaftPlanned`, `mine.shaftSentBack`, `mine.noStain`; the digging lines (`mine.sank`, `mine.dug`, `mine.veinMined`, `mine.clayDug`) are the people's (`src/engine/people/mine.ts`). Reads `goods.stockSet` lines by plate.
- **State owned:** table `shaft` (x, y, metal, to, state `planned` / `sinking` / `done` / `sent`, depth, sentBack, line); system state `sig`, `day` (the last look) and `no-<metal>` (the plate line a `mine.noStain` was said for). Writes no tile: sinking and digging go through map `sinkShaftAt`, `setTile` and `openAround`.
- **Invariants:** at most one live (not `sent`) shaft per metal; `depth` never above `to`.
- **Tests:** `mine.test.ts`: siting past the ring on plain ground with the reason; iron ore in the store with only the plate raised, each line tracing to it; send back once and the refusal; `mine.noStain` once; clay dug and hauled up; determinism.
