# annals

Season and year recaps, each sentence citing its lines (V-191).

- **Public API:** `index.ts`: `annalsSystem`, `annalsOf(world)` (every recap, newest first), `recapOf(world, annal)` (its paragraphs, sentences joined by section).
- **Content:** `annals.json` (schema `schemas-annals.ts`, checked by `validate-annals.ts`): `sections` in order (came, lost, family, built, troubles, leads), each with the line kinds it tells (`event`, `only`, `one` (the ledger's words when left out), `many` and `item` for two or more, `weight`, `title`, `tally` and `per`) and `max` kinds; `skip` (kept lines no section tells: the turn, moods); `titles`, `still` (the elder when no line says who leads), `numbers`, `say`, `year`; and the words for counts, `times`, `repeat`, `listMax`, `more`. Every line of importance 3 or notable must be in a section or in `skip`.
- **Keeping (`collect.ts`, each midnight):** each line written before this tick that is of importance 3 or notable, not skipped and told by a section, becomes an `annalLine` row with its data as JSON, its actors, year and season. The season line that opens a season (and a year) is noted in state.
- **A season's recap (`season.ts`, at the midnight a season turns):** the season's rows grouped by kind; each section tells its `max` weightiest kinds in the order they first happened, a kind of two or more lines as one `many` sentence ("Two tools broke: …"), the same sentence once. Then who leads (`still`, from the colony's elder, when no line said), the numbers at the season's end (people, houses, days of food, levels open) and what people say: up to `say.max` tales, those of this season first, each as the holder who tells it most bent. The title is the weightiest titled kind's (`titles.quiet` when none).
- **A year's recap (`year.ts`, after winter's):** each season's title and its `perSeason` weightiest sentences; the year's tallies (came, died, born, houses); what people say; whose year it was (the person named in the most kept lines, the first on a tie). Then the year's `annalLine` rows go.
- **Events:** none written; reads every line of the day.
- **State owned:** tables `annalLine` (event, type, words, actors, year, season), `annal` (kind, year, season, tick, title, head, line) and `annalText` (annal, section, text, lines, weight); state `seen` (the last line read), `opened` and `yearOpened` (season lines, refs).
- **Invariants:** draws no dice and changes nothing another system reads, so the ledger is the same with or without it (measured on anvil's year 1, 2026-11-20); recaps are written whether or not they are shown. Every sentence cites at least one line, and `annalLine` and `annalText` are held tables (`sim/step.ts` `HELD`), so the lines they cite keep their words past the sealed week (V-177). Whole numbers only.
- **Tests:** `src/worker/annals.test.ts` (a short year of planted lines: titles, a joined sentence, the year's paragraphs and numbers, every cited line whole after the seal, a save round trip); `src/engine/engine.test.ts` (the determinism test covers the recap table). The four seeds' recaps: `npm run annals`.
