# tales

Tales and beliefs (P6.3, V-140, V-155, V-162): events become tales that pass and bend as told; the version held forms a belief; songs and bynames (6B).

- **Public API:** `index.ts`: `talesSystem`, `TALES_TABLES`, `storiesOf(world)`, `talesOf(world, person)` (newest story first), `beliefsOf(world, person)` (strongest first), `taleDef(content, id)`, `taleText(content, story, level)` (a story's words at a level, filled from the event's data), `feedBelief`.
- **Content:** `tales.json`: `tales` (each copies one ledger `event`, with `only` data values; `levels` of words mildest to worst; `truth` the level of what happened; `belief` formed at level `from` or worse; `word` when no one sees it), `beliefs` (id, about person / place / law / colony, text), `see`, `pass`, `bend`, `beliefFadeDays`, `forgetDays`, `strength` (the words for 1 to 4), `evening.mend` (people `evening.ts`). `validate-tales.ts` checks events, beliefs and levels.
- **Seeing (`learn.ts`, every minute):** each new ledger line a tale copies becomes a `story` (its data kept as words, so it outlives the sealed line). Its living actors and the awake who see one (needs `sees`) hold it at `truth`, citing the line; of a `word` tale one awake person (the `social` dice) brings word.
- **Telling (`tell.ts`, half past each hour):** each pair of awake people may pass one tale at `pass` per cent: both eating within `life.relax.reach` (`meal`), both relaxing within reach (`leisure`), both working within 2 tiles (`work`), or both by a lit fire on a winter evening within life.json `sight` (`hearth`, people `byAFire`), or both relaxing inside the tavern (`tavern`, engine inn, 6B). The teller tells the newest story the other lacks, while the listener has heard under `perDay` today (the innkeeper `tavern.keeper.hears`). A telling bends one level at `bend.chance` per cent plus the teller's traits (plus `tavern.bend` after a cup tonight), down `softer` per cent of the time. Each writes `tale.told` (teller, listener, the version told, "It grew/shrank in the telling.") citing the teller's own line, so "why?" walks any version back to the event.
- **Beliefs (`belief.ts`):** a version at `from` or worse feeds the tale's belief: 2 steps seen, 1 heard (a calling holder's `told` push when they told it, the lorekeeper), up to 4. Daily, one unfed for `beliefFadeDays` drops a step and goes at 0 (`fade.by` per cent of the days while someone else holds a calling with a `fade` push for that belief, the champion). Beliefs change nothing yet (Bundles 3 to 6 read them).
- **Forgetting (daily):** a tale heard and neither told nor heard for `forgetDays` goes (`keep` per cent as long when its teller holds a calling with a `keep` push); one seen stays; the tales of the dead (status `dead`, `buried`) go (6A review P6); a story nobody holds goes.
- **Songs (`songs.ts`, 6B):** the bard (`highestOn` the Bard row) spends a free evening making (`tavern.bard.evenings`, one a season, a tale with `song` titles, one picked by seed and story in `titles.ts`, one level up: `song.made`) or singing the least lately sung, at the tavern or a lit fire before one stands (`song.sung`): hearers hold its version, `bard.heard` by singing level; a lazy `singing` skill row. Sung within `forgetDays`, never forgotten.
- **Bynames (`bynames.ts`, 6B):** daily, a tale about someone (story `about`) told `byname.told` times (a song counts once a hearer), held by over `byname.share` per cent of grown-ups: the most-held version's `bynames` word, else while a living person holds that word the level's next `spares` word, else none (one living holder a word, V-206), one for life (`tale.byname` citing the latest telling; a song can give one); `byname-proud`/`-stung`, felt again on hearing it.
- **Events:** `tale.told` (teller and listener as actors), `song.made`, `song.sung`, `tale.byname`.
- **State owned:** tables `story` (tale, event, words, about, told), `song`, `byname`, `tale` (person, story, level, teller (0: saw it), line, heard, day) and `belief` (person, belief, strength, line, fed); state `seen`, the last ledger id read, and `sang` (the evening the bard last sang or made).
- **Invariants:** reads people's places, `doing` and traits and the ledger; writes only its own tables. Whole numbers only.
- **Tests:** `tales.test.ts` (one rumour across three colonists, worse at each forced bend, each line citing the last, beliefs 2 / 1 / 1; nobody told what they hold); people `evening.test.ts` (the idle sit by a lit fire on a winter evening and mend).
