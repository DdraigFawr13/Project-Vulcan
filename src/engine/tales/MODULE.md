# tales

Tales and beliefs (P6.3, V-140, V-155, V-162): an event becomes a tale, a tale passes from person to person and can bend at each telling, and the version held forms a belief.

- **Public API:** `index.ts`: `talesSystem`, `TALES_TABLES`, `storiesOf(world)`, `talesOf(world, person)` (newest story first), `beliefsOf(world, person)` (strongest first), `taleDef(content, id)`, `taleText(content, story, level)` (a story's words at a level, filled from the event's data), `feedBelief`.
- **Content:** `tales.json`: `tales` (each copies one ledger `event`, with `only` data values; `levels` of words mildest to worst; `truth` the level of what happened; `belief` formed at level `from` or worse; `word` when no one sees it), `beliefs` (id, about person / place / law / colony, text), `see`, `pass`, `bend`, `beliefFadeDays`, `forgetDays`, `strength` (the words for 1 to 4), `evening.mend` (people `evening.ts`). `validate-tales.ts` checks events, beliefs and levels.
- **Seeing (`learn.ts`, every minute):** each new ledger line a tale copies becomes a `story` (its data kept as words, so it outlives the sealed line). Its living actors and the awake on the same level within `see` tiles of one hold it at `truth`, citing the line; of a `word` tale one awake person (the `social` dice) brings word.
- **Telling (`tell.ts`, half past each hour):** each pair of awake people may pass one tale at `pass` per cent: both eating within `life.relax.reach` (`meal`), both relaxing within reach (`leisure`), both working within 2 tiles (`work`), or both by a lit fire on a winter evening within `see` tiles (`hearth`, people `byAFire`). The teller tells the newest story the other lacks, while the listener has heard under `perDay` today. A telling bends one level at `bend.chance` per cent plus the teller's traits, down `softer` per cent of the time. Each writes `tale.told` (teller, listener, the version told, "It grew/shrank in the telling.") citing the teller's own line, so "why?" walks any version back to the event.
- **Beliefs (`belief.ts`):** a version at `from` or worse feeds the tale's belief: 2 steps seen, 1 heard, up to 4. Daily, one unfed for `beliefFadeDays` drops a step and goes at 0. Beliefs change nothing yet (Bundles 3 to 6 read them).
- **Forgetting (daily):** a tale heard and neither told nor heard for `forgetDays` goes; one seen stays; a story nobody holds goes.
- **Events:** `tale.told` (teller and listener as actors).
- **State owned:** tables `story` (tale, event, words), `tale` (person, story, level, teller (0: saw it), line, heard, day) and `belief` (person, belief, strength, line, fed); state `seen`, the last ledger id read.
- **Invariants:** reads people's places, `doing` and traits and the ledger; writes only its own tables. Whole numbers only.
- **Tests:** `tales.test.ts` (one rumour across three colonists, worse at each forced bend, each line citing the last, beliefs 2 / 1 / 1; nobody told what they hold); people `evening.test.ts` (the idle sit by a lit fire on a winter evening and mend).
