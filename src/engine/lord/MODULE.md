# lord

The lord, his reeve and the tithe (6C, V-216, V-222).

- **Public API:** `index.ts`: `lordSystem` (every minute, stream `lord`), `lordRow(world)` and `reeveRow(world)` (undefined before the first visit, or while no reeve is here), `lordName(row)`; `charterOf(world)` (the chartered name, line, tick); `tables.ts` `titleOf`; `tithe.ts` `rateOf(ctx)` (the share in 100); read-only for the page: `favourAt`, `favourCauses`, `shareIn`, `rateIn`.
- **Content:** `lord.json` (`titles` by the word a realm's name gives its kind, `title` for any other; `reeve`: sex, `pace` (he rides), `arrive`, `speakBy`, `leave` hours, `reach` tiles, `look` minutes; `tempers` extra weight on the news's memory; `memories` named and bitter); `memories.json` `lord-named`, `lord-bitter`; temper words from the package (region `tempersOf`).
- **Who the lord is (region `lordOf`, `lord.ts`):** the ruler (`Civ.rulerId`) of the realm of the nearest `valley.near` neighbour, by days, whose realm has not fallen (`fell` unset) and whose ruler lives; else the nearest such realm among the region's sites; else the first in the world. A child is under their race's `maturityYears`. A plain seed has no region and no lord (state `none`).
- **The visit (`reeve.ts`):** the morning after each eaten harvest-home supper (a `feast` row with no couple and no feast day, after state `done`), from `arrive` until `speakBy`, the reeve comes in at the map's edge on the lord's side (band `edgeSpot`). The first time, the lord is copied into a `lord` row and the reeve named from the lord's people (a name nobody here has). He rides to the elder, else whoever living is nearest the hall (else the green), looking every `look` minutes, and speaks within `reach` tiles, or at `speakBy` wherever they are; then rides back to the edge, gone by `leave`. His steps reveal nothing.
- **What he says:** the first time `lord.named` (in the lord's name, or "for the young ... and those who rule for him"; the temper's words; the realm; a share owed from the next harvest home), and each of the living gets `memories.named`, or `memories.bitter` if not of the lord's people, its weight raised by `tempers[temper]`. Later visits `lord.counted`, then the tithe.
- **The tithe (`tithe.ts`, Slice 2, V-222):** the first visit sets the Lord law (`tithe.law`) Loyal citing `lord.named` (law `decree`), so everyone answers it. From then goods counts what is brought in (`broughtIn`: harvests less seed, shearing, weaving, smelting, a smith's iron things; `tithe.kinds`). Hourly `setDue`: each kind's share (`tithe.rate`, or `tempers`) times the setting's `tithe` per cent is due, less what was carried; under Defiant only the grown-ups whose Loyal will (law `weigh`) is at `obey` carry their share (`by`). Later visits `lord.tithed`: owed, in the barn, short, taken; what he leaves counts towards the next share. In a lean year (under `lean.days` days' eating in store) a `lean.half` temper takes half; a lean take is a colony standing cause (`leanTithe`, tale `lean-tithe`) and the `lean.memory` for every grown-up. One visit in `search` (a `lord` stream draw) he searches: each stash from the barn still held (goods `hidden`) is found and taken, `lord.found` naming what, whose and where, then `lord.searched`.
- **Favour (`favour.ts`, V-222):** table `favour` (cause, by, text, line, until), 0 to 100 from `favour.start`, each cause `days` long. At a visit: Defiant, else full (owed, none short) or `tenth` per tenth short (naming owed and counted); `fine` if a fine was owed and not paid; `found` a stash; `told` (`poachTold`) for each poaching seen by someone Loyal. Then `climb`: each `ladder` step reached is `lord.step` once: `raised` adds `by` to the share while it holds, `fine`/`free` at the next visit (`shareOf`), `men` at once (`men.ts`: `by` more of owed from barn then store, `lord.men`, a light wound on one grown-up who refuses the Lord law; no child), `forest` grants it; `charter` (`charter.ts`, V-242) needs `charter.least` living, year over `charter.years`, names the valley in the founders' tongue (`lord.charter`) and stays. The forest (`forest.ts`): the `forest.place` find farthest from the green (`fx`, `fy`); defy `poach.ts` keeps it.
- **Barn and hauling:** build `tithebarn.ts` sites the barn once the lord is `named` (Build row `tithe.barn`, `siteHall`, `inn` INN_TITHE, no furniture; `barnSpot`). goods `tithe.ts` keeps rows `tithe` (kind, brought, due, set, barn, by, going, lines) and `hidden` stashes; the barn's goods are counts, never items, so nobody eats them. band `tithehaul.ts`: a free hauler `by` allows takes the nearest free stack of a due kind as a `tithe` load into the barn (`lord.barned` once a kind is in; `lord.carried` under Defiant); no barn, it is the colony's again.
- **Asks:** `asks.ts` (asks MODULE.md).
- **His own visit (Slice 7, V-240):** `visit.ts`: every `visit.every`th harvest home he rides in with the reeve as the supper is called (`lord.came`); once eaten he lays the Feasts law's `table` from the store (`lord.supper`; causes `table-<setting>`, `pleased`/`displeased` by `pleases`); `lord.prize` to his contest's winner; `hothead.ts` (`lord.hothead`, `lord.beaten`); next morning the reeve speaks, then `lord.rodeOut`. `petition.ts`: in a lean year the elder pleads (`lord.plea`), once in `petition.every` years.
- **Events:** the `lord.*` lines above and `lord.reeveCame`, `lord.counted`, `lord.reeveLeft`, each citing the harvest-home line and the eaten supper or his coming; reads `farm.harvestHome` via the feast row.
- **State owned:** tables `lord` (one row, fixed for the run; the region is not saved, so this copy is what a save carries; `tithed`, `tookAt`, `took`: his last take, kept past the sealed day for Stock) and `reeve` (while he is in the valley); state `done`, `none`.
- **Invariants:** no lever touches him (V-041); the lord never changes once copied; draws only from the `lord` stream.
- **Tests:** `visit.test.ts` (visit, petitions, hotheads); `lord.test.ts` (smoke/1: who he is, a save, a fallen realm, the Lord law, barn, hiding, carrying, favour, fine, men, forest, charter).
