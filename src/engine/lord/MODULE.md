# lord

The lord, his reeve and the tithe (6C, V-216, V-222).

- **Public API:** `index.ts`: `lordSystem` (every minute, stream `lord`), `lordRow(world)` and `reeveRow(world)` (undefined before the first visit, and while no reeve is in the valley), `lordName(row)` ("Jarl Gunnil the Builder", "Warlord Ogrgul"); `tables.ts` `titleOf`; `tithe.ts` `rateOf(ctx)` (the share in 100); read-only for the page: `favourAt`, `favourCauses`, `shareIn`, `rateIn`.
- **Content:** `lord.json` (`titles` by the word a realm's name gives its kind, `title` for any other; `reeve`: sex, `pace` (he rides), `arrive`, `speakBy`, `leave` hours, `reach` tiles, `look` minutes; `tempers` extra weight on the news's memory; `memories` named and bitter); `memories.json` `lord-named`, `lord-bitter`; temper words from the package (region `tempersOf`).
- **Who the lord is (region `lordOf`, `lord.ts`):** the ruler (`Civ.rulerId`) of the realm of the nearest `valley.near` neighbour, by days, whose realm has not fallen (`fell` unset) and whose ruler lives; else the nearest such realm among the region's sites; else the first in the world. Age is `region.world.years` less their birth year; a child is under their race's `maturityYears`. A plain seed has no region and no lord (state `none`).
- **The visit (`reeve.ts`):** the morning after each eaten harvest-home supper (a `feast` row with no couple and no feast day, after state `done`), from `arrive` until `speakBy`, the reeve comes in at the map's edge on the lord's side (band `edgeSpot`). The first time, the lord is copied into a `lord` row and the reeve named from the lord's people (no name a colonist or the lord bears). He rides to the elder, else whoever living is nearest the hall (else the green), looking again every `look` minutes, and speaks within `reach` tiles, or at `speakBy` wherever they are. Then he rides back to the edge and goes, or is gone at `leave`. His steps reveal nothing (band `stepAlong(..., false)`).
- **What he says:** the first time `lord.named` (in the lord's name, or "for the young ... and those who rule for him"; a lord with no byname by name and the temper's words; the realm; a share owed from the next harvest home), and each of the living gets `memories.named`, or `memories.bitter` if not of the lord's people, its weight raised by `tempers[temper]`. Later visits `lord.counted` (the store's food and days for the living), then the tithe.
- **The tithe (`tithe.ts`, Slice 2, V-222):** the first visit sets the Lord law (`tithe.law`) Loyal citing `lord.named` (law `decree`), so everyone answers it. From then goods counts what is brought in (`broughtIn`: harvests less seed, shearing, weaving, smelting, a smith's iron things; `tithe.kinds`). Hourly `setDue`: each kind's share (`tithe.rate`, or `tempers`) times the setting's `tithe` per cent is due, less what was carried; under Defiant only the grown-ups whose Loyal will (law `weigh`) is at `obey` carry their share (`by`). Later visits `lord.tithed`: owed (all brought in since his last visit × rate), what is in the barn, what is short, what he took; what he leaves counts towards the next share. In a lean year (under `lean.days` days' eating in store) a `lean.half` temper takes half; a lean take is a colony standing cause (`leanTithe`, tale `lean-tithe`) and the `lean.memory` for every grown-up. One visit in `search` (a `lord` stream draw) he searches: each stash from the barn still held (goods `hidden`) is found and taken, `lord.found` naming what, whose and where, then `lord.searched`.
- **Favour (`favour.ts`, V-222):** table `favour` (cause, by, text, line, until), 0 to 100 from `favour.start`, each cause `days` long. At a visit: Defiant, else full (owed, none short) or `tenth` per tenth short (naming owed and counted); `fine` if a fine was owed and not paid; `found` a stash; `told` (`poachTold`) for each poaching seen by someone Loyal. Then `climb`: each `ladder` step reached is `lord.step` once: `raised` adds `by` to the share while it holds, `fine`/`free` at the next visit (`shareOf`), `men` at once (`men.ts`: `by` more of owed from barn then store, `lord.men`, a light wound on one grown-up who refuses the Lord law; no child), `forest` grants it. The forest (`forest.ts`): the `forest.place` find farthest from the green (`fx`, `fy`); defy `poach.ts` keeps it.
- **Barn and hauling:** build `tithebarn.ts` sites the barn once the lord is `named` (Build row `tithe.barn`, `siteHall`, `inn` INN_TITHE, no furniture; `barnSpot`). goods `tithe.ts` keeps rows `tithe` (kind, brought, due, set, barn, by, going, lines) and `hidden` stashes; the barn's goods are counts, never items, so nobody eats them. band `tithehaul.ts`: a free hauler `by` allows takes the nearest free stack of a due kind as a `tithe` load into the barn (`lord.barned` once a kind is in; `lord.carried` under Defiant); no barn, it is the colony's again.
- **Asks:** `asks.ts` (asks MODULE.md).
- **Events:** emits `lord.reeveCame`, `lord.named`, `lord.counted`, `lord.tithed`, `lord.searched`, `lord.found`, `lord.favour`, `lord.step`, `lord.men`, `lord.told`, `lord.reeveLeft`, each citing the harvest-home line and the eaten supper or his coming; reads `farm.harvestHome` through the feast row.
- **State owned:** tables `lord` (one row, fixed for the run; the region is not saved, so this copy is what a save carries; `tithed`, `tookAt`, `took`: his last take, kept past the sealed day for Stock) and `reeve` (while he is in the valley); state `done`, `none`.
- **Invariants:** no lever touches him (V-041); the lord never changes once copied; draws only from the `lord` stream (the reeve's name, the search, the men).
- **Tests:** `lord.test.ts` (smoke/1: the lord is the nearest live realm's ruler and the reeve's lines follow the first harvest home and cite it; a save carries the lord; a fallen realm is skipped; the Lord law is Loyal on his word; the barn counted against the share; hiding; the share carried unordered; favour for a shortfall; the fine step; the lord's men; the forest).
