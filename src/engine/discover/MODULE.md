# discover

New ways of working, found from hints (6C Slice 5, V-218).

- **Public API:** `index.ts`: `discoverSystem` (hourly, after `lordSystem`, stream `discover`), `puzzleOf(world, person)`, `hintsOf(world, puzzle)`, `knows`, `knowsOf`, `knowersOf` (the living), `methodDef`, `waysOf`, `trying`, `NEW_WAYS`.
- **Content:** `methods.json` (schema `schemas-methods.ts`): the twelve `methods` (trade `job`, `skill` and `from`, `hints`, `where`, `failures`), `start`, `work`, `credit`, `failure`, `drop`, `book`, `seen`, `tale`, `luck`, `dream`, `memories`, `mishap`, `spread`, `kinds` (each hint's words), `failed`; events `discover.*`; memory `found-way`; tale `found-way` and belief `valley-finders`.
- **Puzzles (`trade.ts`):** hourly, each living person at `work` at a way's trade adds an hour to a 28-day ring (table `trade`); a `credit` line (milling, healing) adds its hours. The `trade` row keeps the line of the latest work there, which puzzle, work and luck lines cite. Daily at midnight someone with no puzzle whose most-worked trade has `start.hours` in the window begins on its first way they have the skill for, don't know and no living colonist knows (`discover.puzzle`). One puzzle a person; a puzzle whose window has held under `drop.hours` at its trade for `drop.days` running is put aside (`discover.dropped`), and a dead person's ends.
- **Hints:** work, every `work` hours at the trade; failure (`reads.ts`), a line in the way's `failures`, to a puzzler named in it (`failed` `by` `actor`) or (`trade`) whoever puzzling there worked the trade today and most this month, once a person in `failure.days`; seen, a `seen.event` line (a stranger's coming), 1 in `seen.odds` each puzzler, at most `seen.most` a way; book, a `book.event` line (a book read) of the puzzle's skill, once in `book.days`; tale, a newly held `found-way` tale of another's find in the same trade, 1 in `tale.odds`; luck (`night.ts`), at midnight after `luck.hours` at the trade that day, 1 in `luck.days` (`luck.lucky` with a byname in `luck.bynames`); dream, the last hint, 1 in `dream.nights` once `dream.days` passed since the last. Each is `discover.hint` citing its source and the puzzle's line.
- **The find (`find.ts`):** at the last hint, `discover.found` cites every hint and lists their words; a `knows` row, the `found-way` memory, the tale (and so maybe a byname); every other puzzle on that way ends with `discover.beaten`.
- **New Ways (`ways.ts`):** law `new-ways` in `laws.json` (Old ways only, Allowed the custom, Encouraged), each setting's `ways`: `start` (puzzles begin), `luck`, `work` hours a hint, 1 in `mishap` work hints going wrong, 1 working minute in `trying` spent trying (people `mind.ts` asks `trying`); a refuser keeps the custom. Answers are the law system's.
- **Mishaps (`mishap.ts`):** a work hint gone wrong: at a `hot` trade a burned hand (health `wound`, `mishap.burn`) at `mishap.hand` per cent or a small fire at `mishap.fire` (it burns one of the `ruins` and lights nothing on the map), else spoiled materials (one of the way's `ruins` taken from a store); `discover.mishap` citing the hint, tale `trying-mishap`, memory `mishap`. Nobody dies.
- **Spreading and loss (`spread.ts`):** hourly, someone at a way's trade gains an hour (table `learn`) while apprenticed to a knower in its skill (`spread.apprentice` hours) or on a day a knower worked it (`spread.beside`); a book by a knower teaches every way of its skill it holds. `discover.learned` (citing the learner's work and the bond or the knower's line, or the book), `learned-way`, their own puzzle on it ends. With no living knower and no kept book by one, `discover.lost` cites the last one's death or leaving (state `k.<way>`).
- **State owned:** tables `trade`, `puzzle`, `hint`, `knows`, `learn`; state `seen` (ledger) and `tales` (tale rows read).
- **Invariants:** no lever names a puzzler (V-041): the Work sheet's priorities decide who works a trade. Reads people rows, skills, bynames, tales and the ledger; writes its tables and memories.
- **Tests:** `discover.test.ts` (a smith finds steel with a line citing each hint; a lucky night's line; no puzzle over a way a living colonist knows; a puzzle left a month is put aside); `ways.test.ts` (a mishap's line; New Ways answers and Old ways only; learning beside a knower; the loss line on the last knower's death).
- **Effects (V-219):** each way's `effect.by` is read where its trade works (`entity/ways.ts`): steel (`goods/tools.ts` `layTool`, `band/tools.ts`), cleaner-smelt (`crafts/smelt.ts`), heavy-plough and resting (`land/farm.ts`, `build/soillife.ts`), mill-wheel, treadle-loom, bark-tanning, clamp, glazed-pots (`crafts/`, `keep/spoil.ts`), lime-mortar (`build/plans.ts`, `fire/air.ts`), remedies (`health/heal.ts`), skeps (`bees/winter.ts`); `effects.test.ts`.
