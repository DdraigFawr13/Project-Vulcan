# worker

The Web Worker that owns the engine, runs the tick loop and answers the main thread.

- **Public API:** `index.ts`: `spawnSimWorker()`, `TICKS_PER_SECOND`, `SAVE_VERSION`, and the types `ToWorker`, `FromWorker`, `Speed`, `ClockView`, `ChunkView`, `LedgerLine`, `TileView` (`protocol.ts`; DECISIONS V-002; ARCHITECTURE "Worker protocol") and `SaveFile` (`save.ts`). `worker.ts` is the worker's entry; `pace.ts` the tick clock; `save.ts` builds and checks saves.
- **Messages in:** `init {seed, contentHash}` (a content mismatch stops the worker with an error naming both hashes; init and `load` both end in `adopt`: paused, everything resent), `setSpeed {speed}` (0 paused, 1 to 3), `command {tick, kind, data}`, `requestSnapshot`, `load {save}` (checked for format version, content hash and shape before anything is replaced; a refused save posts `loadRefused` and the running world carries on; an accepted one ends in `adopt`, like `init`), `save {auto}`, `export {kind: 'ndjson'}`, `inspect {x, y, z}` (z defaults to the surface), `why {id}`. Save, export, inspect and why only read the world between ticks: they never step it, write a ledger line or draw from an RNG stream.
- **Messages out:** `ready {from: 'seed' | 'save', seed, contentHash, width, height, depth}`; on each request, `events {batch}` (new ledger lines as `LedgerLine`s with their text, clock and `why`, false when the line cites nothing or only `world.begun`) then `snapshot {tick, speed, clock, chunks, reached}` (`reached`: which levels below have tiles); `saved {auto, save, clock}`; `loadRefused {reason}`; `exported {kind, name, text}`; `inspected {tile}` (`TileView`: its level, ground, and on the surface biome, height and fertility; what is on it or the vein in it with its yield; what lies straight below; its last-changed line; undefined off the map or on an unreached level); `causes {id, lines}` (`whyOf`, nearest first, with depth); `error {message}`.
- **Tick scheduler:** wakes every 10 ms but trusts `performance.now()`, not the timer: each wake runs the whole ticks owed for the real time elapsed at the current speed (10, 30, 100 a second), carrying the fraction. At most 250 ms is caught up after a stall; the rest is dropped. Pausing drops the fraction, so the tick stops dead.
- **Snapshots:** pulled, never pushed: the page asks once per frame with at most one request in flight. Chunks of every reached level go out when their chunk version differs from the last one sent (all of a level's the first time, including when it is first reached), as transferred `Uint8Array`s of ground and features, each naming its level `z`.
- **Rules:** the only place the engine is instantiated. Speed and pause are worker settings, never game commands. Any throw stops the loop and posts `error`; the page shows it and offers a reload.
- **Tests:** `pace.test.ts` (speeds, pause, catch-up cap); `save.test.ts` (a save survives a structured clone whole; other content, another format or a damaged save is refused); the browser smoke (pause stops the tick, 3x runs, save and load).
