# worker

The Web Worker that owns the engine, runs the tick loop and answers the main thread.

- **Public API:** `index.ts`: `spawnSimWorker()`, `TICKS_PER_SECOND`, `SAVE_VERSION`, and the types `ToWorker`, `FromWorker`, `Speed`, `ClockView`, `ChunkView`, `LedgerLine`, `TileView` (`protocol.ts`; DECISIONS V-002; ARCHITECTURE "Worker protocol") and `SaveFile` (`save.ts`). `worker.ts` is the worker's entry; `pace.ts` the tick clock; `save.ts` builds and checks saves; `views.ts` the inspector's clock, weather and tile views; `people.ts` the `PersonView`s (P2.1); `timing.ts` the per-system tick timing.
- **Messages in:** `init {seed, contentHash}` (a plain seed rolls its region and makes the three valley worlds, `<seed>/1` to `/3`, then posts `picker`; a valley seed begins at once; one naming a valley its region lacks opens that region's picker), `settle {site}` (adopts that valley's world as made), `region` (answers `region` with the running world's region, none for a plain-seed world), (a content mismatch stops the worker with an error naming both hashes; init and `load` both end in `adopt`: paused, everything resent), `setSpeed {speed}` (0 paused, 1 to 3), `skip`, `command {tick, kind, data}`, `requestSnapshot`, `load {save}` (checked for format version, content hash and shape before anything is replaced; a refused save posts `loadRefused` and the running world carries on; an accepted one ends in `adopt`, like `init`), `save {auto, standby?}` (both echoed in `saved`), `export {kind: 'ndjson'}`, `inspect {x, y, z}` (z defaults to the surface), `why {id}`. Save, export, inspect and why only read the world between ticks: they never step it, write a ledger line or draw from an RNG stream.
- **Ledger and saves (P2.0):** `why` and the inspector's last change read `recordOf` and `whyOf`, so a line whose day is sealed comes back as a `LedgerLine` with `older: true` and the words "An older record, from Day … of …, year …", never its type id; after each run the worker posts the days sealed meanwhile as `segments {items: {key, text}[]}` and empties `world.sealed`, so a save never carries them (`adopt` does the same for a world that arrives with some); `exported` carries the archived days' keys and the recent days' NDJSON for the page to join. Saves are format 2: they carry `stubs`, `segments`, `sealed`, `entities` and `nextEntityId`, and `checkSave` checks the entity tables against `sim.tables` (`tablesProblem`). A format 1 save is refused, so Continue starts afresh once (GAME-05 override).
- **Timing and skip (P2.0):** the simulation is built from the game's systems with each `run` wrapped in a timer (`timing.ts`); the worker runs one tick at a time, timing each, and every 1,000 ticks works out the mean, the 95th percentile and the slowest system, sent as each snapshot's `timing`. Skip ahead (P2.7): `skip` sets `pace.skip`, which runs ticks one at a time for `SKIP_MS` (8 ms) each wake instead of at the speed's rate, until a tick writes a notable line (`sim.notable`); then `stopSkip` leaves it paused. `setSpeed` (pause included) ends a skip at once. A lever sent meanwhile is stamped for the next tick, as at 3x. On a throw, `error` also carries the tick and the command log, for the page's rescue save.
- **Messages out:** `ready {from: 'seed' | 'save', seed, contentHash, width, height, depth, place, fresh, people}` (P2.1: `place` is the valley's name from the region's `colony` row, so no region is rolled again; `fresh` is true at the world's first tick; `people` are `PersonView`s: name, race, culture, age, home and how far, trade, skills, best three, which of building and cooking they can do, traits with their meaning, look and position, and their arrival line); on each request, `events {batch}` (new ledger lines as `LedgerLine`s with their text, clock and `why`, false when the line cites nothing or only `world.begun`; a notable line has `notable` and, when about a person, `where`: their spot as it is sent and `placeWords` from the camp) then `snapshot {tick, speed, skipping, day, clock, chunks, reached, walkers}` (`reached`: which levels below have tiles; `walkers`: everyone's `{id, x, y, z}` this tick, P2.3, with their needs, `skills` (level, potential, practice %) and `work` (each priority kind's level, word and any decline line, P2.5); `day`: the colony's day); `saved {auto, save, clock}`; `loadRefused {reason}`; `exported {kind, name, text}`; `inspected {tile}` (`TileView`: its level, ground, and on the surface biome, height and fertility; what is on it or the vein in it with its yield; what lies straight below; its last-changed line; undefined off the map or on an unreached level); `causes {id, lines}` (`whyOf`, nearest first, with depth); `picker {seed, region, valleys}` and `region {region, site}` (`region.ts`: the region less its cells, as `RegionView`, and each valley's `ValleyPreview`: its numbers and a thumbnail's layers, transferred); `error {message}`.
- **Tick scheduler:** wakes every 10 ms but trusts `performance.now()`, not the timer: each wake runs the whole ticks owed for the real time elapsed at the current speed (10, 30, 100 a second), carrying the fraction. At most 250 ms is caught up after a stall; the rest is dropped. Pausing drops the fraction, so the tick stops dead.
- **Weather and aura (P1.4):** snapshots carry `weather {words, temperature}` and `aura` (the spots, as `AuraView`s); chunks carry `ice` (frozen or not, surface only); the inspector's `TileView` gains `temperature`, `ice` (frozen over or open water, and since when, from the ice line the tile cites) and `aura`, worked out by the engine's `tileTemperature`, `isFrozen` and `auraAt`. Saves are checked for the aura, ice and floor fields, known schools and the weather state.
- **Goods (P2.4):** `goods.ts`: snapshots carry `goods` (items lying about by art frame, store tiles, test marks) and `stock` (per good: name, have, want, level and the levels); the inspector's `TileView` gains `goods` (text and the line that made each), `store`, and for test mode `marked`, `tree` and `open`.
- **Snapshots:** pulled, never pushed: the page asks once per frame with at most one request in flight. Chunks of every reached level go out when their chunk version differs from the last one sent (all of a level's the first time, including when it is first reached), as transferred `Uint8Array`s of ground and features, each naming its level `z`.
- **Rules:** the only place the engine is instantiated. Speed and pause are worker settings, never game commands. Any throw stops the loop and posts `error`; the page shows it and offers a reload.
- **Tests:** `pace.test.ts` (speeds, pause, catch-up cap); `save.test.ts` (a save survives a structured clone whole; other content, another format or a damaged save is refused); the browser smoke (pause stops the tick, 3x runs, save and load).
