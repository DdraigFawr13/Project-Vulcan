# worker

The Web Worker that owns the engine, runs the tick loop and answers the main thread.

- **Public API:** `index.ts`: `spawnSimWorker()`, `TICKS_PER_SECOND`, `SAVE_VERSION`, and the types `ToWorker`, `FromWorker`, `Speed`, `ClockView`, `ChunkView`, `LedgerLine`, `TileView` (`protocol.ts`; DECISIONS V-002; ARCHITECTURE "Worker protocol") and `SaveFile` (`save.ts`). `worker.ts` is the worker's entry; `pace.ts` the tick clock; `save.ts` builds and checks saves; `views.ts` the inspector's clock, weather and tile views; `people.ts` the `PersonView`s (P2.1); `health.ts` the words for the hurt and their carers (P5.6); `timing.ts` the per-system tick timing.
- **Messages in:** `init {seed, contentHash}` (a plain seed rolls its region and makes the three valley worlds, `<seed>/1` to `/3`, then posts `picker`; a valley seed begins at once; one naming a valley its region lacks opens that region's picker), `settle {site}` (adopts that valley's world as made), `region` (answers `region` with the running world's region, none for a plain-seed world), (a content mismatch stops the worker with an error naming both hashes; init and `load` both end in `adopt`: paused, everything resent), `setSpeed {speed}` (0 paused, 1 to 3), `skip`, `command {tick, kind, data}`, `requestSnapshot`, `load {save}` (checked for format version, content hash and shape before anything is replaced; a refused save posts `loadRefused` and the running world carries on; an accepted one ends in `adopt`, like `init`), `save {auto, standby?}` (both echoed in `saved`), `export {kind: 'ndjson'}`, `inspect {x, y, z}` (z defaults to the surface), `why {id}`. Save, export, inspect and why only read the world between ticks: they never step it, write a ledger line or draw from an RNG stream.
- **Ledger and saves (P2.0):** `why` and the inspector's last change read `recordOf` and `whyOf`, so a line whose day is sealed comes back as a `LedgerLine` with `older: true` and the words "An older record, from Day … of …, year …", never its type id; after each run the worker posts the days sealed meanwhile as `segments {items: {key, text}[]}` and empties `world.sealed`, so a save never carries them (`adopt` does the same for a world that arrives with some); `exported` carries the archived days' keys and the recent days' NDJSON for the page to join. Saves are format 2: they carry `stubs`, `segments`, `sealed`, `entities` and `nextEntityId`, and `checkSave` checks the entity tables against `sim.tables` (`tablesProblem`). A format 1 save is refused, so Continue starts afresh once (GAME-05 override).
- **Timing and skip (P2.0):** the simulation is built from the game's systems with each `run` wrapped in a timer (`timing.ts`); the worker runs one tick at a time, timing each, and every 1,000 ticks works out the mean, the 95th percentile and the slowest system, sent as each snapshot's `timing`. Skip ahead (P2.7): `skip` sets `pace.skip`, which runs ticks one at a time for `SKIP_MS` (8 ms) each wake instead of at the speed's rate, until a tick writes a notable line (`sim.notable`); then `stopSkip` leaves it paused. `setSpeed` (pause included) ends a skip at once. A lever sent meanwhile is stamped for the next tick, as at 3x. On a throw, `error` also carries the tick and the command log, for the page's rescue save.
- **Messages out:** `ready {from: 'seed' | 'save', seed, contentHash, width, height, depth, place, fresh, people}` (P2.1: `place` is the valley's name from the region's `colony` row, so no region is rolled again; `fresh` is true at the world's first tick; `people` are `PersonView`s: name, race, culture, age, home and how far, trade, skills, best three, which of building and cooking they can do, traits with their meaning, look and position, and their arrival line); on each request, `events {batch}` (new ledger lines as `LedgerLine`s with their text, clock and `why`, false when the line cites nothing or only `world.begun`; a notable line has `notable` and, when about a person, `where`: their spot as it is sent and `placeWords` from the camp) then `snapshot {tick, speed, skipping, day, clock, chunks, reached, walkers}` (`reached`: which levels below have tiles; `walkers`: everyone's `{id, x, y, z}` this tick, P2.3, with their needs, `skills` (level, potential, practice %) and `work` (each priority kind's level, word and any decline line, P2.5); `day`: the colony's day); `saved {auto, save, clock}`; `loadRefused {reason}`; `exported {kind, name, text}`; `inspected {tile}` (`TileView`: its level, ground, and on the surface biome, height and fertility; what is on it or the vein in it with its yield; what lies straight below; its last-changed line; undefined off the map or on an unreached level); `causes {id, lines}` (`whyOf`, nearest first, with depth); `picker {seed, region, valleys}` and `region {region, site}` (`region.ts`: the region less its cells, as `RegionView`, and each valley's `ValleyPreview`: its numbers and a thumbnail's layers, transferred); `error {message}`.
- **Tick scheduler:** wakes every 10 ms but trusts `performance.now()`, not the timer: each wake runs the whole ticks owed for the real time elapsed at the current speed (10, 30, 100 a second), carrying the fraction. At most 250 ms is caught up after a stall; the rest is dropped. Pausing drops the fraction, so the tick stops dead.
- **Weather and aura (P1.4):** snapshots carry `weather {words, temperature}` and `aura` (the spots, as `AuraView`s); chunks carry `ice` (frozen or not, surface only); the inspector's `TileView` gains `temperature`, `ice` (frozen over or open water, and since when, from the ice line the tile cites) and `aura`, worked out by the engine's `tileTemperature`, `isFrozen` and `auraAt`. Saves are checked for the aura, ice and floor fields, known schools and the weather state, and (P4.1) for ore stains naming a feature, `known` marks of 0 or 1 and each shaft row in a known state with `depth` at most `to` and `to` a level there is.
- **Goods (P2.4):** `goods.ts`: snapshots carry `goods` (items lying about by art frame, store tiles, test marks) and `stock` (per good: name, have, want, level and the levels); the inspector's `TileView` gains `goods` (text and the line that made each), `store`, and for test mode `marked`, `tree` and `open`.
- **Houses and rooms (P3.7, P3.3, P3.8):** `build.ts` puts the engine's answers in words (`protocol-build.ts` holds `PlanView`, `HouseView`, `RoomView`, `BuildView`): snapshots carry `build`, sent only when its JSON changes: the green, the Houses want (level and word), the need ("N people sleep under the open sky" or "none now", from `homeless`), a no-ground flag, and a row per house or room (name, size, state, rect, `of`, beds, direction from the green, whether it can be sent back, why, material have and cost). Each plan in `goods.plans` carries its `house`. A tile in a house gains `house` (name, state, why, send-back, roof, the Sited line) and `room` (`roomAt`: tiles, role, beauty, impressiveness of `rooms.top`, beds).
- **Furniture and comfort (P3.4, P3.5):** `furniture.ts` puts the engine's answers in words (`PieceView` in `protocol-build.ts`): `goods.furniture` (art and facing per piece, planned ones flagged; resent with every snapshot's goods, the page redraws only on change); a tile's `piece` (kind, state, quality and maker, a bed's owner, beauty added, rest, privacy, wood and maker while planned, the bench's making, maker and Next from `nextWant`, and the placed, made and claimed lines); and a carpenter's "doing now" text key (`carpentryDoing`, content `work.doing`). A tile's `room` is named "Pedr's bedroom" when every bed in it is one person's. `comfort.ts` gives each Beauty and Privacy `NeedView` a `feeds` word from the room the person stands in and the room they sleep in, worked out once a tick.
- **Fire and progress (P3.6):** `fires.ts` puts the fire engine's answers in words: a hearth piece's art by its fire (`furn.hearth`, `.lit`, `.banked`), `goods.fires` (each lit fire: tile, glow reach in tiles, banked) for the night's firelight, a built hearth's tile rows (`PieceView.fire`: lit, banked or out, its fuel and kind in hours and minutes, what it warms, and its last line with a label), the room's `warmth` row (the felt temperature at the tile, `feltAt`, as a word and degrees, with "no hearth" or the hearth's state), and the "doing now" of tending (the reason from its took-up line), splitting, making a hearth or campfire and warming up by the fire. A tile's temperature is the felt one (weather plus fire heat). `goods.work` is a bar for each plan, part or piece someone is working now (`done` of `total` minutes; the engine counts minutes left, so the worker remembers the largest it has seen, per world and never saved), sent apart from the lists the map redraws on. `jobbars.ts`: bars for timed jobs; `metalstatus.ts`: a plate's line.
- **Shafts (Bundle 2):** `mine.ts` puts the shaft table in words (`ShaftView` in `protocol-build.ts`): `goods.shafts` (planned or sinking shaft tiles, for the map's half-opacity plan), a surface tile's `shaft` (what, why here from the plan's own `why`, work in hours from `work.mine`, who is at it, whether it can still be sent back once, the planning line), the snapshot's `mining` (any shaft not sent back, which shows the Work sheet's Mining row) and the `{ore}` count in an ingot plate's note. A `mine.shaftPlanned` alert carries `where` (the shaft's tile) for Show. Tests: `mine.test.ts`.
- **Wild valley (Phase 5A Bundle 2):** `wild.ts` puts the wild engine in words (`protocol-wild.ts`): the snapshot's `animals` (`AnimalView`: id, art kind, tile, facing, moving; sent only when one differs from the last list, `animalFeed`), `leftovers` (hides, bone, fat, and the game and fish that count as food), the chunks' `snowLie`, `printKind`, `printAge`, `printDir`, a tile's `prints`, `fade` and `snowLies` rows, the `animal` request answered by `animalPage` (a herd's page or the pack's: who, rows, its last two lines) and `goods.middens` (a hook, empty until the people engine lays middens). Tests: `wild.test.ts`.
- **Metal (Bundle 3):** `works.ts`: the smelter and clamp as map pieces (`goods.furniture`) and the tile page of the smelter, clamp or smithy (`TileView.works`: what, state, why here, makes, doing now, lines; `ingotInStore` and `sendBackBlock` back the Build sheet's `BuildView.smithy`). `jobrows.ts`: a Work row appears once its station exists (V-071). `items.ts`: `item {id}` answers `item` with an `ItemView` (made by, held by and why from the week's usage, speed against a plain stone tool, wear, and Made from, the item's `trail` walked back to the ore; an unknown ore tile says so) and each walker's `tools`; a tile's goods carry `item` for tools and ingots.
- **Death and burial (P5.6, P5.8):** `places.ts` puts the engine's answers in words (`PlaceView` in `protocol-build.ts`; words in content `health.words`): `goods.graves` (dug or filled, marker) and `goods.burial` (the ground's rectangle) for the map, a surface tile's `place` (a grave's page or the ground's: who, about, rows, lines), `BuildView.burial` (one Send back until a grave is dug, `back`, `sent`), a sickroom's `HouseView.sick` with its role and beds ("2: Hrakmar (broken leg), free") on the room rows, and `withLying`, which adds `lying` (`down`, `dead`, `buried`) and `carried` to each snapshot's walkers without touching `people.ts`.
- **Health view (P5.6, P5.8):** `healthview.ts` puts the health engine's answers in words from content `health.words` (`HealthView` in `protocol-people.ts`): `WalkerView.health` (none for anyone never hurt) with a state sentence, a strip badge (`hurt`, `dead`) or `buried`, blood (per cent, the faint line, a word), the six parts' status, wounds (title, care and healing in words, cause, line for "why?") and lasting marks; the dead's wounds are their closed ones and the fatal part shows bleeding. `deadNow` is the dead's "doing now" (`work.doing.dead`) citing the death line. Tests: `healthview.test.ts`.
- **Snapshots:** pulled, never pushed: the page asks once per frame with at most one request in flight. Chunks of every reached level go out when their chunk version differs from the last one sent (all of a level's the first time, including when it is first reached), as transferred `Uint8Array`s of ground and features, each naming its level `z`.
- **Rules:** the only place the engine is instantiated. Speed and pause are worker settings, never game commands. Any throw stops the loop and posts `error`; the page shows it and offers a reload.
- **Tests:** `pace.test.ts` (speeds, pause, catch-up cap); `build.test.ts` (the Build view and a house tile's rows); `fires.test.ts` (the hearth's rows and art, the room's warmth row with heat, the doing-now words, the progress values only rising); `furniture.test.ts` (the bench's and a bed's tile rows, the map's pieces, the feeds under Beauty and Privacy, a carpenter's words); `save.test.ts` (a save survives a structured clone whole; other content, another format or a damaged save is refused); the browser smoke (pause stops the tick, 3x runs, save and load).
