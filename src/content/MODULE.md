# content

Game data: the forked Story Generator raws plus Vulcan's own tiles, recipes, jobs and laws, with validators.

- **Public API:** `index.ts`: `content` (all files, typed and validated on load), `contentHash` (14 hex digits; changes when any entry does), `validateContent(files)` (one line per problem), `hashContent(files)`, `rawContent`, type `Content`.
- **Layout:** `data/<file>.json`, one list entry or top-level field per line (exempt from the 400-line cap and from Prettier). `schemas.ts` holds each file's shape, `schema.ts` the small schema language whose specs also give the TypeScript types, `validate.ts` ids, references and tier caps, `hash.ts` the fingerprint.
- **Fork:** `FORK.md` records the Story Generator commit and every change made since. `tools/fork-content.mjs` made the copy once; it refuses to overwrite `data/` without `--force`.
- **Vulcan's own files:** `band.json` (P2.1: the band's size, skills, must-have skills and the `able` level, age, attribute, potential, level and trade dials, traits per person, standing spots, hair styles and colours by race, clothes by culture; schema in `schemas-people.ts`, read by `src/engine/people`), `seasons.json` (P1.4: each month's temperature offset, the chance a spell changes, each forked `weather.json` kind's shift, wet chance and words by temperature, and the ice dials; schema in `schemas-climate.ts`), `region.json` (P1.5, reworked in P1.6: the region grid; the Story Generator world behind it (size, years, window, land share, height scale, edge warp, windows tried, the package's old race ids); mountain, river, range, valley, site and history-line dials; walking days per cell; the glyph for each race and the valley name patterns, by culture where a tongue has its own; schema in `schemas-region.ts`), `events.json` (one ledger template per event type the engine emits, with its importance 1 to 3) and `terrain.json` (ground kinds with their art names and buildable or stone flags, the surface map's size, the floor each biome shows, and the surface generator's dials: climates by weight, elevation, noise, rock shares, grove clumping and the start floors) `features.json` (trees, bushes, herbs and boulders: what each yields, whether it blocks the tile, its density per biome) and `buildings.json` (P3.1: the build job, its skill dial, the share of minutes to take a part down, and each part's kind, material, cost, minutes and art; schema in `schemas-life.ts`, read by `src/engine/build`) are not forked; the fork tool never writes them.
- **Wild (Phase 5 Bundle 2):** `wild.json` (schema `schemas-wild.ts`, read by `src/engine/wild`): game species with their herds, leftovers and breeding, the predators, prints and soft grounds, movement, placement and the pack's hunger dials; items `hide`, `bone`, `fat`, `offal`; `seasons.json` `snowMelt` and `snowShare`.
- **Tools (Bundle 3):** `tools.json` (schema `schemas-tools.ts`): tool kinds, materials, quality bonus, the cart's stone tools (`cartMaterial`), the smith `job`; see `docs/TOOLS_AND_SMITHY.md`.
- **Metal (Bundle 3):** `metal.json` (schema `schemas-metal.ts`, read by goods, build and people): the charcoal clamp (`wood`, logs, yield, build, burn and open minutes, size), the smelter (stone cost, minutes, size, `reach` beyond the ring), `recipes` (copper, tin, iron, bronze) and the trail length. Items carry an optional `ore` (an ingot's ore).
- **Adding content:** add the entry to its JSON file; a new field or file needs its spec in `schemas-world.ts` or `schemas-people.ts` first. Read the schema, not the entries.
- **Fixed in schemas, not data:** the power tier ids, creature tiers (beast, great, legendary) and the tier-cap rule for `magic` abilities; a new tier or family is a schema change.
- **Rules:** pure data, same purity rules as the engine; may import engine types.
- **Invariants:** every file matches its schema; ids are unique in every list; every reference names an existing id; every magic ability's damage, radius and uses fit the caps of the tier its power falls in.
- **Tests:** `content.test.ts`: the fork passes; a broken sample fails with one line per problem; tier caps; the hash changes with any entry.
