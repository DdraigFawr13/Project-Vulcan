# render

Phaser scenes, tilemap layers, sprites and camera. Reads snapshots from the worker and never changes game state.

- **Public API:** `src/render/index.ts`: `startRenderer(parent, {onHover, onSelect})` boots Phaser with the valley scene and returns a `Renderer` (`setMapSize`, `applyChunks`, `zoomStep`, `setTopInset(px)`, `setLevel(z)` (shows level `z`; an unreached level draws nothing until its chunks arrive), `setPeople(people)`, `personAt(x, y)` (P2.1) and `movePeople(walkers, at)` (P2.3), `mark` (the inspected tile's corner marks), `thumbnail` (a 112 by 80 JPEG of the view for the save list, '' on failure), `fps`, `destroy`). `onSelect` fires on a tap or click that did not end a drag or pinch; type `Hover`.
- **Drawing:** three plain tilemap layers (not Phaser's `TilemapGPULayer`, whose shader mis-reads tile indices on some phones): ground from the packed `tiles-base` and `tiles-world` sheets, one tileset after the other, then shore and deep-water frames (`water-art.ts` picks one of the 47 `water.shore.<mask>` blob frames from a water tile's eight neighbours, over the picture of a land neighbour, or a `water.deep.<mask>` frame over open deep water), and features (trees, bushes, herbs, boulders, and a kind's `marker` such as a spring) from `sprites-nature` over it; a changed chunk on the level on screen repaints its tiles and the ring around it, since water pictures depend on neighbours; every level's chunks are kept, so switching level repaints from memory. Veins are `vein.<ore>` sprites from `sprites-nature` over rock. Chunks that arrive before the scene is ready are held. A position hash picks each tile's picture variant: a picture choice, never state.
- **Fog and stains (Bundle 2):** unknown ground below (`known` 0) draws as `underground.fogArt`, black, showing only a vein, which glints at `fogVein` brightness; a chunk repaints when its `known` changes. `chunk-store.ts` keeps the level arrays. `stains.ts` speckles surface tiles with `stains` (14 from the position hash, colour `vein.stainColour`) at depth 1, under features (2). `frames.ts`: sheet-grid and hash helpers.
- **Weather and aura (P1.4):** frozen lake tiles swap their `water.` frames for the matching `ice.` frames (`ice.shore.<mask>`, `ice.deep.<mask>`, `tile.ice.1`) from the chunk's `ice` bytes; `setAura(spots)` paints a fourth layer of glows (`aura-art.ts`: `aura.<school>.core` on the spot, `aura.<school>.1` around it, fading with the ring out to the radius) on the level on screen.
- **People (P2.1):** `people.ts` stands each `PersonView` on its tile as a body from the `sprites-people` sheet with clothes and hair laid over it, tinted to their colours (`person.<race>.<facing>.<frame>`, `clothes.<race>.<facing>.<frame>`, `hair.<style>.<race>.<facing>`), and since P2.3 `movePeople(walkers)` moves them to each snapshot's tiles: they walk there (`trail.ts`: 3 game minutes behind, each step at its real pace), facing the way they go with their two walk frames, then stand; only those on the level on screen are drawn, over features and aura, under the selection marks. Cold coats: `coats.ts` (V-190).
- **Goods (P2.4):** `goods.ts` draws each snapshot's `goods` on the level on screen from the `items` sheet: home store tiles (`zone.stockpile`, depth 5; stores of a kind are drawn by their built parts), items lying about by frame (`item.<kind>`, `item.bedroll.laid`, `cart`, depth 6), skeps (`goods.skeps`, a plan at half opacity) and test marks (`mark.fell`, depth 7), under the people; redrawn only when they change. `setGoods(view, sky)` on the `Renderer` (`sky`: the snapshot's speed, skipping and clock). Bundle 3's smelter (`furn.smelter`) and charcoal clamp ride in the same list: the clamp is four tiles (`furn.clamp.<nw|ne|sw|se>`, faint while cold) and smokes while lit through numbered frames (`furn.clamp.lit.<quarter>.<1-3>`) that cycle like flames; the forge, anvil block and quench tub are ordinary pieces.
- **Built parts and plans (P3.1, P3.2):** `build.ts` keeps the surface chunks' `built` arrays and the snapshot's `goods.plans`, and draws them from the `buildings` sheet: floors at depth 4, then walls and doors at depth 7. A slim wall is a band through the middle of its tile joined to the walls and doors on its four sides (`build.wall-<material>.<mask>`); on a side or inner corner that meets a floor the floor's half or quarter shows under it, the rest of the tile showing the ground beneath, so a wall reads over every floor and ground. A door runs north-south when only its north or south side joins a wall, else east-west. Plans draw at half opacity as what they will be (a stair as `build.stair-<material>`); a part ordered down is tinted. Redrawn only when the built tiles or the plans change.
- **Furniture (P3.4):** `furniture.ts` draws each snapshot's `goods.furniture` from the `buildings` sheet at depth 6: the frame for the piece's facing when the sheet has one (`furn.bed.v`), else the plain art; planned pieces at half opacity; redrawn only when they change.
- **Fields (P4.7):** `fields.ts` draws `goods.fields` (a frame and its tiles) at depth 3, furrows with no edge.
- **Shaft plans (Bundle 2):** `shafts.ts` draws `goods.shafts` as `tile.shaft.1` at half opacity, depth 4, surface only.
- **Injury and death (P5.6, P5.8):** `people.ts` lays a walker with `lying` down (the stacked body, clothes and hair turned 90°), greys the unburied dead, draws a carried person over their carrier a few pixels off and does not draw the buried. `burial.ts` draws `goods.graves` (`grave.dug`, or `grave.mound` plus `grave.marker`) at depth 5 to 6 and the hurdle edge of `goods.burial` (`burial.hurdle.n|e|s|w` on each edge tile, a corner gets two) from the `items` sheet; a frame the sheet lacks is skipped.
- **Wild valley (Phase 5A Bundle 2):** `wild.ts` (sheet `sprites-wild`): snow is a tile layer of `tile.snow.1` over the ground and stains (depth 1.1) where a chunk's `snowLie` is set; prints are `print.<paw|hoof|hare>.<dir>` sprites under the features (depth 1.8) at alpha 0.15 + 0.85 x (1 - age/`printHours`); animals are `animal.<kind>.<e|w>.<0|1>` sprites that walk (`trail.ts`) with two walk frames (depth 8, with people); middens (a hook) are `site.midden`. Repainted only when a chunk or the list changes. `setAnimals`, `animalAt(x, y)` (a tap opens its page). The smoke's `vulcan:find-animal` event on the page's `#game` centres the view on an animal and writes `data-animals` and `data-animal-at`.
- **Worn ground and roads (P5.10):** `wear.ts`, a `tiles-world` layer at depth 1.05 (`wear.<stage>.<1|2>` where a chunk's `worn` is set, on `terrain.wear.kinds`). A road part draws like a floor (`build.ts`).
- **Night, firelight and progress (P3.6):** `firelight.ts` dims the surface for 45 minutes either side of the clock's `dusk` and `dawn` (P5.13; blue-grey up to 0.5 alpha, depth 8.5, surface only) and lays a soft warm additive pool (depth 8.6) over each lit fire as wide as its reach, dimmer banked, shimmering; a candlelit room (`goods.lit` boxes) gets one paler `--bone` pool at 0.4 strength; rebuilt only when fires or lit rooms change; no roofs are drawn. `progress.ts` draws a progress bar (iron track, ember fill, oak edge, 24 art px, depth 9.5) over each plan, part or piece in `goods.work`, gliding at the sim speed towards the minutes done, at most a minute ahead, never backwards unless the total changes (maths in `progress-math.ts`). A lit hearth cycles its numbered frames (`furn.hearth.lit.1` to `.3`) every 170 ms.
- **Seen at work (V-194):** `at-work.ts` draws each walker's `held` tool, `load` and `act` in their sprite (numbers in `work-math.ts`, bits in `specks.ts`, wrong-facing side frames mirrored by `look.ts`); `vulcan:find-worker` serves the browser check.
- **Finding people (2026-10-25):** `finder.ts` rings the person whose sheet is open in ember under their feet (an ellipse with a dark edge, depth 7.8), following their sprite, with two widening waves when it goes on (`follow(id, pulse)`), and `showNames(on)` rings everyone on the level on screen the same way with their first name over their head in bone, kept 14 screen px at any zoom; `placeOf(id)` and `focus(x, y, below)` (centres a tile between the bar and a bottom sheet, coming in to 1× from farther out) let the page find someone. View only (V-015). The find check reads `data-found` (the followed person's screen px) on `#game`.
- **Thought bubbles (V-154):** `bubbles.ts`, a DOM layer over the canvas (`.bubble`, paper and ink, no pointer events): the followed person's thought while their sheet is open, and for 4 s anyone whose `thought` changed (2 at most), only at 1x or closer, above the head or below it when the bar is in the way, never over another bubble. View only.
- **Camera:** drag, wheel, pinch, WASD, arrows and +/− (1.5× glides) from 3× down to the fitted map below the bar (`setTopInset(px)`; the top row scrolls clear of it) or ¼×; below 1× is an overview (VR-37); a fitted view stays fitted on resize; bounded by the map.
- **State owned:** none of the game's; only view state (camera, selection).
- **Rules:** may import Phaser, worker protocol types and content art names.
- **Tests:** `trail.test.ts` (even pace, leaps, the clock); `work-math.test.ts`; `progress-math.test.ts` (the bar's glide); the rest is covered by the browser smoke (`tests/browser`).
