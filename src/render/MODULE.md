# render

Phaser scenes, tilemap layers, sprites and camera. Reads snapshots from the worker and never changes game state.

- **Public API:** `src/render/index.ts`: `startRenderer(parent, {onHover, onSelect})` boots Phaser with the valley scene and returns a `Renderer` (`setMapSize`, `applyChunks`, `zoomStep`, `setTopInset(px)`, `setLevel(z)` (shows level `z`; an unreached level draws nothing until its chunks arrive), `setPeople(people)`, `personAt(x, y)` (P2.1) and `movePeople(walkers)` (P2.3), `mark` (the inspected tile's corner marks), `thumbnail` (a 112 by 80 JPEG of the view for the save list, '' on failure), `fps`, `destroy`). `onSelect` fires on a tap or click that did not end a drag or pinch; type `Hover`.
- **Drawing:** three plain tilemap layers (not Phaser's `TilemapGPULayer`, whose shader mis-reads tile indices on some phones): ground from the packed `tiles-base` and `tiles-world` sheets, one tileset after the other, then shore and deep-water frames (`water-art.ts` picks one of the 47 `water.shore.<mask>` blob frames from a water tile's eight neighbours, over the picture of a land neighbour, or a `water.deep.<mask>` frame over open deep water), and features (trees, bushes, herbs, boulders, and a kind's `marker` such as a spring) from `sprites-nature` over it; a changed chunk on the level on screen repaints its tiles and the ring around it, since water pictures depend on neighbours; every level's chunks are kept, so switching level repaints from memory. Veins are `vein.<ore>` sprites from `sprites-nature` over rock. Chunks that arrive before the scene is ready are held. A position hash picks each tile's picture variant: a picture choice, never state.
- **Weather and aura (P1.4):** frozen lake tiles swap their `water.` frames for the matching `ice.` frames (`ice.shore.<mask>`, `ice.deep.<mask>`, `tile.ice.1`) from the chunk's `ice` bytes; `setAura(spots)` paints a fourth layer of glows (`aura-art.ts`: `aura.<school>.core` on the spot, `aura.<school>.1` around it, fading with the ring out to the radius) on the level on screen.
- **People (P2.1):** `people.ts` stands each `PersonView` on its tile as a body from the `sprites-people` sheet with clothes and hair laid over it, tinted to their colours (`person.<race>.<facing>.<frame>`, `clothes.<race>.<facing>.<frame>`, `hair.<style>.<race>.<facing>`), and since P2.3 `movePeople(walkers)` moves them to each snapshot's tiles: they glide there on screen (at least a tile a game minute at 1x, never more than about 0.1 s behind; a jump over 20 tiles is not walked out), facing the way they go with their two walk frames, then stand; only those on the level on screen are drawn, over features and aura, under the selection marks. People set before the scene is created are drawn once it is.
- **Goods (P2.4):** `goods.ts` draws each snapshot's `goods` on the level on screen from the `items` sheet: store tiles (`zone.stockpile`, depth 5), items lying about by frame (`item.<kind>`, `item.bedroll.laid`, `cart`, depth 6) and test marks (`mark.fell`, depth 7), under the people; redrawn only when they change. `setGoods(view)` on the `Renderer`.
- **Built parts and plans (P3.1, P3.2):** `build.ts` keeps the surface chunks' `built` arrays and the snapshot's `goods.plans`, and draws them from the `buildings` sheet: floors at depth 4, then walls and doors at depth 7. A slim wall is a band through the middle of its tile joined to the walls and doors on its four sides (`build.wall-<material>.<mask>`); on a side or inner corner that meets a floor the floor's half or quarter shows under it, the rest of the tile showing the ground beneath, so a wall reads over every floor and ground. A door runs north-south when only its north or south side joins a wall, else east-west. Plans draw at half opacity as what they will be (a stair as `build.stair-<material>`); a part ordered down is tinted. Redrawn only when the built tiles or the plans change. Sheet keys live in `sheets.ts`.
- **Furniture (P3.4):** `furniture.ts` draws each snapshot's `goods.furniture` from the `buildings` sheet at depth 6 (over the floors, under the people): the frame for the piece's facing when the sheet has one (`furn.bed.v`, `furn.chair.e`, `furn.shelf.n`), else the plain art (`furn.table`, `furn.chest`, `furn.bench`); planned pieces at half opacity like part plans; redrawn only when the pieces change.
- **Camera:** drag to pan; wheel and pinch zoom smoothly about the pointer or the fingers' midpoint, and the +/− buttons and keys glide 1.5× in or out (Morgan, 2026-10-18: the fixed steps jumped too far), from 3× down to the zoom that fits the whole map below the page's bar (`setTopInset(px)`; the bounds also reach that far above the map, so the top row scrolls clear of the bar), or ¼× if that is farther; below 1× is an overview under the 32 px rule (VR-37); a fitted view stays fitted on resize; WASD and arrows; bounded by the map.
- **State owned:** none of the game's; only view state (camera, selection).
- **Rules:** may import Phaser, worker protocol types and content art names.
- **Tests:** covered by the browser smoke (`tests/browser`).
