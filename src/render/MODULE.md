# render

Phaser scenes, tilemap layers, sprites and camera. Reads snapshots from the worker and never changes game state.

- **Public API:** `src/render/index.ts`: `startRenderer(parent, {onHover, onSelect})` boots Phaser with the valley scene and returns a `Renderer` (`setMapSize`, `applyChunks`, `zoomStep`, `setTopInset(px)`, `setLevel(z)` (shows level `z`; an unreached level draws nothing until its chunks arrive), `setPeople(people)`, `personAt(x, y)` (P2.1) and `movePeople(walkers)` (P2.3), `mark` (the inspected tile's corner marks), `thumbnail` (a 112 by 80 JPEG of the view for the save list, '' on failure), `fps`, `destroy`). `onSelect` fires on a tap or click that did not end a drag or pinch; type `Hover`.
- **Drawing:** three plain tilemap layers (not Phaser's `TilemapGPULayer`, whose shader mis-reads tile indices on some phones): ground from the packed `tiles-base` and `tiles-world` sheets, one tileset after the other, then shore and deep-water frames (`water-art.ts` picks one of the 47 `water.shore.<mask>` blob frames from a water tile's eight neighbours, over the picture of a land neighbour, or a `water.deep.<mask>` frame over open deep water), and features (trees, bushes, herbs, boulders, and a kind's `marker` such as a spring) from `sprites-nature` over it; a changed chunk on the level on screen repaints its tiles and the ring around it, since water pictures depend on neighbours; every level's chunks are kept, so switching level repaints from memory. Veins are `vein.<ore>` sprites from `sprites-nature` over rock. Chunks that arrive before the scene is ready are held. A position hash picks each tile's picture variant: a picture choice, never state.
- **Fog and stains (Bundle 2):** unknown ground below (`known` 0) draws as `underground.fogArt`, black, showing only a vein, which glints at `fogVein` brightness; a chunk repaints when its `known` changes. `chunk-store.ts` keeps the level arrays. `stains.ts` speckles surface tiles with `stains` (14 from the position hash, colour `vein.stainColour`) at depth 1, under features (2). `frames.ts`: sheet-grid and hash helpers.
- **Weather and aura (P1.4):** frozen lake tiles swap their `water.` frames for the matching `ice.` frames (`ice.shore.<mask>`, `ice.deep.<mask>`, `tile.ice.1`) from the chunk's `ice` bytes; `setAura(spots)` paints a fourth layer of glows (`aura-art.ts`: `aura.<school>.core` on the spot, `aura.<school>.1` around it, fading with the ring out to the radius) on the level on screen.
- **People (P2.1):** `people.ts` stands each `PersonView` on its tile as a body from the `sprites-people` sheet with clothes and hair laid over it, tinted to their colours (`person.<race>.<facing>.<frame>`, `clothes.<race>.<facing>.<frame>`, `hair.<style>.<race>.<facing>`), and since P2.3 `movePeople(walkers)` moves them to each snapshot's tiles: they glide there on screen (at least a tile a game minute at 1x, never more than about 0.1 s behind; a jump over 20 tiles is not walked out), facing the way they go with their two walk frames, then stand; only those on the level on screen are drawn, over features and aura, under the selection marks. People set before the scene is created are drawn once it is.
- **Goods (P2.4):** `goods.ts` draws each snapshot's `goods` on the level on screen from the `items` sheet: store tiles (`zone.stockpile`, depth 5) and granary tiles (the same frame, tinted gold), items lying about by frame (`item.<kind>`, `item.bedroll.laid`, `cart`, depth 6) and test marks (`mark.fell`, depth 7), under the people; redrawn only when they change. `setGoods(view, sky)` on the `Renderer` (`sky`: the snapshot's speed, skipping and clock). Bundle 3's smelter (`furn.smelter`) and charcoal clamp ride in the same list: the clamp is four tiles (`furn.clamp.<nw|ne|sw|se>`, faint while cold) and smokes while lit through numbered frames (`furn.clamp.lit.<quarter>.<1-3>`) that cycle like flames; the forge, anvil block and quench tub are ordinary pieces.
- **Built parts and plans (P3.1, P3.2):** `build.ts` keeps the surface chunks' `built` arrays and the snapshot's `goods.plans`, and draws them from the `buildings` sheet: floors at depth 4, then walls and doors at depth 7. A slim wall is a band through the middle of its tile joined to the walls and doors on its four sides (`build.wall-<material>.<mask>`); on a side or inner corner that meets a floor the floor's half or quarter shows under it, the rest of the tile showing the ground beneath, so a wall reads over every floor and ground. A door runs north-south when only its north or south side joins a wall, else east-west. Plans draw at half opacity as what they will be (a stair as `build.stair-<material>`); a part ordered down is tinted. Redrawn only when the built tiles or the plans change.
- **Furniture (P3.4):** `furniture.ts` draws each snapshot's `goods.furniture` from the `buildings` sheet at depth 6: the frame for the piece's facing when the sheet has one (`furn.bed.v`), else the plain art; planned pieces at half opacity; redrawn only when they change.
- **Fields (P4.7):** `fields.ts` draws `goods.fields` (a frame and its tiles) at depth 3, furrows with no edge.
- **Shaft plans (Bundle 2):** `shafts.ts` draws `goods.shafts` as `tile.shaft.1` at half opacity, depth 4, surface only.
- **Injury and death (P5.6, P5.8):** `people.ts` lays a walker with `lying` down (the stacked body, clothes and hair turned 90°), greys the unburied dead, draws a carried person over their carrier a few pixels off and does not draw the buried. `burial.ts` draws `goods.graves` (`grave.dug`, or `grave.mound` plus `grave.marker`) at depth 5 to 6 and the hurdle edge of `goods.burial` (`burial.hurdle.n|e|s|w` on each edge tile, a corner gets two) from the `items` sheet; a frame the sheet lacks is skipped.
- **Wild valley (Phase 5A Bundle 2):** `wild.ts` (sheet `sprites-wild`): snow is a tile layer of `tile.snow.1` over the ground and stains (depth 1.1) where a chunk's `snowLie` is set; prints are `print.<paw|hoof|hare>.<dir>` sprites under the features (depth 1.8) at alpha 0.15 + 0.85 x (1 - age/`printHours`); animals are `animal.<kind>.<e|w>.<0|1>` sprites that glide between tiles with two walk frames (depth 8, with people); middens (a hook) are `site.midden`. Repainted only when a chunk or the list changes. `setAnimals`, `animalAt(x, y)` (a tap opens its page). The smoke's `vulcan:find-animal` event on the page's `#game` centres the view on an animal and writes `data-animals` and `data-animal-at`.
- **Night, firelight and progress (P3.6):** `firelight.ts` dims the surface after dusk (19:00 to 21:00, back by 07:00; a deep blue-grey at up to 0.5 alpha, depth 8.5, surface only) and lays a soft warm pool (one additive image per lit fire, depth 8.6) over each lit fire as wide as its reach, dimmer when banked, with a slow shimmer; rebuilt only when the set of fires changes, the hours and alphas move with each snapshot's clock; roofs are never drawn, so a room's pool simply lies over it. `progress.ts` draws the build progress bar over every plan, part or piece the snapshot's `goods.work` names: a track of dark iron, an ember fill and a 1 px oak edge, 24 art px wide just above the tile (depth 9.5); each frame the fill glides on at the sim speed (`setGoods`'s second argument; paused it holds, 1x to 3x proportionally faster) towards the latest minutes done, a little ahead of it at most (a minute, or 50 ms of minutes at 3x), never backwards unless the total changes; the glide's maths is `progress-math.ts`, tested apart from Phaser. A lit hearth cycles its numbered frames (`furn.hearth.lit.1` to `.3`) every 170 ms.
- **Camera:** drag to pan; wheel and pinch zoom smoothly about the pointer or the fingers' midpoint, and the +/− buttons and keys glide 1.5× in or out, from 3× down to the zoom that fits the whole map below the page's bar (`setTopInset(px)`; the bounds also reach that far above the map, so the top row scrolls clear of the bar), or ¼× if that is farther; below 1× is an overview under the 32 px rule (VR-37); a fitted view stays fitted on resize; WASD and arrows; bounded by the map.
- **State owned:** none of the game's; only view state (camera, selection).
- **Rules:** may import Phaser, worker protocol types and content art names.
- **Tests:** `progress-math.test.ts` (the bar's glide: forwards only at every speed, holds when paused, starts over on a new total); the rest is covered by the browser smoke (`tests/browser`).
