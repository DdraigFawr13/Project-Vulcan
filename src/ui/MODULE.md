# ui

The page entry, panels, inspector, ledger browser and controls. Sends commands to the worker and shows snapshots and events.

- **Public API:** none; `src/ui/main.ts` is the entry `index.html` loads.
- **Files:** `main.ts` (worker, renderer, keys, message routing), `picker.ts` and `regionmap.ts` (P1.5, below), `hud.ts` (clock with the level on screen, speed buttons, Ledger and the ⋯ menu (Stock, Region, Saves), readout, the level control above the zoom buttons, the not-reached note, screens), `panel.ts` (the side panel, a bottom sheet under 640 px wide: tile inspector, ledger with "why?", save list with New game), `saves.ts` (saving, loading, autosave, export download, the ledger's newest 200 lines), `title.ts`, `arrival.ts`, `person.ts` and `portrait.ts` (P2.1, below), `dom.ts`; `sheets.ts`, `play.ts`, `personsheet.ts` and `work.ts` and `stepper.ts` (P2.5 to P2.7, below), `build.ts` (P3.7, below), `piecerows.ts` (P3.4, below).
- **Look (P2.8, Hearth and Page, `docs/UI_STYLE.md`):** `tokens.css` holds every colour, face and art piece; `hearth.css` (bar, plates, ⋯ menu, sheet, ledger, saves) and `hearth-screens.css` (title, picker, arrival, person rows) read only those. Buttons are iron plates 9-sliced from `public/art/ui/` (`tools/draw-ui.mjs`), reading surfaces are paper in an oak frame. On a phone the ⋯ menu folds Stock, Region and Saves and turns ember while one of them is open; on a desktop they sit in the bar. Ledger lines show the entry number in a rubric column, and a "why?" chain steps each cause in one notch (`--depth`). The smoke checks tap targets (44 px, V-077) and text (11 px) at 390 px.
- **Levels:** ▲ and ▼ (or `<` and `>`, PageUp and PageDown) step the view between the surface and the four levels below; taps inspect the level on screen. With `?debug`, the tile panel's TEST MODE "Cut stairs down" and "Sink a shaft" buttons send the map's test orders for the next tick (they wait while paused) until colonists can dig.
- **Stock and test tools (P2.4):** the bar's Stock button opens `stock.ts`: per good what the colony has and wants and a button per level (None, Some (50), Plenty (200)), each sending `setStock`; redrawn only when the snapshot's `stock` changes. The tile sheet lists what lies there and whether it is part of the store, each with its "Made by" line and why?. The direct levers (cut stairs, sink a shaft, mark a tree to fell, add a store tile) are only in `testtools.ts`, a Test tools row badged TEST MODE shown in test mode (V-015): `testmode.ts` turns it on from the Saves page's switch (remembered per device) or `?debug`, and the bar shows a TEST MODE badge by the clock; `orders.ts` stamps them for the next tick and asks for the tile again once answered, keeping a refusal (`map.digRefused`, or `command.refused` for any other order) to show once as "Not done". Since P3.1 the row also plans a part on a surface tile with no plan (Plan stone wall, Plan wood wall, Plan floor (wood), Plan door (wood), Plan stair down (wood): `placeBuilding`) and takes a plan or part down (`deconstruct`); colonists choose their own sites from P3.7 (V-049). The tile sheet shows a plan or built part (`planrows.ts`): what it is and its state, the material here of its cost, the builder and what they are doing, what it does to paths, then its Waiting, Planned or Ordered down, and Built lines. Paused, every lever applies at once (`applyDue` in the worker), so no sheet says a lever waits.
- **Build sheet and house tiles (P3.7, P3.3, P3.8):** ⋯ → Build (`build.ts`, wired in `sheets.ts` like Work) shows the Houses stepper (the Work sheet's own, `stepper.ts`, sending `setBuild {kind: 'house', level}`), the need, each plan's line with material have and cost, why it was sited and Send back (`sendBack {house}`, once) or "sent back once", and "No ground near the green fits a house" when the engine says so; it names no tile or person (V-049). `planrows.ts` adds to a planned part's rows the house name, roof and Send back, and to a built one its room, role, beauty, impressiveness, beds and warmth, all words from the worker. The map's green ring and "cottage (planned)" labels are `render/green.ts`. Levers apply at once while paused.
- **Furniture, beauty and privacy (P3.4, P3.5):** a tile with a piece opens on `piecerows.ts` (the land's rows and the floor's last change are left out): built or planned, quality and maker, a bed's owner, beauty added, rest, room (named for its owner when every bed is one person's), role and privacy; for the bench what it is making, its maker and Next (what the colony makes next and why, or "nobody makes furniture now", or "nothing; every room has its furniture"); lines Claimed, Made and Placed with "why?". A piece blocks no one's way, and no control places furniture, names a maker or assigns a bed (V-041). The Work sheet's Carpentry row comes from content like the others, with a footnote built from the rooms' wants. Beauty and Privacy are need rows like the rest, with the worker's word for what feeds them under the bar (`feeds`: "plain room", "own bedroom", "sleeps with three others"), and "why?" when low. A carpenter's "Doing now" is the `work.json` text, never "hauling".
- **Hearths and warmth (P3.6):** a hearth's tile page (`piecerows.ts`) reads "campfire" or "hearth": lit, banked or out and where, its fuel and kind in hours and minutes, what it warms, then the room and its warmth, and the fire's last line (Lit, Banked, Out, Tended, Built) with its "why?" link; a planned one names its stone. A room's page shows a warmth row (a word and degrees, "· no hearth" or the hearth's state), from the worker. The Work sheet's Tending row and the Stock sheet's Firewood plate come from content like the others.
- **People, work, alerts and skip (P2.5 to P2.7):** `sheets.ts` wires `play.ts` (the portrait strip under the bar, a face per person ringed by mood, and the alert line: the newest notable line, as the worker marks it, with a count not yet read), `personsheet.ts` (the person sheet's tabs: Needs live in place, Skills with practice, Work, and Ledger lines whose `actors` name them) and `work.ts` (the Work sheet under ⋯: one person at a time with 1 to 4 steppers on a phone, a grid on a desktop, the decline line with why?, and the colony's day; `setPriority` and `setDay` go out for the next tick and say they wait while paused). The Alerts sheet's Show moves the camera to the place (`renderer.lookAt`), never a person (V-015). Skip ▸▸ after 3× sends `skip`; the worker stops it paused on a notable line and the bar follows. Styles in `hearth-play.css`; the bar grows by the strip, and `--bar-h` keeps a desktop sheet below it.
- **Weather (P1.4):** the clock line adds the day's weather ("Winter · A blizzard blows, -7 °C"); the inspector adds the tile's temperature, ice and aura with the spot that gives it.
- **Seed and picker (P1.5):** the URL's `?seed=` (default `ledger-1`) opens `picker.ts`, the site picker: the region map (`regionmap.ts`: the worker's raster in the game's palette with peaks, trees and rivers, then names, race-coloured site glyphs and numbered valley markers as SVG with tap areas of about 40 px; labels that would collide are left off), a race legend, three valley cards (a thumbnail of the real map, climate, temperature, rain, biomes, lakes, springs, aura, nearest neighbours) and Settle here, New seed and a seed field. Settling sets `?seed=…&site=n`, so a reload begins that valley at once; the save panel's New game opens the picker for a new seed. The Region button opens the same map read-only in the panel with only the settled valley marked; a tapped site shows its culture, size, people, race, rule and faith, then its realm, founding year and history line (P1.6). Every site is named on the map (its label is placed before the rivers', above or below when the sides are taken); the legend lists only the races present.
- **Continue (Phase 1 review):** reloading a settled valley (`?seed=…&site=n`) loads its newest save instead of a new Day 1. A seed alone still opens the picker.
- **Title, band and arrival (P2.1):** the bare link shows `title.ts` ("Project Vulcan", the working title) over the hidden HUD (`#hud.covered`): New game opens the picker; with a save, the newest of any world (RESCUE included) loads behind the dimmed title and Continue names its valley and date; a save the worker refuses leaves a plain sentence and New game only. A fresh world from a seed with people shows `arrival.ts`: the place and date, where the band came from, a card per person (portrait, race, culture, age, home, best three skills as level bars with a potential tick, traits, "can build" and "can cook" badges), who can build and cook, and Arrive. Tapping a person on the map opens their sheet (`PersonSheet`, tabs since P2.6): name and home, distance, trade, every skill with its number, traits with their meaning, and "Came with the band" with "why?". `portrait.ts` draws a person from the `sprites-people` sheet with their colours. Names, trades and who is able come from the worker's `PersonView`s.
- **Closing the tab:** every 5 s of play the page asks the worker for a standby copy and holds it unwritten; hiding, closing or reloading the tab writes the newest one into the autosave slot at once (unless a save as new exists), so at most 5 s of play is lost.
- **Ledger archive and rescue (P2.0):** `segments` messages are archived through persist in order, and every save waits for the archive first, so no save names a day it lacks. Export downloads `<name>.ndjson.gz`: the stored gzipped days and the recent ones gzipped, joined without unpacking. On an engine error the newest standby copy, with the levers given since it was taken, goes to the rescue slot (a RESCUE row in Saves, loadable), and the error screen says which day it keeps. A failed archive write stays shown in Saves until reload. Known gap: a day sealed in the last moments before the tab closes may miss the archive (its gzip is still in flight); the export then says how many older days are missing.
- **Debug:** `?debug` in the URL shows the fps and tick line (with the worker's tick timing: mean and p95 ms over the last 1,000 ticks and the slowest system) and the inspector's chunk row; players see neither. The bar's height is passed to the renderer (`setTopInset`) so the fitted map starts below it.
- **Saves:** "Save now" and the autosave ask the worker for a `saved` record, add a name (seed and game date) and a thumbnail, and write it through persist. The autosave runs every 60 s of real time while the game runs and when the tab is hidden, into one slot, and skips if no tick has passed since the last one or since the world began or was loaded (so a fresh or just-loaded world never replaces it). Load pauses, sends the record to the worker and shows a loading screen; `ready {from: 'save'}` ends it, `loadRefused` shows the reason with the game paused behind it; closing it restores the speed.
- **Rules:** never computes a game rule (GAME-02); everything shown comes from a snapshot, an event line or a worker answer. Typing in the seed field is not a game key; Escape closes the panel.
- **Tests:** the browser smoke (title, arrival, inspector, "why?", save, reload, load, export, Continue, and at 390 px a person's tabs, the Work stepper and its Carpentry and Tending rows (42 px), the Firewood plate, Skip, the alerts and the Build sheet's Houses stepper).
