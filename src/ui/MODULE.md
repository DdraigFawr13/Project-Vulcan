# ui

The page entry, panels, inspector, ledger browser and controls. Sends commands to the worker and shows snapshots and events.

- **Public API:** none; `src/ui/main.ts` is the entry `index.html` loads.
- **Files:** `main.ts` (worker, renderer, keys, message routing), `picker.ts` and `regionmap.ts` (P1.5, below), `hud.ts` (clock with the level on screen, speed buttons, Ledger and the ⋯ menu (Stock, Region, Saves), readout, the level control above the zoom buttons, the not-reached note, screens), `panel.ts` (the side panel, a bottom sheet under 640 px wide: tile inspector, ledger with "why?", save list with New game), `saves.ts` (saving, loading, autosave, export download, the ledger's newest 200 lines), `title.ts`, `arrival.ts`, `person.ts` and `portrait.ts` (P2.1, below), `dom.ts`, `version.ts` (the build's version, stamped by `tools/version.ts`: a quiet label at the end of the clock's second line, the full build on the Saves sheet); `sheets.ts`, `play.ts`, `personsheet.ts` and `work.ts` and `stepper.ts` (P2.5 to P2.7, below), `build.ts` (P3.7, below), `laws.ts` and `daystrip.ts` (P4.8: the Laws sheet under ⋯, the day strip the Work sheet shares), `piecerows.ts` (P3.4, below).
- **Look (P2.8, Hearth and Page, `docs/UI_STYLE.md`):** `tokens.css` holds every colour, face and art piece; `hearth.css` (bar, plates, ⋯ menu, sheet, ledger, saves) and `hearth-screens.css` (title, picker, arrival, person rows) read only those. Buttons are iron plates 9-sliced from `public/art/ui/` (`tools/draw-ui.mjs`), reading surfaces are paper in an oak frame. On a phone the ⋯ menu folds Stock, Region and Saves and turns ember while one of them is open; on a desktop they sit in the bar. Ledger lines show the entry number in a rubric column, and a "why?" chain steps each cause in one notch (`--depth`).
- **Levels, stains, headings (Bundle 2):** ▲ ▼ and the level label show once a level below is reached (test mode: always). The tile page words stains, unknown ground and veins from the worker. The Work sheet (`workheads.ts`) keeps haul, build and tend on top and folds trades under `priority.headings`: a 44 px ▾/▸ and a stepper sending a `setPriority` per job (common or lowest level).
- **Shaft plans (Bundle 2):** `shaftrows.ts` (the tile page and Send back, `sendBackShaft`); `landrows.ts` (land rows). The Mining row shows when the snapshot says `mining`.
- **Levels:** ▲ and ▼ (or `<` and `>`, PageUp and PageDown) step the view between the surface and the levels below; taps inspect the level on screen.
- **Stock and test tools (P2.4):** the bar's Stock button opens `stock.ts`: per good what the colony has and wants, a button per level (`setStock`); redrawn only when `stock` changes. The tile sheet lists what lies there and whether it is in the store, each with its "Made by" line and why?. Direct levers (cut stairs, sink a shaft, mark a tree, add a store tile, `placeBuilding`, `deconstruct`) live only in `testtools.ts`, the TEST MODE row (V-015): `testmode.ts` turns it on from the Saves switch or `?debug`, with a badge by the clock; `orders.ts` stamps them for the next tick and shows a refusal once as "Not done". The tile sheet shows a plan or built part (`planrows.ts`): its state, cost, builder, paths and lines. Paused, every lever applies at once (`applyDue` in the worker), so no sheet says a lever waits.
- **Wild valley (Phase 5A Bundle 2):** a tap on an animal asks the worker for its page (`animal`), and `animalsheet.ts` shows it in the panel (`PanelKind` `animal`): the `pwho` line, `prow` rows and its last two lines with why?. View only: no button but why? and ✕. The tile page's prints, fade and snow rows are in `landrows.ts`; the Stock sheet ends in a Leftovers block (hides, bone, fat) and the Food plate names the game and fish counted (`stock.ts`); counts fill in place.
- **Build sheet and house tiles (P3.7, P3.3, P3.8):** ⋯ → Build (`build.ts`, wired in `sheets.ts` like Work) shows the Fields then Houses steppers (`stepper.ts`, `setBuild {kind, level}`), each need (Fields in tiles of crops), each plan's line with why and Send back (`sendBack {house|field}`, once) or "sent back once"; it names no tile or person (V-049). the Needs tab ends in Wearing, Memories. `planrows.ts` adds to a planned part's rows the house name, roof and Send back, and to a built one its room, role, beauty, impressiveness, beds and warmth, all words from the worker. The map's green ring and "cottage (planned)" labels are `render/green.ts`. Levers apply at once while paused.
- **Furniture, beauty and privacy (P3.4, P3.5):** a tile with a piece opens on `piecerows.ts` (the land's rows and the floor's last change are left out): built or planned, quality and maker, a bed's owner, beauty added, rest, room (named for its owner when every bed is one person's), role and privacy; for the bench what it is making, its maker and Next (what the colony makes next and why, or "nobody makes furniture now", or "nothing; every room has its furniture"); lines Claimed, Made and Placed with "why?". A piece blocks no one's way, and no control places furniture, names a maker or assigns a bed (V-041). The Work sheet's Carpentry row comes from content like the others, with a footnote built from the rooms' wants. Beauty and Privacy are need rows like the rest, with the worker's word for what feeds them under the bar (`feeds`), and "why?" when low. A carpenter's "Doing now" is the `work.json` text, never "hauling".
- **Hearths and warmth (P3.6):** a hearth's tile page (`piecerows.ts`) reads "campfire" or "hearth": lit, banked or out and where, its fuel and kind in hours and minutes, what it warms, then the room and its warmth, and the fire's last line (Lit, Banked, Out, Tended, Built) with its "why?" link; a planned one names its stone. A room's page shows a warmth row (a word and degrees, "· no hearth" or the hearth's state), from the worker. The Work sheet's Tending row and the Stock sheet's Firewood plate come from content like the others.
- **People, work, alerts and skip (P2.5 to P2.7):** `sheets.ts` wires `play.ts` (the portrait strip under the bar, a face per person ringed by mood, and the alert line: the newest notable line, as the worker marks it, with a count not yet read), `personsheet.ts` (the person sheet's tabs: Needs live in place, Skills with practice, Work, and Ledger lines whose `actors` name them) and `work.ts` (the Work sheet under ⋯: one person at a time with 1 to 4 steppers on a phone, a grid on a desktop, the decline line with why?, and the colony's day as a read-only strip set by the work-hours law (P4.8); `setPriority` goes out for the next tick). The Alerts sheet's Show moves the camera to the place (`renderer.lookAt`), never a person (V-015). Skip ▸▸ after 3× sends `skip`; the worker stops it paused on a notable line and the bar follows. Styles in `hearth-play.css`; the bar grows by the strip, and `--bar-h` keeps a desktop sheet below it.
- **Weather (P1.4):** the clock line adds the day's weather ("Winter · A blizzard blows, -7 °C"); the inspector adds the tile's temperature, ice and aura with the spot that gives it.
- **Seed and picker (P1.5):** `?seed=` (default `ledger-1`) opens `picker.ts`: the region map (`regionmap.ts`: the worker's raster with names, site glyphs and numbered valleys as SVG), a race legend, three valley cards and Settle here, New seed and a seed field. Settling sets `?seed=…&site=n`, so a reload begins that valley. The Region button opens the same map read-only with the settled valley marked; a tapped site shows its culture, people, rule, faith, realm and history line (P1.6).
- **Continue (Phase 1 review):** reloading a settled valley (`?seed=…&site=n`) loads its newest save instead of a new Day 1. A seed alone still opens the picker.
- **Title, band and arrival (P2.1):** the bare link shows `title.ts` ("Project Vulcan", the working title) over the hidden HUD (`#hud.covered`): New game opens the picker; with a save, the newest of any world (RESCUE included) loads behind the dimmed title and Continue names its valley and date; a save the worker refuses leaves a plain sentence and New game only. A fresh world from a seed with people shows `arrival.ts`: the place and date, where the band came from, a card per person, who can build and cook, and Arrive. Tapping a person on the map opens their sheet (`PersonSheet`, tabs since P2.6). `portrait.ts` draws a person from the `sprites-people` sheet with their colours. Names, trades and who is able come from the worker's `PersonView`s.
- **Closing the tab:** every 5 s of play the page holds a standby copy from the worker and writes it to the autosave slot when the tab hides, closes or reloads (unless a save as new exists).
- **Ledger archive and rescue (P2.0):** `segments` are archived through persist in order, and every save waits for the archive. Export downloads `<name>.ndjson.gz` (stored days plus recent ones, joined without unpacking). On an engine error the newest standby copy plus later levers goes to the RESCUE slot. Known gap: a day sealed just before the tab closes may miss the archive; the export says how many days are missing.
- **Metal (Bundle 3, V-041):** the Bronze plate, the Smithy row (`sendBack {smithy}`), the Metal heading's rows; `worksrows.ts` tile pages; `itemsheet.ts` item page (P4.6). Detail: `docs/TOOLS_AND_SMITHY.md`.
- **Graves, burial ground, sickroom (P5.6, P5.8, V-041):** `placerows.ts`: a grave's and the burial ground's tile page (the aside, who lies there and a line about them, rows, the death, burial and siting lines with "why?"; nothing on it is tappable but "why?"), and a sickroom's title. The Build sheet lists the burial ground with its line and one Send back (`sendBack {burial}`) until the first grave is dug, then "sent back once" or "A grave is dug: it stays.".
- **Debug:** `?debug` in the URL shows the fps and tick line (with the worker's tick timing: mean and p95 ms over the last 1,000 ticks and the slowest system) and the inspector's chunk row; players see neither. The bar's height is passed to the renderer (`setTopInset`) so the fitted map starts below it.
- **Saves:** "Save now" and the autosave ask the worker for a `saved` record, add a name (seed and game date) and a thumbnail, and write it through persist. The autosave runs every 60 s of real time while the game runs and when the tab is hidden, into one slot, and skips if no tick has passed since the last one or since the world began or was loaded (so a fresh or just-loaded world never replaces it). Load pauses, sends the record to the worker and shows a loading screen; `ready {from: 'save'}` ends it, `loadRefused` shows the reason (game paused).
- **Health and the dead (P5.6, P5.8, V-041):** `healthtab.ts` is the person sheet's Health tab (between Needs and Skills): a six-part SVG figure (red bleeding, amber healing, hatched lasting), the state sentence, the Blood bar with its faint tick (moved in place each snapshot, so an open "why?" chain stays), Wounds and Lasting marks each with "why?", the death line for the dead; all words are the worker's `WalkerView.health`. Nothing there orders the hurt or the dead (V-041); in test mode alone a TEST MODE row (part, severity, Hurt) sends `hurt {person, part, severity}`. The strip (`play.ts`) badges the hurt with a red "+", greys the dead with a "†" and drops the buried. A Work heading over one row (Food, Care) is a label with no stepper or fold; the Work sheet ends with the Care footnote from content (`health.words.workNote`).
- **Rules:** never computes a game rule (GAME-02); everything shown comes from a snapshot, an event line or a worker answer. Typing in the seed field is not a game key; Escape closes the panel.
- **Tests:** the browser smoke (title, arrival, inspector, "why?", save, reload, load, export, Continue, and at 390 px a person's tabs, the Work stepper and its Carpentry and Tending rows (42 px), the Firewood plate, Skip, the alerts and the Build sheet's Houses stepper).
