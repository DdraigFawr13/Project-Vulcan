# ui

The page entry, panels, inspector, ledger browser and controls. Sends commands to the worker and shows snapshots and events.

- **Public API:** none; `src/ui/main.ts` is the entry `index.html` loads.
- **Files:** `main.ts` (worker, renderer, keys, message routing), `picker.ts` and `regionmap.ts` (P1.5, below), `hud.ts` (clock with the level on screen, speed and panel buttons (Ledger, Region, Saves), readout, the level control above the zoom buttons, the not-reached note, screens), `panel.ts` (the side panel, a bottom sheet under 640 px wide: tile inspector, ledger with "why?", save list with New game), `saves.ts` (saving, loading, autosave, export download, the ledger's newest 200 lines), `dom.ts`.
- **Levels:** ▲ and ▼ (or `<` and `>`, PageUp and PageDown) step the view between the surface and the four levels below; taps inspect the level on screen. The tile panel's "Cut stairs down" and "Sink a shaft" buttons send the map's test orders for the next tick (they wait while paused) until colonists can dig.
- **Weather (P1.4):** the clock line adds the day's weather ("Winter · A blizzard blows, -7 °C"); the inspector adds the tile's temperature, ice and aura with the spot that gives it.
- **Seed and picker (P1.5):** the URL's `?seed=` (default `ledger-1`) opens `picker.ts`, the site picker: the region map (`regionmap.ts`: the worker's raster in the game's palette with peaks, trees and rivers, then names, race-coloured site glyphs and numbered valley markers as SVG with tap areas of about 40 px; labels that would collide are left off), a race legend, three valley cards (a thumbnail of the real map, climate, temperature, rain, biomes, lakes, springs, aura, nearest neighbours) and Settle here, New seed and a seed field. Settling sets `?seed=…&site=n`, so a reload begins that valley at once; the save panel's New game opens the picker for a new seed. The Region button opens the same map read-only in the panel with only the settled valley marked; a tapped site shows its culture, size, people, race, rule and faith, then its realm, founding year and history line (P1.6). Every site is named on the map (its label is placed before the rivers', above or below when the sides are taken); the legend lists only the races present.
- **Continue (Phase 1 review):** opening the bare link, or reloading a settled valley (`?seed=…&site=n`), loads the newest save (of that valley, or of any world) instead of a new Day 1; a save another version refuses starts the world afresh. A seed alone still opens the picker. The title screen's Continue is P2.1's.
- **Closing the tab:** every 5 s of play the page asks the worker for a standby copy and holds it unwritten; hiding, closing or reloading the tab writes the newest one into the autosave slot at once (unless a save as new exists), so at most 5 s of play is lost.
- **Debug:** `?debug` in the URL shows the fps and tick line and the inspector's chunk row; players see neither. The bar's height is passed to the renderer (`setTopInset`) so the fitted map starts below it.
- **Saves:** "Save now" and the autosave ask the worker for a `saved` record, add a name (seed and game date) and a thumbnail, and write it through persist. The autosave runs every 60 s of real time while the game runs and when the tab is hidden, into one slot, and skips if no tick has passed since the last one or since the world began or was loaded (so a fresh or just-loaded world never replaces it). Load pauses, sends the record to the worker and shows a loading screen; `ready {from: 'save'}` ends it, `loadRefused` shows the reason with the game paused behind it; closing it restores the speed.
- **Rules:** never computes a game rule (GAME-02); everything shown comes from a snapshot, an event line or a worker answer. Typing in the seed field is not a game key; Escape closes the panel.
- **Tests:** the browser smoke (inspector, "why?", save, reload, load, export).
