# ui

The page entry, panels, inspector, ledger browser and controls. Sends commands to the worker and shows snapshots and events.

- **Public API:** none; `src/ui/main.ts` is the entry `index.html` loads.
- **Files:** `main.ts` (worker, renderer, keys, message routing), `picker.ts` and `regionmap.ts` (P1.5, below), `hud.ts` (clock with the level on screen, speed and panel buttons (Ledger, Region, Saves), readout, the level control above the zoom buttons, the not-reached note, screens), `panel.ts` (the side panel, a bottom sheet under 640 px wide: tile inspector, ledger with "why?", save list with New game), `saves.ts` (saving, loading, autosave, export download, the ledger's newest 200 lines), `title.ts`, `arrival.ts`, `person.ts` and `portrait.ts` (P2.1, below), `dom.ts`.
- **Levels:** ▲ and ▼ (or `<` and `>`, PageUp and PageDown) step the view between the surface and the four levels below; taps inspect the level on screen. The tile panel's "Cut stairs down" and "Sink a shaft" buttons send the map's test orders for the next tick (they wait while paused) until colonists can dig.
- **Weather (P1.4):** the clock line adds the day's weather ("Winter · A blizzard blows, -7 °C"); the inspector adds the tile's temperature, ice and aura with the spot that gives it.
- **Seed and picker (P1.5):** the URL's `?seed=` (default `ledger-1`) opens `picker.ts`, the site picker: the region map (`regionmap.ts`: the worker's raster in the game's palette with peaks, trees and rivers, then names, race-coloured site glyphs and numbered valley markers as SVG with tap areas of about 40 px; labels that would collide are left off), a race legend, three valley cards (a thumbnail of the real map, climate, temperature, rain, biomes, lakes, springs, aura, nearest neighbours) and Settle here, New seed and a seed field. Settling sets `?seed=…&site=n`, so a reload begins that valley at once; the save panel's New game opens the picker for a new seed. The Region button opens the same map read-only in the panel with only the settled valley marked; a tapped site shows its culture, size, people, race, rule and faith, then its realm, founding year and history line (P1.6). Every site is named on the map (its label is placed before the rivers', above or below when the sides are taken); the legend lists only the races present.
- **Continue (Phase 1 review):** reloading a settled valley (`?seed=…&site=n`) loads its newest save instead of a new Day 1. A seed alone still opens the picker.
- **Title, band and arrival (P2.1):** the bare link shows `title.ts` ("Ledger", a working title) over the hidden HUD (`#hud.covered`): New game opens the picker; with a save, the newest of any world (RESCUE included) loads behind the dimmed title and Continue names its valley and date; a save the worker refuses leaves a plain sentence and New game only. A fresh world from a seed with people shows `arrival.ts`: the place and date, where the band came from, a card per person (portrait, race, culture, age, home, best three skills as level bars with a potential tick, traits, "can build" and "can cook" badges), who can build and cook, and Arrive. Tapping a person on the map opens their panel (`panel.showPerson`): name and home, distance, trade, every skill with its number, traits with their meaning, and "Came with the band" with "why?". `portrait.ts` draws a person from the `sprites-people` sheet with their colours. Names, trades and who is able come from the worker's `PersonView`s.
- **Closing the tab:** every 5 s of play the page asks the worker for a standby copy and holds it unwritten; hiding, closing or reloading the tab writes the newest one into the autosave slot at once (unless a save as new exists), so at most 5 s of play is lost.
- **Ledger archive and rescue (P2.0):** `segments` messages are archived through persist in order, and every save waits for the archive first, so no save names a day it lacks. Export downloads `<name>.ndjson.gz`: the stored gzipped days and the recent ones gzipped, joined without unpacking. On an engine error the newest standby copy, with the levers given since it was taken, goes to the rescue slot (a RESCUE row in Saves, loadable), and the error screen says which day it keeps. A failed archive write stays shown in Saves until reload. Known gap: a day sealed in the last moments before the tab closes may miss the archive (its gzip is still in flight); the export then says how many older days are missing.
- **Debug:** `?debug` in the URL shows the fps and tick line (with the worker's tick timing: mean and p95 ms over the last 1,000 ticks and the slowest system) and the inspector's chunk row; players see neither. The bar's height is passed to the renderer (`setTopInset`) so the fitted map starts below it.
- **Saves:** "Save now" and the autosave ask the worker for a `saved` record, add a name (seed and game date) and a thumbnail, and write it through persist. The autosave runs every 60 s of real time while the game runs and when the tab is hidden, into one slot, and skips if no tick has passed since the last one or since the world began or was loaded (so a fresh or just-loaded world never replaces it). Load pauses, sends the record to the worker and shows a loading screen; `ready {from: 'save'}` ends it, `loadRefused` shows the reason with the game paused behind it; closing it restores the speed.
- **Rules:** never computes a game rule (GAME-02); everything shown comes from a snapshot, an event line or a worker answer. Typing in the seed field is not a game key; Escape closes the panel.
- **Tests:** the browser smoke (title, arrival, inspector, "why?", save, reload, load, export, Continue).
