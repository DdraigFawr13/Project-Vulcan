# Milestone 1: tick loop, map, colonists, event log, pause/speed

Goal from [CONCEPT.md](../CONCEPT.md): a running colony to build the law and belief systems on.

## Scope

- Deterministic engine in a Web Worker: one tick is 6 game minutes, 10 ticks a second at 1×, speeds 1×/2×/5×/10×, pause and step one hour (D-001, D-007).
- 64×48 map from two noise fields: grass, water, rock; trees in moist ground, berry bushes scattered; a cleared camp with a hearth and a store in the middle.
- 10 founders with three needs (food, rest, company, 0-1000), a liked job, and a plan chosen each time they are idle.
- Jobs as data: foraging (bush → food) and woodcutting (tree → wood), each with a stock target that sets demand. Stoking the hearth, eating, sleeping, chatting and wandering are built in.
- Event log with cause links and template text, a chronicle panel, a per-colonist inspector, and NDJSON export.

## Defaults picked (no owner input needed; say if any is wrong)

- Event log in memory with NDJSON export; sql.js waits for approval (D-005).
- Plain DOM and canvas, no UI framework (D-006).
- Starving or exhausted colonists don't die yet: death is milestone 4.
- The hearth keeps sleepers warm (rest refills about 70% faster). It burns 8 wood a day and nobody tends it at night, so it usually goes out before dawn. That is the first unattended chore a law could assign.
- Trees don't regrow. Bushes regrow one meal every 1.5 days.

## Open questions for milestone 2

- Food is easy: the store sits at its cap of 60 meals and most colonists idle by day. A food-allocation law needs scarcity to matter. Recommended: tune bush yield down in milestone 2's spec, with the law, rather than now.
- Wood runs out between day 164 and day 278 and the hearth stays cold after. Recommended: add slow tree regrowth in milestone 2 so the forest is a managed commons, not a countdown.
