# Wild valley and the flock, the people's side

Moved out of `src/engine/people/MODULE.md` (2026-10-26) so milestone reading lists stay under budget; read it when working on hunting, fishing, fleeing, the pack's attacks or herding.

- **The wild valley, the people's side (P5.4a, V-041, V-072, V-109 to V-112, V-122):** `hunt.ts`, `fish.ts` (Wild heading, Hunt and Fish rows after Forage; content `work.hunt`, `work.fish`, `wild.folk`). While food is under `from` per cent of its Food plate a free able person takes up hunting (`work.tookUpHunting`; at most `crew`): the largest herd worth the hunt (`huntable`: never one at its last `spare`), nearest the green among equals, stalked games before hares; they walk to it and, within `near` tiles of a calm herd, stalk it: every `every` minutes a kill chance of `chance` + `perLevel` per level of hunting, times the spear's speed (`killAnimal`, the meat and leftovers lie for haulers), else the herd bolts (`scareHerd`). A stalking hunter does not frighten a herd (wild leaves `job: hunt` persons out of the frightened). Hares are snared, one an `snare.every` minutes. A hunter on fresh hoof prints (`follow.hours`) turns to the herd they lead to (`work.followedTracks`, once a hunt). If the pack attacked within `tracks.packDays`, two who put Hunt above `pack.level` and see fresh paw prints near the town go after it (`work.trackingPack`; task `pack`). Hunting and fishing end at dusk and when the plate is full. Fishing: the nearest unfrozen stream, river or lake tile reachable, a catch chance each `every` minutes (`fish.chance` + `perLevel`), the fish laid by the angler (state `fished` counts them); frozen water ends it. `tracksMinute` (each minute, anyone on the surface): fresh wolf prints within the ring + `tracks.ring` write `wild.tracksFound` (once a game day, citing the pack's hungry line) and give `saw-tracks`.
- **Fight or flee, and the pack's attacks (V-109, V-111):** `flee.ts` `predatorRung`, the first thing `care.ts` `danger` asks. Awake on the surface, with a predator not at its den (`folk.den`) in sight (`folk.sight`, day or night): stand if bold (a `folk.bold` trait, no `timid` one), armed (`folk.armed` tool kind held) and another within `together` tiles is standing or bold and armed; else flee to the nearest closed house (`roomAt`) or away. `doing` is `stand` or `flee` while one stays in sight. In a shut room someone fleeing stays in, and at their bedtime (their hours' night, or rest under its `seek`) lies down there; a sleeper there with a predator in sight does not get up for food, and wakes to `flee` once rested by day. A stander makes for shelter at bedtime. Everyone newly in sight decides together; one `wild.sighted` line an hour names them (citing the pack's edge or hungry line); the standers get `stood-together`. `fight.ts`: with pack hunger at `attackAfter` and night, a wolf within `prowl` tiles walks towards, and at one tile falls on, a person who is alone in the open or the last fleeing to shelter (`exposed`): up to `rounds` blows; each blow standing armed people beside the target strike first (`strike`, by hunting level and weapon), a hit hurts the wolf (the second kills it, wild `woundPredator`); a miss, and it may bite (`bite`: part and depth by weight, into `health` `wound`). One `wild.attacked` line citing the sighting, `markAttack`, `saw-attack` for the awake within `witness`, then `drawOff` (`wild.packDrewOff`). `syncLures` tells wild where the midden is.
- **Herding (Phase 5 Bundle 3, V-041):** `herd.ts` + `herdtask.ts`, a Work sheet kind after Farming (`herd`, the farming skill; content `flock`). Nobody is ordered; the Herding row is the only lever. Two jobs are the day's own and come before the herder's other work, even outside work hours: *fold* from `night.from` less `fold.before` minutes while a flock has animals outside the fold and is not folded (shut it in, to `night.to` plus `fold.open`), and *let out* after that while it is folded. The free herder who puts Herding highest (ties in person order; not carrying, not at work they rank above Herding) takes it; from `fold.wait` minutes into the duty's time any free herder may; either only with a way to beside the fold gate. They walk there, work `fold.minutes` and call `foldFlock` / `openFlock`. At `night.from` `herdWatch` writes `noteLeftOut` once with the reason of whoever holds the fold duty, else the best-placed herder (`why`: abed, warming, eating, toFold, busy, noWay, none). At most `perWorkplace` (jobs.json `herd`) herders work one flock at once (the fold duty aside). Work-hours tasks in order: butcher a marked animal (`cull.minutes`; whoever tamed it gets `memories.cull`), shear (`shear.minutes`), carry cured hay to the rick (the `hay` person field, `hay.carry` a trip), mow (`hay.mow` minutes shorter with farming skill), tame (at most `tame.crew` at once on a flock; walk to `flee + 2` tiles of the herd's nearest animal, sit `tame.session` minutes a sitting, then look again later like any finished work; the total `sat` for the herd `herdId` reaching `tame.youngMinutes` in a `tame.young` month or `tame.adultMinutes` calls `takeIn`; another herd starts again). Fields `task` (`fold` `unfold` `butcher` `shear` `hay` `mow` `tame`), `plan` (the flock), `target` (gate tile, animal, tile or wild herd), `hay`, `herdId`, `sat`. When a flock's `takenLine` changes every herder and each tamer gets `memories.taken`.
