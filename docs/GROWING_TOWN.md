# The growing town

Detail for Phase 6D (`docs/phases/PHASE_6D.md`), from Morgan's design form (V-244) and the spec form (V-245). Each slice's build thread reads its own section. What the game already does, read from the code on 2026-11-28 (main `6f02f10`): homes are sited round the green inside a ring that widens when full (`build/ring.ts`, `site.ts` `chooseHouse`, scored for level ground, nearness to the green and trees, leaning toward the owner's work); once every founder has a home the first house becomes the hall (`homes.ts`, feasts and mourning); worn paths become roads (`roads.ts`); stores by kind are sited within `buildings.stores.near` (40) of the green; the smithy and tavern keep apart from homes; the elder is voted every 7 days (`colony/vote.ts`); the lord's charter gives a founders'-tongue name (V-242). There is no wind and no rest day.

## Systems (VR-62)

- **Stages (Slice 1).** Owns the stage in a new `town` system's state and its line. Content `town.json` `stages`: Camp (the start), Hamlet (every household has a home of its own), Village (about 12 living, a well, a shrine or a tavern, a road to the green), Market town (about 20 living and the lord's market right). Sizes are data, tuned at the 6D balance. Daily check; reaching a stage writes `town.stage` citing the last thing it waited on, gives a feast under the Feasts law (the hall's harvest feast, reused) and opens that stage's wants (Hall, Lanes, Market, works), never builds them. A stage falls back the same way (Slice 7). Reads: living count, homes (`beds.ts`), wells (`well.ts`), shrine (faith), tavern, roads, the lord's ladder. Fact home: the stage id and its line in `town` state; what each stage needs in `town.json`. Fails: a valley that never reaches Village stays a hamlet, with no line saying it failed (the stage page says what it waits on). Test: a hamlet with 12 living, a well, a shrine and a road becomes a village with a line citing the last of them.
- **Streets (Slice 1).** Owns the street weights in `chooseHouse`: from Hamlet (spec form q2) a home plot scores for a door within `town.street.door` tiles of a built or planned road or lane, and a wall within `town.street.beside` tiles of a neighbour's (but not touching, so a fire gap is kept from Slice 5), weighed by the Town ways answer. When no road-side plot is left within the ring, `planLane` plans a lane: a `road` row (`kind` lane) from the nearest road toward the best free ground, laid by builders under the Roads row; homes then line it. The High Street: the most walked road from where the home road enters to the green, laid `town.high.width` tiles wide. Reads: `roads.ts` (wear, plans), `site.ts`, the ring, the Town ways answer. Fact home: lane rows in the `road` table; weights in `town.json`. Fails: no stone for a lane, it stays a worn path and homes still line it; ground too steep, the home falls back to the old scoring with a line. Test: a village home's door is on a road or lane and its `build.homeSited` line says so.
- **The Town ways law (Slice 1).** A law in `laws.json`: Wherever suits (the old scoring), Along the lanes (the default from the street stage), Set out from the square (lanes run straight out from the green, homes face them). Weighed like every law (`law/answer`): a builder who obeys sites by it; one with a grievance against it, or a trait the setting's `traits` lean against (a restless or solitary builder under Along the lanes, as every law's settings have), may site a home where the ground suits them best, with a line naming the trait or the grievance. Test: Town ways answers are law events; a grumbling builder's home cites the law.
- **Work by its ground (Slice 2).** Owns quarters and yards. Noisy and smelly work (smithy, tan pits, kiln, midden) sits at the edge of the ring farthest from most homes (the smithy's `apart` rule, widened); crafts sit near the homes of those who work them. A work whose ground is far (spec form q1) is set up by that ground: when the clay dug in a season was walked more than `town.yard.walk` steps a load (tallied from the haul), the colony sites a yard by the clay bank (a clay heap, the kiln and a drying floor), the potter and brickmaker work there, and only pots and bricks are carried to town. The same rule serves lime by the limestone. Reads: land `clay.ts`, haul walks (band `haul.ts`), stores by kind, `metalsite.ts`. Fact home: a `yard` row (kind, x, y, line) in build; thresholds in `town.json`. Fails: a bank too wet to build by, the yard goes to the nearest dry ground within `town.yard.reach`; the bank is dug out, the yard is left (a ruin in Slice 7). Test: a far bank gets a yard with a line citing the steps walked; clay is not carried to town while the yard stands.
- **The hall (Slice 3).** Owns the hall's steps. Step 1: the first house (today). Step 2: when at a moot `hall.outside` or more stood outside, the hall adds a room (`grow.ts`'s rule, citing the moot). Step 3: at Village, a Hall want on the Build sheet cites the overflow ("6 of 13 stood outside in the rain"); the colony sites a hall of `hall.size` on the edge of the green, door facing it (`chooseHouse` with a `facing` hint), and the old hall becomes a home again. Holds: moots, feasts, mourning, the lord's visit and the reeve's count (6C), the charter and the chronicle on a shelf, later carvings (Phase 8). Fact home: the hall's house row (`hall` line) as today. Test: an overflowing moot brings a room with a line; at Village the hall want cites it.
- **The moot (Slice 3).** Owns the weekly meeting (spec form q4): every seventh evening, after work hours. At `moot.hour` people walk to the hall; each chooses to go by mood, standing and grudges: a person with a grudge against the elder or a law may stay home, a line. At the moot: the elder vote (today's weekly vote moves here, so the elder is chosen by those who came), grudges and a faction's petition are heard (`colony` petitions), a law the player changed since the last moot is talked over (a nudge to its answers, both ways, by who spoke). The elder's chair: the elder sits `moot.chair` hours a week in the hall; a grudge heard there fades `moot.heard` times faster, one ignored grows. Slack-season works (spec form q5). Reads: colony (elder, factions, petitions, standing), law answers, defy grievances. Fact home: a `moot` table (day, came, stayed, heard, line). Fails: nobody comes (all grudging, or a storm), a quiet moot line, the vote waits a week, except after the elder's death, when it is held among whoever is home that evening. Test: a moot counts the vote among those who came; a grudge holder stays home with a line citing the grudge.
- **Growing pains (Slice 5).** Owns strains and the works they bring. Each strain is tallied from what colonists do and feel, with a content entry in `town.json` `strains` (what is counted, over how many days, the threshold, the want it brings, its Build row): minutes waited at the well (a second well near the newest homes, `well.ts`); the midden's smell near homes and sickness after it (cesspits behind homes, then a drain to the river); thatch roofs within a tile of each other, standing in for the design's fire near-miss until Phase 8 brings fire (a gap kept between rows from then on, fire buckets with Phase 8); strayed beasts in a field (a pound by the pasture); carts and stalls crowding the green on market day (the High Street widened and a cart yard, Slice 6, once there is a market); evening walks after dark (lamps at the hall door and the market cross, `lamp`); a crossing walked many times a day (stepping stones, then a bridge, a new `paths` cost). Every want names its strain on the Build sheet and in its line. Fails: no material, the want waits with a line. Test: a well queue over the threshold brings a second well whose line cites the minutes.
- **The pedlar and the market (Slice 6).** Owns trade before coin. A pedlar or a neighbour's cart (from a region neighbour, whose wares follow its land) stops on the green `market.pedlar.perYear` times a year and swaps what the store holds above its stock level for what the colony lacks (salt, iron, cloth, seed), at fixed swap values in content. The lord's ladder gains a market right step (listed in favour order with the others, as the Laws sheet shows them) (above favour 75 and at Village; 75 is a spec default between the free year at 70 and the charter at 80, tuned at the slice's mock-up); with it a market day each month, more carts, now and then a traveller who asks to stay (the Strangers law answers), and wants for the square (the green paved), stalls and a market cross. The Punishment law's stocks stand by the cross, so a sentence is seen. Fact home: `market` state (pitches, swaps, the right); swap values and wares in `town.json` `market`. Fails: nothing above stock levels, the pedlar leaves with a line; the right taken back (favour falls), market days stop. Test: a pedlar swaps only above the stock level, the line naming both sides.
- **Names, ruin and the fall (Slice 7).** At Village the colonists name the town from their own story (the first wedding, a landmark, a founder; spec form q3); lanes take names from what is on them (Smith Lane, Well Street, Weaver's Row). A stage falls back when its needs are no longer met, with a line citing the deaths or leavings; an empty home stands cold for `ruin.cold` days, then its roof and walls go (`ruin` state on the house row), a place colonists name in tales; the market right taken back empties the square; a hall with no moot for `ruin.quiet` weeks goes quiet. Fact home: names on the `town` state and `road` rows; ruin on house rows. Test: a stage drop cites its cause; an empty home becomes a ruin with a line and a tale.
- **On screen (Slices 4 and 8).** Slice 4: the stage under the colony's name and in recaps; the Town ways row on the Laws sheet; the Build sheet's Hall and Lanes rows with their reasons; the hall page (the last moot: who came, who stayed away and why, what was heard); lanes and the hall drawn. Slice 8: strains on the Build sheet; the market page (pedlars and swaps); street names on the map; art for the square, stalls, cross, wells, cesspits, pound, lamps, stepping stones, bridge and ruins (`art-sheet` skill). Every lever works while paused.

## Spec form answers (2026-11-28, V-245)

Form https://claude.ai/artifact/9MaE7Ewton5wvaN96bLFK5, every recommendation ("Go with recommended for all"). Measured before it (main `6f02f10`, default levers, two to three years on the six balance valleys): the middle home door 9 to 19 tiles from the green, the farthest 16 to 25, the middle gap to the nearest house 2 to 6 tiles, and on anvil/1 and crow/1 about half the doors with no laid road within 2 tiles. Runner and data: project files `vulcan/p6d-spec/`.

- **q1 A, work goes to its ground.** A yard by the far clay bank (clay heap, kiln, drying floor); the potter and brickmaker work there; only pots and bricks are carried to town. Lime by the limestone the same way.
- **q2 A, streets from Hamlet.** Once every household has a home of its own, new homes score a door on a road and a neighbour beside them, shaped by the Town ways law (amends V-244's "from Village").
- **q3 A, the colonists name it.** At Village, from their own story; the charter confirms that name. The founders'-tongue name (V-242) only for a valley chartered before it is a village.
- **q4 A, every seventh evening.** After work hours; no rest day. Going costs an evening's leisure, so some stay home.
- **q5 A, the moot picks a common work.** When many stood idle that week, one work from the felt strains goes first on the Build sheet for the season, with a line citing the idle hours and the strain; a row the player sets lower stays where they put it.
- **q6 A, set numbers.** Three game years on the six balance valleys: idle under 30% of work hours on every valley, walking down at least 10 points against the 6C numbers; a miss is fixed before the phase is handed over.
- **q7 A, streets first.** The slice order in the phase spec.
