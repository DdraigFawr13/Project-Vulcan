# Herding

Phase 5 Bundle 3 (V-041), people `herd.ts` and `herdtask.ts`; the flock itself is `src/engine/flock/MODULE.md`. Moved here from the people module note to keep reading lists short.

`herd.ts` + `herdtask.ts`, a Work sheet kind after Farming (`herd`, the farming skill; content `flock`). Nobody is ordered; the Herding row is the only lever. Two jobs are the day's own and come before the herder's other work, even outside work hours: *fold* from `night.from` less `fold.before` minutes while a flock has animals outside the fold and is not folded (shut it in, to `night.to` plus `fold.open`), and *let out* after that while it is folded. The free herder who puts Herding highest (ties in person order; not carrying, not at work they rank above Herding) takes it; from `fold.wait` minutes into the duty's time any free herder may; either only with a way to beside the fold gate. They walk there, work `fold.minutes` and call `foldFlock` / `openFlock`. At `night.from` `herdWatch` writes `noteLeftOut` once with the reason of whoever holds the fold duty, else the best-placed herder (`why`: abed, warming, eating, toFold, busy, noWay, none). At most `perWorkplace` (jobs.json `herd`) herders work one flock at once (the fold duty aside). Work-hours tasks in order: butcher a marked animal (`cull.minutes`; whoever tamed it gets `memories.cull`), shear (`shear.minutes`), carry cured hay to the rick (the `hay` person field, `hay.carry` a trip), mow (`hay.mow` minutes shorter with farming skill), tame (at most `tame.crew` at once on a flock; walk to `flee + 2` tiles of the herd's nearest animal, sit `tame.session` minutes a sitting, then look again later like any finished work; the total `sat` for the herd `herdId` reaching `tame.youngMinutes` in a `tame.young` month or `tame.adultMinutes` calls `takeIn`; another herd starts again). Fields `task` (`fold` `unfold` `butcher` `shear` `hay` `mow` `tame`), `plan` (the flock), `target` (gate tile, animal, tile or wild herd), `hay`, `herdId`, `sat`. When a flock's `takenLine` changes every herder and each tamer gets `memories.taken`.
