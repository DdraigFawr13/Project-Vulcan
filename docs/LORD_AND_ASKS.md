# The lord, asks and discoveries

Detail for Phase 6C (`docs/phases/PHASE_6C.md`), from Morgan's design form (V-210) and the spec form (V-211). Each slice's build thread reads its own section.

## Systems (VR-62)

- **The lord (Slice 1).** Owns the colony's lord: the ruler (`Civ.rulerId`) of the realm of the nearest region neighbour that has one, with their temper (`Person.temper`, one of bold, pious, greedy, cruel, cautious, learned) and byname, read from the pinned `story-generator` package (checked 2026-11-22: commit `6430856` carries both, `src/content/tempers.ts`). The lord is fixed for the run; a new heir waits for Phase 9's moving world (e8 Later). Reads region `neighboursOf`, the calendar's harvest home. The reeve, the lord's man, rides in by the home road the morning after the first harvest home, names the lord and what is owed from next year, and comes each harvest home after with the cart. Fact home: the lord's id and temper in a new `lord` system's state (the region is not saved, so the lord is copied once at arrival); temper effects in content `lord.json` `tempers`. Fails: a valley whose window has no realm takes the nearest realm in the world (small default; region test already shows a realm in every window). Test: a seed's lord is the nearest realm's ruler, and the reeve's line follows the first harvest home.
- **The tithe, the Lord law and favour (Slice 2).** Owns the tithe owed each harvest home: a share of grain, wool, ore or ironwork, from the lord's temper and what the colony made that year (a richer colony pays more). Colonists site a tithe barn (a Build sheet want, like the tavern) and haul to it; the Stock sheet shows the tithe as a reserved row with its cause. The **Lord law** (Loyal / Grudging / Defiant, `laws.json`) is weighed like every law: Loyal pays in full and works asks, Grudging pays the least the reeve takes, Defiant pays nothing; a hungry family may hide grain under Loyal (defiance's take, V-174, the line citing the memory and belief), a loyal smith may pay their share under Defiant. **Favour** is one number like standing (V-173), made of causes that count for their days: raised by full tithes, asks done and gifts; lowered by shortfalls, asks let pass, hidden goods the reeve finds and Defiant. High favour grants charters (content entries: a tithe-free year, a market right, the lord's forest if spec form q4 keeps it); low favour raises the tithe, then a fine, then the lord's men take goods by force (spec form q2). From Phase 8, his men at a raid or an outlawed valley. A heavy tithe in a lean year is a standing cause and a grievance, so factions and leavers get new reasons. Fails: an empty barn at the reeve's visit is a shortfall line, never a death. Test: a shortfall lowers favour with a line naming the sacks owed and counted.
- **Asks (Slice 3).** Owns asks: content `asks.json` entries with a source kind (lord, neighbour, wanderer, faith, ambition), what is wanted (goods, a fine thing of a quality, hosting a guest for a season, days of work, a building), a reason taken from a record (a neighbour's famine in its history, a priest's want for a bell, a colonist's ambition), a deadline, what it brings and what letting it pass costs. Every ask cites the line it came from; none come from nowhere. The player takes an ask up or lets it pass and gives a taken ask a priority of 1 to 4 like a Build row; colonists pick it up through their own job choice, weighting by trade, traits and what they think of the asker (a colony that dislikes the lord works slowly on his asks). Letting an ask pass costs favour or a neighbour's regard, never a person. Lord asks start here; sending men to war (Phase 8) and errands abroad (Phase 9) plug in later. Fails: an ask whose deadline passes undone is a line and the pass cost. Test: a taken-up lord's ask for a fine blade is made by the colony's best smith unordered, and its line cites the ask.
- **The lord and asks on screen (Slice 4).** The Asks sheet (⋯ menu): open asks with asker, reason (why?), deadline, reward and cost, Take up / Let pass and a 1 to 4 stepper; taken and closed asks below. The Laws sheet gains the Lord law row and the favour number with its causes; the Stock sheet the tithe row; the Region panel names the lord, temper and byname; the reeve walks on the map (spec form q1). Every lever works while paused.
- **Hints and breakthroughs (Slice 5, V-142).** Owns methods and each person's hints: content `methods.json` (about 12, spread over the trades the colony has; list below), each with the hints it needs (3 to 6), the kinds that give one (long work in its trade, a failure that teaches, something seen on a stranger or caravan, a book, a visitor from an ask, a tale) and its effect as content (faster or better work in a job, a lower wall leak, a longer keep, rarely a new recipe). Luck (Morgan's note): each working day in the trade has a small chance of a free hint, a little more for the lucky, and a rare dream after long work on one problem gives the last one. At the last hint comes the breakthrough: a heavy memory, a line naming every hint, a tale that spreads and sometimes a byname. Who knows a method is spec form q3. The **New Ways law** (Old ways only / Allowed / Encouraged): Encouraged gives hints faster but spends time and materials; a faith that holds tradition sacred leans against it, so a faction can form. Trade priority speeds hints too. Chasing a breakthrough can go wrong: ruined materials, a burned hand, a small fire, each a line and a tale. Fails: no work in a trade, no hints. Test: a smith with the method's hints gains it with a line citing each hint.
- **Discoveries on screen (Slice 6).** The person inspector shows "puzzling over harder iron, 3 of 5 hints" with why?; the ledger and recaps carry breakthroughs; the Laws sheet gains New Ways.
- **Extras (Slice 7, form e1 to e8).** The lord visits once in a while: the colony prepares a feast by the Feasts law, his mood moves favour, someone may petition or insult him. Petition: after a bad harvest the elder asks for a lower tithe; the answer follows temper and favour and is remembered. Wandering masters: a mason, scholar or tinker asks (an ask) to stay a season; housed and fed, they teach, leave hints and may stay. Contests at feasts: ploughing, ale, the strongest arm, with bynames, rivals and the lord's prize. The valley's charter: at high favour and a size, the lord grants a name and a charter, a lasting ledger mark. Later (not 6C): keeping secrets (Methods law), outlaws in the woods, a rival lord or heir.

The first dozen methods (content, numbers at Slice 5's mock-up): steel (smith), a heavier plough (farm), resting a field each third year (farm), better mill wheel (mill), treadle loom (weave), lime mortar (mason, lower leak), herb remedies (heal), glazed pots (potter, longer keep), a better charcoal clamp (charcoal), bark tanning (tan), bee skeps that winter well (beekeep), and a cleaner smelt (smelt). Job ids copied from `jobs.json`.

## Spec form answers (2026-11-23, V-211)

- **q1 A, the reeve walks in.** He comes by the home road with a cart and a guard, stays the day, counts the tithe barn and leaves; tapping him opens a page like a stranger's.
- **q2 A, goods and beatings.** At the bottom of the ladder the lord's men take about twice what was owed from the store and barn; anyone who stands in their way may be beaten (wounds with a line, nobody killed). No hostages.
- **q3 A, the finder, then teaching.** A method is known only by its finder; others learn it as their apprentice or from a book the teacher writes (V-189, V-199); a finder who dies untaught takes it with them (V-141).
- **q4 A, the lord's forest.** The old woods farthest from the green are the lord's; hunting or felling there is poaching, a defiance some do anyway (the hungry, the defiant); favour falls if the reeve hears; a charter makes it the colony's. One content entry for the forest, one for the charter.

## Slice 5 mock-up answers (2026-11-23, V-218)

Form https://claude.ai/artifact/3BKNdjhaQvjpUZpT8d9i3u, every recommendation. Ten game years on the four test worlds (main 08f2f64, after the newcomers fix), hint rules estimated on top: 7, 5, 5 and 5 finds (anvil, smoke, tallow, ledger-1), the last in year 6 to 10; 0 to 3 mishaps a world (3 to 6 under Encouraged); 24 to 63 strangers came. Source, scratch model and runs: project files `vulcan/p6c-slice5/`.

- **Pace (q1 A):** a work hint every 120 hours at the trade; Encouraged two thirds of that.
- **The twelve (q2 A):** the list below, with skill gates and hints steel 6/5, cleaner smelt 5/4, heavier plough 5/4, resting a field 4/3, mill wheel any/4, treadle loom 5/4, lime mortar 5/4, herb remedies 4/4, glazed pots 5/4, charcoal clamp 4/3, bark tanning 4/3, skeps 4/3. Effects, only for a knower's work: tools wear half as fast; one more ingot in four smelts; sowing and reaping a fifth faster; a resting field's soil back twice as fast; grinding a quarter faster; weaving a third faster; stone walls lose a third less heat; dressed wounds and fevers heal a quarter faster; food in their pots spoils half as fast; a quarter more charcoal a clamp; hides tan in two thirds the time; half as many skeps lost over winter. Numbers tuned at build.
- **Luck (q3 A):** 1 in 100 working days at the trade; twice that for a Lucky or Twice-spared byname (tales.json). No new trait.
- **Mishaps (q4 A):** on 1 in 6 work hints (1 in 4 under Encouraged): ruined materials; at forge, smelter, clamp or kiln sometimes a burned hand (a wound) or a small fire; nobody killed; each a line and a tale.
- **New Ways (q5 A):** Allowed is the custom; Encouraged costs one working hour in eight and more mishaps; Old ways only starts no puzzle and gives no luck to those who keep it; faiths holding learning sacred lean for Encouraged, order against.
- **Spreading (q6 B, widens spec form q3):** besides an apprentice in the skill and a book, anyone who works the same trade on the same days as a knower for about 200 hours learns the method; it is still lost if every knower dies or leaves first.
- **Build notes:** one puzzle a person at a time; 40 h at the trade in four weeks to start one; nobody puzzles over a method a living colonist knows, and a find ends others' puzzles on it; failure hints at most one a person a 28 days; milling and healing are short jobs, so count their work from their lines; a stranger's coming gives each person puzzling a seen hint at 1 in 8, at most 2 towards one method (default; seen hints are the second source after work in the runs).

## Slice 1 mock-up answers (2026-11-23, V-216)

Form https://claude.ai/artifact/REXNtmdRPMMK3vJr8pGBha, every recommendation. Measured on main (valley 1 of each test world): Ogrgul (Horde of Ashaglug, bold), Tarainach (Queendom of Esgairarrig, cautious), Gunnil the Builder (Jarldom of Brandby, bold, aged 10), Yarknak (Lordship of Rustnakpit, greedy); first harvest home day 142 or 143.

- **No byname:** the name alone; the reeve's line gives the temper's words (the package's temper `line`).
- **A child ruler** stays lord; the reeve speaks for "the young jarl and those who rule for him". Nothing in 6C harms the child.
- **The news:** each colonist gets a memory, weighed by the lord's temper and bitter for anyone not of the lord's people.
- **Visits:** every harvest home from the first, the morning after; he counts the store in a line and takes nothing until Slice 2.

- **Build notes:** skip a realm with `fell`; a plain seed has no lord (test with a valley seed); no test valley has a pious, cruel or learned lord, so temper tests make one in the test.

## Slice 2 mock-up answers (2026-11-23, V-222)

Form https://claude.ai/artifact/91We7kCTAGYGzkkTaCU8DD, every recommendation. Measured on main (five years on anvil/1, smoke/1, ledger-1/1, four on tallow/1, no tithe): three of four valleys went hungry in some year; a tenth came to 23 to 40 days' eating (estimate, 4 food a person a day).

- **Size and goods:** a tenth of all brought in since the reeve's last visit (each harvest less seed kept, fleece and wool, ingots, iron things); 15 in 100 for greedy or cruel; flax, logs and stone untithed.
- **The barn:** each harvest's share is hauled to the tithe barn as it comes in; the store cannot eat it; the reeve counts the barn.
- **Lord law:** starts Loyal at the first visit, answered by everyone then (not a custom); Grudging pays half; Defiant nothing.
- **Hiding:** at defiance's take step, or hungry while the store will not last, a colonist takes from the barn and hides it at home; under Defiant one who wants the lord paid carries their share. Missing goods are short; one visit in three the reeve searches, and a stash found is a line naming what and whose house.
- **Favour:** start 50; full tithe +6, each tenth short −1, Defiant −10, unpaid fine −10, stash found −8, seen poaching told −4; each cause lasts three years. Ladder: 35 tithe raised by half, 20 a fine (one more tithe), 10 the lord's men (V-211); 70 a tithe-free year, 85 the lord's forest. Paying alone peaks near 68, so charters also need asks (Slice 3). The market right waits for trade.
- **Lean year:** the store will not last to the harvest: cautious, pious and learned lords take half and say so, bold, greedy and cruel all; either way a standing cause and a memory against the Lord law for each grown-up.
- **Forest:** in this slice; woodcutters and hunters keep out unless hungry or set against the Lord law; someone Loyal who sees a poacher tells the reeve (−4, a line naming the deed).
- **Estimated paths (five years):** Defiant meets the lord's men in year 5 on two valleys; Grudging is raised from year 4; Loyal stays 46 to 68. Source and runs: /mnt/project-files/vulcan/p6c-s2-mockup/.

## Slice 3 mock-up answers (2026-11-23, V-225)

Form https://claude.ai/artifact/RHuQLTHnr7pqrCYrjjoxKQ, every recommendation. Measured on main 59cbaef: five years on anvil/1, smoke/1, ledger-1/1, three on tallow/1 (6 to 22 people), default levers, no tithe or asks; the asks are estimated over the runs (source, runs and model: project files `vulcan/p6c-s3-mockup/`).

- **Pace (q1):** each asker its moment: the lord one at each tithe visit, due at the next; a village when a stranger from there comes with its trouble (its `strangers.reasons` kind), one a village a year, due in 84 days; a turned-away stranger may ask to be a guest, one a season; the shrine's priest once a year at harvest home; a dream (ambition) 56 days unfulfilled. Estimated 24 or 25 asks in five years on anvil, smoke and ledger-1, guests the biggest share.
- **The lord's ask (q2):** by temper, entries in `asks.json`: bold a fine blade for his hall; cruel the same due by midwinter; greedy two good metal things over the tithe; cautious a twentieth of the year's food; pious twenty candles; learned a book. A blade (2 ingots) is a new `tools.json` kind made only for asks.
- **Favour (q3):** done +6, let pass −4, taken up and late −6 (cruel doubles both costs), each a favour cause for three years, settled at his next visit. Estimated with full tithes: smoke 80 in year 4, 86 in year 5; ledger-1 75; anvil 69.
- **Regard (q4):** per village, from 50: done +10, let pass −5, late −7; done sends a gift back with the village's next stranger by its kind (mine ore, farming village seed, herding village wool). Later phases (fair, begging) read it. Village asks by trouble: hunger food (a tenth of the store), sickness herbs or a healer's kit, war two spears, feud a bed for a family for a season.
- **Guests (q5):** any stranger turned away may ask to stay a season; taken up, they stay in the hall, eat from the store and work a little; those who voted against get a sour memory; at the end they leave a tale (and a seen hint, Slice 5) and the colony votes again.
- **Shrine and dreams (q6):** the priest asks for the faith's first `sacred` tag: war a fine spear, honour a fine carved piece, kin a second feast at harvest home, learning candles, order the shrine rebuilt in stone, wealth two ingots, freedom a cask of ale; a good memory for the faithful if done, a sour one if let pass. A dream's ask sets aside what it needs and the dreamer and trade-mates work it at its priority; a memory either way. Saying no costs goodwill, never a person.
- **Who works it (q7):** a taken ask is a work row at its priority; a free colonist whose trade fits takes it, most skilled first. Under the Lord law, grumblers work his asks a quarter slower, refusers never; shrine asks by its faithful only; a village's by anyone, its own people first.
- **Handing over (q8):** goods are carried to the tithe barn set apart; the reeve takes the lord's at his next visit; someone from the village walks in by the home road at the deadline for theirs; the shrine's is laid at the shrine; anything set apart can be taken by the hungry or angry, like the tithe, and is then short.
- **Build notes:** on anvil, smoke and ledger-1 every stranger was turned away (41 of 41, 28 for sickness); all 36 dreams came true on their own (5 to 140 days, 13 over 56); the colonies smith bronze, so the blade is bronze; tallow/1 smithed nothing in three years; a `self.role` line rendered a literal "{about}" (ledger-1/1, a memory's placeholder unfilled where roles are written).
