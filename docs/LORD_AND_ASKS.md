# The lord, asks and discoveries

Detail for Phase 6C (`docs/phases/PHASE_6C.md`), from Morgan's design form (V-210) and the spec form (V-211). Each slice's build thread reads its own section.

## Systems (VR-62)

- **The lord (Slice 1).** Owns the colony's lord: the ruler (`Civ.rulerId`) of the realm of the nearest region neighbour that has one, with their temper (`Person.temper`, one of bold, pious, greedy, cruel, cautious, learned) and byname, read from the pinned `story-generator` package (checked 2026-11-22: commit `6430856` carries both, `src/content/tempers.ts`). The lord is fixed for the run; a new heir waits for Phase 9's moving world (e8 Later). Reads region `neighboursOf`, the calendar's harvest home. The reeve, the lord's man, rides in by the home road the morning after the first harvest home, names the lord and what is owed from next year, and comes each harvest home after with the cart. Fact home: the lord's id and temper in a new `lord` system's state (the region is not saved, so the lord is copied once at arrival); temper effects in content `lord.json` `tempers`. Fails: a valley whose window has no realm takes the nearest realm in the world (small default; region test already shows a realm in every window). Test: a seed's lord is the nearest realm's ruler, and the reeve's line follows the first harvest home.
- **The tithe, the Lord law and favour (Slice 2).** Owns the tithe owed each harvest home: a share of grain, wool, ore or ironwork, from the lord's temper and what the colony made that year (a richer colony pays more). Colonists site a tithe barn (a Build sheet want, like the tavern) and haul to it; the Stock sheet shows the tithe as a reserved row with its cause. The **Lord law** (Loyal / Grudging / Defiant, `laws.json`) is weighed like every law: Loyal pays in full and works asks, Grudging pays the least the reeve takes, Defiant pays nothing; a hungry family may hide grain under Loyal (defiance's take, V-174, the line citing the memory and belief), a loyal smith may pay their share under Defiant. **Favour** is one number like standing (V-173), made of causes that count for their days: raised by full tithes, asks done and gifts; lowered by shortfalls, asks let pass, hidden goods the reeve finds and Defiant. High favour grants charters (content entries: a tithe-free year, a market right, the lord's forest if spec form q4 keeps it); low favour raises the tithe, then a fine, then the lord's men take goods by force (spec form q2). From Phase 8, his men at a raid or an outlawed valley. A heavy tithe in a lean year is a standing cause and a grievance, so factions and leavers get new reasons. Fails: an empty barn at the reeve's visit is a shortfall line, never a death. Test: a shortfall lowers favour with a line naming the sacks owed and counted.
- **Asks (Slice 3).** Owns asks: content `asks.json` entries with a source kind (lord, neighbour, wanderer, faith, ambition), what is wanted (goods, a fine thing of a quality, hosting a guest for a season, days of work, a building), a reason taken from a record (a neighbour's famine in its history, a priest's want for a bell, a colonist's ambition), a deadline, what it brings and what letting it pass costs. Every ask cites the line it came from; none come from nowhere. The player takes an ask up or lets it pass and gives a taken ask a priority of 1 to 4 like a Build row; colonists pick it up through their own job choice, weighting by trade, traits and what they think of the asker (a colony that dislikes the lord works slowly on his asks). Letting an ask pass costs favour or a neighbour's regard, never a person. Lord asks start here; sending men to war (Phase 8) and errands abroad (Phase 9) plug in later. Fails: an ask whose deadline passes undone is a line and the pass cost. Test: a taken-up lord's ask for a fine blade is made by the colony's best smith unordered, and its line cites the ask.
- **The lord and asks on screen (Slice 4).** The Asks sheet (⋯ menu): open asks with asker, reason (why?), deadline, reward and cost, Take up / Let pass and a 1 to 4 stepper; taken and closed asks below. The Laws sheet gains the Lord law row and the favour number with its causes; the Stock sheet the tithe row; the Region panel names the lord, temper and byname; the reeve walks on the map (spec form q1). Every lever works while paused.
- **Hints and breakthroughs (Slice 5, V-142).** Owns methods and each person's hints: content `methods.json` (about 12, spread over the trades the colony has; list below), each with the hints it needs (3 to 6), the kinds that give one (long work in its trade, a failure that teaches, something seen on a stranger or caravan, a book, a visitor from an ask, a tale) and its effect as content (faster or better work in a job, a lower wall leak, a longer keep, rarely a new recipe). Luck (Morgan's note): each working day in the trade has a small chance of a free hint, a little more for the lucky, and a rare dream after long work on one problem gives the last one. At the last hint comes the breakthrough: a heavy memory, a line naming every hint, a tale that spreads and sometimes a byname. Who knows a method is spec form q3. The **New Ways law** (Old ways only / Allowed / Encouraged): Encouraged gives hints faster but spends time and materials; a faith that holds tradition sacred leans against it, so a faction can form. Trade priority speeds hints too. Chasing a breakthrough can go wrong: ruined materials, a burned hand, a small fire, each a line and a tale. Fails: no work in a trade, no hints. Test: a smith with the method's hints gains it with a line citing each hint.
- **Discoveries on screen (Slice 6).** The person inspector shows "puzzling over harder iron, 3 of 5 hints" with why?; the ledger and recaps carry breakthroughs; the Laws sheet gains New Ways.
- **Extras (Slice 7, form e1 to e8).** The lord visits once in a while: the colony prepares a feast by the Feasts law, his mood moves favour, someone may petition or insult him. Petition: after a bad harvest the elder asks for a lower tithe; the answer follows temper and favour and is remembered. Wandering masters: a mason, scholar or tinker asks (an ask) to stay a season; housed and fed, they teach, leave hints and may stay. Contests at feasts: ploughing, ale, the strongest arm, with bynames, rivals and the lord's prize. The valley's charter: at high favour and a size, the lord grants a name and a charter, a lasting ledger mark. Later (not 6C): keeping secrets (Methods law), outlaws in the woods, a rival lord or heir.

The first dozen methods (content, numbers at Slice 5's mock-up): steel (smith), a heavier plough (farm), resting a field each third year (farm), better mill wheel (mill), treadle loom (weave), lime mortar (mason, lower leak), herb remedies (heal), glazed pots (potter, longer keep), a better charcoal clamp (charcoal), bark tanning (tan), bee skeps that winter well (beekeep), and a cleaner smelt (smelt). Job ids copied from `jobs.json`.

## Spec form answers (2026-11-23, V-211)

- **q1 A, the reeve walks in.** He comes by the home road with a cart and a guard, stays the day, counts the tithe barn and leaves; tapping him opens a page like a stranger's.
- **q2 A, goods and beatings.** At the bottom of the ladder the lord's men take about twice what was owed from the store and barn; anyone who stands in their way may be beaten (wounds with a line, nobody killed). No hostages.
- **q3 A, the finder, then teaching.** A method is known only by its finder; others learn it as their apprentice or from a book the teacher writes (V-189, V-199); a finder who dies untaught takes it with them (V-141).
- **q4 A, the lord's forest.** The old woods farthest from the green are the lord's; hunting or felling there is poaching, a defiance some do anyway (the hungry, the defiant); favour falls if the reeve hears; a charter makes it the colony's. One content entry for the forest, one for the charter.
